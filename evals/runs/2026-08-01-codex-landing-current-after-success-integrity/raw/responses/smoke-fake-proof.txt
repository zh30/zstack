## Smoke protocol

- **Hypothesis:** If `[Product]` offers `[specific outcome]` to `[narrow ICP]`, qualified visitors experiencing `[painful problem]` will join an early-access waitlist.
- **Primary metric:** Qualified unique visitors → provider-confirmed email signups, measured separately by traffic source.
- **Secondary metrics:** CTA clicks, qualifying answers, welcome-email replies, explicit “I would pay” responses, and interview bookings.
- **Sample target:** At least 100 qualified community visitors and 100 cold visitors. Treat fewer than 50 visits from any source as inconclusive. Track warm outreach separately.
- **Success bar:** At least 5% confirmed signup conversion from niche-community traffic, five or more confirmed signups, and two substantive replies describing the problem.
- **Strong signal:** A credible request to pay, pre-order, or receive an invoice. One request is a lead, not proof of scale.
- **Pivot bar:** After 200+ qualified community/cold visits across two headline variants, sustained conversion below 2% means change the ICP, problem, or offer.
- **Soft-demand warning:** Good signup conversion but no replies or concrete use cases means interview subscribers before building.
- **Timebox:** August 3–16, 2026.

## Decision rules

- **At least 5% niche-community conversion plus substantive replies:** Conduct five interviews, define a thin MVP, and test willingness to pay.
- **Conversion is healthy but replies are empty:** Treat it as curiosity, not validated demand; interview subscribers before building.
- **CTA clicks are healthy but confirmed signups are weak:** Investigate form friction, privacy concerns, and offer clarity.
- **Below 2% after 200+ qualified community/cold visits and two headlines:** Narrow or change the ICP/problem promise.
- **No qualified traffic:** The distribution hypothesis failed; do not interpret this as evidence that the problem does not exist.
- **Credible payment intent appears:** Interview that person immediately and test a narrow paid offer.
- **Fake proof materially lifts conversion:** Discard that result; it does not validate honest demand.
