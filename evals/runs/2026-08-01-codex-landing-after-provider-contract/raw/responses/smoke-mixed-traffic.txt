No—6% is encouraging enough to continue validating, but not enough to justify building the full product.

The main issue is attribution: 20 friend visits and 180 broad-ad visits are different traffic classes. Because the 12 emails aren’t split by source, the cold-ad conversion rate could be anywhere from 0% to 6.7%. The pooled 6% conceals that uncertainty, and broad ads may not represent your actual ideal customer.

What I’d do next:

- Contact all 12 signups. Ask what prompted them to join, what they currently use, and whether they would book a call, try an early version, or pay a founding price.
- Run another test with source attribution and confirmed-email submissions.
- Target at least 200 ICP-qualified cold visitors—not merely broad traffic.
- Pre-register cold-traffic rules:
  - Above 5%, plus several credible replies/bookings: build a thin MVP.
  - 2–5%: refine the ICP, offer, or page and retest.
  - Below 2% after two controlled variants: reshape or park the offer.
- Test stronger intent, such as a deposit, preorder, scheduled call, or qualifying question.

Current verdict: continue the experiment; do not build the full product. At most, create a narrow manual or prototype experience needed to serve and learn from the most motivated signups.
