## Smoke protocol

**Hypothesis:** If `[Product]` offers `[outcome]` to `[narrow ICP]`, at least 5% of qualified mixed-social visitors and 10% of qualified warm visitors will submit a confirmed email.

**Primary metric:** Qualified unique visitor → provider-confirmed waitlist signup.

**Secondary signals:** CTA-click rate, qualifier completion, welcome-email replies, credible willingness-to-pay statements, and interview acceptance. Opens are diagnostic only.

**Sample plan:**

- Warm outreach: 100 qualified visitors and at least 10 confirmed signups.
- Mixed social/community traffic: 100 qualified visitors and at least 5 confirmed signups.
- Keep the lanes separate; do not combine their conversion rates.
- Rates below 50 qualified visits per lane are too unstable for a decision.

**Timebox:** 14 days after tracking and form acceptance have been tested.

**Continue:** Meet the threshold in a lane and obtain at least five detailed replies or three interview acceptances.

**Rewrite and retest:** Traffic is qualified, but the lane misses its conversion threshold.

**Park or change the audience/problem:** After 200+ qualified visits across two controlled headline variants, confirmed conversion stays below 2%, with no strong replies.

A signup signals interest, not product-market fit. Payment or real switching behavior would be stronger evidence.

## Low-cost implementation

Use a one-page static site on Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, or GitHub Pages, connected to a hosted form service.

Form data:

- `email` — required
- `biggest_challenge` — optional
- `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` — preserved as hidden metadata
- `traffic_lane` — allow-listed value: `warm`, `community`, `social`, or `unattributed`

Because provider documentation could not be checked during this run, keep the endpoint as `VERIFY_FORM_ENDPOINT` and block traffic until one valid and one invalid test submission prove the integration. Confirm the current endpoint, authentication, accepted response, metadata semantics, storage location, and opt-in behavior from the provider’s official documentation.

Use the provider’s confirmed subscriber record as the conversion source of truth. A click, submission attempt, URL parameter, or local browser flag is not a signup. If the page cannot verify acceptance, show only:

> Submission sent. Check your inbox if the provider accepts it.

Instrument these events:

- `landing_view`
- `click_waitlist`
- `attempt_waitlist`
- `confirmed_waitlist` — recorded only from verified provider acceptance

Use Plausible or Umami for lightweight analytics. Never log email addresses, qualifier text, secrets, or raw provider responses.
