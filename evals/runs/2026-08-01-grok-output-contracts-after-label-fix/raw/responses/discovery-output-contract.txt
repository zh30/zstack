## 7. Decision

**Verdict: Advance**

**Why:** Among five interviews, three show bleeding + real spend/workaround cost; one shows switch history and integration preference; only one is polite-only noise. Gates are not uniform “5,” but behavioral support is enough for a **narrow smoke / paid pilot**, not a large build.

**If Advance — wedge to take into smoke/PRD:**  
**Client-onboarding data handoff for agency PMs and bookkeepers (and similar ops) who already pay Zapier and/or VA/assistant time**, with: (1) cut manual multi-tool copy, (2) explicit ownership of onboarding steps, (3) migration path, (4) sensitive-file controls, (5) CSV import/export. Lead commercial motion with a **real pilot** (as requested by agency PM), not template dumps.

**Reshape note (secondary):** Broad “any founder” ICP is wrong — drop pitch-only founders with no recent pain/spend.

**Park would require:** If next 5 cold ICP interviews showed only compliments and free tools with no time/money cost — not the case here.
