## 1. Hypothesis

If we offer **one-click restore of client-specific Chrome tab workspaces** to **hands-on support agents at small US SaaS teams**, via a single **Join the early-access waitlist** CTA with a truthful **from $9/month** planned price, then:

- **Cold/community traffic** (Reddit + niche forums + ads): confirmed waitlist CVR **≥ 3%** after **≥ 150** qualified unique visits in that class, **and**
- **Warm outreach** (personal DMs / known operators): confirmed waitlist CVR **≥ 12%** after **≥ 25** qualified unique visits in that class, **and**
- At least **5** replies or “I’d pay / need this” messages with a real client-workspace story  

within **10 days**, under the traffic plan below.

**Hard rule:** Never pool warm outreach with cold traffic when computing CVR or applying thresholds.

## 2. Metrics & decision rules

### Definitions

| Metric | Definition | Source of truth |
|--------|------------|-----------------|
| Unique visitors | Plausible unique pageviews on the landing URL, filtered by UTM / traffic class | Plausible |
| CTA clicks | Clicks on primary CTA buttons (`click_cta`) | Plausible custom event |
| Attempted submits | Form submit handler fired (`attempt_waitlist`) — **not** a conversion | Plausible |
| Confirmed waitlist | Subscriber appears as accepted in **Buttondown** | Buttondown export |
| Visitor → email CVR | Confirmed waitlist ÷ unique visitors **within the same traffic class** | Plausible + Buttondown metadata |
| Welcome open rate | Opens on Buttondown welcome (diagnostic only) | Buttondown |
| Intent signals | Replies with use case, “I’d pay”, or calendar ask | Inbox / DM logs |
| Revenue | $0 expected (no charge in smoke) | N/A |
| CPE | Ad spend ÷ confirmed waitlist from ads only | Ads dashboard + Buttondown tag |

### Traffic classes (do not pool)

| Class | UTM pattern / tag | Includes |
|-------|-------------------|----------|
| **warm** | `utm_medium=warm` or tag `warm` | Personal DMs, known operators, founder network |
| **community** | `utm_medium=community` | Reddit, Indie Hackers, niche Slack/Discord (permissioned) |
| **social** | `utm_medium=social` | X/Twitter organic |
| **paid** | `utm_medium=cpc` | Meta/Google/Reddit ads within $150 |

### Source-specific thresholds (pre-registered)

| Class | Min sample before decide | Continue (strong) | Retest offer (weak CVR) | Kill / park class |
|-------|--------------------------|-------------------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| **warm** | ≥ 25 uniques **or** Day 10 | CVR ≥ 12% **and** ≥ 2 intent replies | CVR 5–12% or silent list | CVR &lt; 5% after min sample |
| **community** | ≥ 80 uniques **or** Day 10 | CVR ≥ 4% **and** ≥ 2 qualified answers | CVR 1.5–4% | CVR &lt; 1.5% after min sample + 1 hero rewrite |
| **social** | ≥ 40 uniques **or** Day 10 | CVR ≥ 3% | CVR 1–3% | CVR &lt; 1% after min sample |
| **paid** | ≥ $80 spent **and** ≥ 40 uniques **or** Day 10 | CVR ≥ 2.5% **and** CPE ≤ $12 | CVR 1–2.5% or CPE $12–25 | CVR &lt; 1% **or** CPE &gt; $25 after ≥ $80 |

**Aggregate rule:** Do **not** average warm + cold into one “headline CVR.” Report four class rows. Overall decision uses the matrix below after min samples where possible.

### Decision matrix (Day 10)

| Result | Next step |
|--------|-----------|
| **Strong** — ≥2 classes hit Continue **and** ≥5 intent replies | Thin MVP / tighten with `z-write-prd`; interview top waitlist |
| **Mixed** — 1 class strong, others weak | Keep winning channel + rewrite hero for weak classes; **do not** build full product |
| **Traffic OK, CVR weak** across cold classes | Reshape promise/ICP; A/B one new hero; no new features |
| **CVR OK, zero engagement** after 1 follow-up email | Soft demand; run `z-customer-discovery` before code |
| **Paid fails, organic weak, warm silent** after honest push | Park or reshape ICP — do not “build more features” |
| **Any credible “invoice me / I’ll pay $9 now”** | Treat as lead (not scale proof); prioritize paid-slice learning |

### Kill / pivot bar (product thesis)

After **≥ 200** combined **non-warm** (community + social + paid) qualified uniques **and** one controlled hero rewrite mid-test, if confirmed non-warm CVR stays **&lt; 1.5%** and intent replies **&lt; 2**, reshape or park — not “ship more Chrome APIs.”

If you cannot get traffic (distribution failure), that is **not** the same as product death; log it under exclusions.

#### Problem / status quo (3 bullets max)
- You reopen the same stack of client tools after every context switch — and lose minutes (and focus) each time.  
- Bookmarks and “pin everything” collapse under 20+ accounts; windows get messy, wrong client open, wrong tenant.  
- Shared browser profiles and sticky notes don’t travel cleanly when you’re the one who actually does the work.

#### Solution (how it works — 3 steps)
1. **Save a client workspace** — Name the client, capture the tab set you actually use for them.  
2. **Switch with one click** — Jump from ticket → full context without hunting URLs.  
3. **Keep it agent-simple** — Built for hands-on support at small US SaaS teams, not enterprise browser lockdown theater.

#### Who it’s for / not for
**For**
- Support / CS / success agents who live in Chrome all day  
- Small US SaaS teams where one person owns many client contexts  
- People who already maintain informal “tab rituals” per account  

**Not for**
- Enterprise IT looking for managed browser policy and SSO-only lock-down  
- Casual multi-tab users who don’t work client-by-client  
- Teams that need a full shared knowledge base (this is workspace restore, not a wiki)
