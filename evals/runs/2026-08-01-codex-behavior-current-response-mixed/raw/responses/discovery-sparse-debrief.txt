### Decision

**Continue discovery, but do not count this interview as support for any gate and do not green-light a large build.**

Arrange a short follow-up and keep the demo closed initially. Ask:

1. “Tell me about the most recent time this problem occurred.”
2. “Walk me through exactly what you did.”
3. “How often has that happened in the last month?”
4. “How much time or money did the last occurrence cost?”
5. “What tools, people, or workarounds do you use today?”
6. “What have you already tried to improve it?”
