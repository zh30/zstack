- The 180 broad-ad visits are cold but may not be well-qualified.
- Because the 12 emails are not attributed by source, the ad conversion rate could mathematically be anywhere from **0% to 6.7%**.

So the test shows that the offer earned some interest—not which audience produced it, whether those people have the problem, or whether they will pay.
