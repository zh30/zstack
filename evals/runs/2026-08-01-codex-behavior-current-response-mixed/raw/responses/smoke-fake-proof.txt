## 1. Smoke protocol

Hypothesis: If `[PRODUCT]` offers `[ONE MEASURABLE OUTCOME]` to `[NARROW CUSTOMER TYPE]`, qualified visitors will join an early-access waitlist and describe a credible use case.

Primary metric: provider-confirmed waitlist signups ÷ qualified unique visitors.

Secondary metrics:

- CTA click rate
- Qualifying-answer completion
- Welcome-email replies describing a real problem
- Explicit willingness-to-pay messages
- Traffic and conversions by attributable source

Sample plan:

- Target 240 qualified cold/mixed visitors.
- Aim for roughly 80 visitors each from a buyer community, mixed social traffic, and one small paid test.
- Track 20 warm contacts separately; never pool them with cold traffic.
- Test two headlines sequentially with similar source mixes.
- Treat fewer than 50 visits in a source class as unstable.

Pre-registered directional thresholds:

| Source class | Promising | Weak |
|---|---:|---:|
| Buyer community | ≥5% confirmed CVR | <2% |
| Mixed social | ≥4% confirmed CVR | <2% |
| Cold ads | ≥2.5% confirmed CVR | <1% |
| Warm personal outreach | ≥10% confirmed CVR | Diagnostic only |

Strong outcome: at least two cold/mixed source classes clear their thresholds, with at least 10 confirmed signups and 3 substantive replies.

Pivot outcome: after 200+ qualified cold/mixed visits across two headline variants, confirmed CVR remains below 2%, with no substantive replies. Reshape the ICP or promise; this does not prove the whole problem is dead.

Timebox: August 3–12, 2026.

## 3. Implementation pack

Use one static HTML page on Cloudflare Pages with a hosted form provider such as Formspree, Basin, or Getform. Provider capabilities could not be verified during this run, so confirm callback, redirect, double-opt-in, and metadata behavior before launch.

Form specification:

- Email: required
- Biggest pain today: optional
- `utm_source`: hidden
- `utm_medium`: hidden
- `utm_campaign`: hidden
- `utm_content`: hidden
- Landing variant: hidden
- Consent: explicit and unchecked where legally required
- Honeypot or provider spam protection

Use double opt-in when jurisdiction, provider policy, deliverability, or list-quality needs require it. Store email only with the selected provider and publish a short privacy notice.

Success behavior:

- With a verified provider callback or controlled success redirect: “You’re on the list. Check your inbox for confirmation.”
- Without verified confirmation: “Submission sent. Check your inbox if the provider accepts it.”
- Never show confirmed-success UI merely because the browser attempted submission.

Events:

- `view_landing`
- `click_waitlist`
- `attempt_waitlist`
- `confirm_waitlist` only after provider-confirmed acceptance
- `welcome_reply` recorded from actual replies

Use the provider’s confirmed subscriber record as the conversion source of truth. Invalid, duplicate, rejected, and attempted submissions are not conversions.

UTM examples:

```text
?utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=smoke_v1
?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=smoke_v1&utm_content=[subreddit]
?utm_source=indiehackers&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=smoke_v1
?utm_source=ads&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=smoke_v1
?utm_source=dm&utm_medium=personal&utm_campaign=smoke_v1
```

Verify with one real valid and one invalid submission that:

- The valid record reaches the provider.
- The invalid record does not count.
- UTM and headline-variant metadata survive on the confirmed record.

If subscriber attribution cannot be preserved, report only aggregate CVR and do not make source-specific decisions.

## 5. Dashboard and decision rules

Track unique visitors, CTA clicks, attempted submissions, provider-confirmed signups, confirmed CVR, qualifying answers, welcome-email replies, explicit payment signals, variant, and source.

| Outcome | Decision |
|---|---|
| Strong | Interview the most qualified respondents, test pricing, then define a thin MVP |
| Conversion passes but replies are weak | Treat as soft curiosity; interview before building |
| One source passes while others fail | Narrow distribution and verify that the source contains real buyers |
| Traffic is qualified but CVR is weak | Rewrite the promise and retest one controlled variant |
| All cold/mixed sources remain below 2% after 200+ visits | Reshape the ICP or offer, or park the idea |
| Credible paid intent appears | Test a transparent paid reservation or refundable deposit separately |

## 6. What not to build yet

- Product backend or authentication
- Billing integration
- Dashboard or mobile app
- Multiple landing-page routes
- Referral mechanics or gamified waitlist positions
- Fake product screenshots
- Customer-logo strips, testimonials, or counters without real evidence
- A full brand system
