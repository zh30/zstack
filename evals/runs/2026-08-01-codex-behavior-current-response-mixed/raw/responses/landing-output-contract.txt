# Landing smoke test: ClientTabs

“ClientTabs” is a working name, not a final brand.

Evidence limit: web and browser access were unavailable, so channel availability, community rules, Buttondown’s current API fields, and the deployed funnel could not be independently verified. Treat those items as pre-launch checks, not observed results. No files were created because the workspace is read-only and the request prohibits modifications.

## 1. Smoke protocol

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | August 1, 2026 |
| Test window | August 3–12, 2026 |
| ICP | Hands-on support agents and support leads at small US SaaS companies who repeatedly switch among client-specific tabs |
| Problem | Reconstructing each client’s browser context wastes time and increases the chance of opening the wrong account or losing work |
| Promise | Restore a client-specific tab workspace in one click |
| CTA | **Join the early-access waitlist** |
| Price signal | Planned pricing from **$9/month**; final plans may change before launch |
| Stack | Cloudflare Pages, Pages Functions, Buttondown, Plausible |
| Paid-media cap | $150 total |
| Stage | Concept validation; extension is not built and has no firm release date |

### Hypothesis

If small US SaaS support teams are offered one-click restoration of client-specific browser workspaces, qualified cold visitors will join an early-access waitlist at the source-specific rates below, and some subscribers will reply with a concrete workflow or credible willingness to pay from $9/month.

### Funnel and metrics

```text
Qualified unique visitor
→ CTA click
→ Form attempt
→ Buttondown-accepted subscriber
→ Double-opt-in confirmation
→ Welcome-email reply
→ Credible paid-intent statement
```

Primary metric: confirmed waitlist CVR per traffic source:

```text
Buttondown double-opt-in-confirmed subscribers attributed to source
÷
Plausible unique landing visitors from the same source and test window
```

Never combine warm outreach with cold traffic. Report every source separately.

Secondary metrics:

- CTA click rate.
- Form-attempt rate.
- Confirmation rate among accepted submissions.
- Welcome-email opens, used only as a deliverability diagnostic.
- Replies containing a concrete support workflow.
- Replies affirming that pricing from $9/month is reasonable.
- Requests for a trial, invoice, or team plan.
- Cost per confirmed subscriber for paid traffic.

Invalid, rejected, duplicate, bot, and merely attempted submissions are not conversions.

### Source-specific thresholds

Rates below are pre-registered hypotheses, not benchmarks proven by research.

| Source class | Minimum evaluable sample | Strong | Mixed | Weak/pivot |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Reddit cold community | 50 qualified visitors | ≥4 confirmed, 8% | 2–3 confirmed | 0–1 confirmed |
| Permission-based niche community | 40 | ≥4 confirmed, 10% | 2–3 confirmed | 0–1 confirmed |
| X cold/mixed social | 40 | ≥3 confirmed, 7.5% | 2 confirmed | 0–1 confirmed |
| Paid cold traffic | 60 | ≥3 confirmed, 5% and ≤$50 each | 2 confirmed or $51–$75 each | 0–1 confirmed or >$75 each |
| Warm personal outreach | 20 landing visitors | ≥4 confirmed, 20% | 2–3 confirmed | 0–1 confirmed |

A source below its minimum sample is **inconclusive**, regardless of its apparent rate. Warm results may validate interview access and problem resonance, but never cold acquisition.

### Contact-intent thresholds

Across all confirmed subscribers, while preserving each subscriber’s source:

- Strong: at least 5 concrete-workflow replies, including at least 2 from cold sources, and at least 3 credible “$9/month is reasonable” or stronger responses.
- Mixed: 2–4 concrete replies or price interest only from warm contacts.
- Weak: 0–1 concrete replies after every confirmed subscriber receives the welcome email and one follow-up.

No prepayment is requested in this test. A payment request therefore remains untested.

## 3. Implementation package

### Minimal architecture

```text
Cloudflare Pages
├── index.html
├── styles.css
├── app.js
├── og-clienttabs.png
└── functions/api/waitlist.ts
        └── Buttondown API
```

Required configuration placeholders:

- `YOUR_DOMAIN`
- `BUTTONDOWN_API_KEY` as a Cloudflare secret
- `BUTTONDOWN_LIST_OR_TAG_CONFIGURATION`
- `PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN`
- `hello@YOUR_DOMAIN`

Do not expose the Buttondown API key in browser code.

### Form fields

| Field | Requirement | Destination |
|---|---|---|
| `email` | Required, HTML and server validated | Buttondown subscriber |
| `workflow` | Optional, maximum 500 characters | Provider metadata or secure internal note |
| `utm_source` | Hidden, allowlisted | Subscriber metadata/tag |
| `utm_medium` | Hidden, allowlisted | Subscriber metadata/tag |
| `utm_campaign` | Hidden, fixed to `clienttabs_smoke_202608` | Subscriber metadata/tag |
| `utm_content` | Hidden, allowlisted | Subscriber metadata/tag |
| `traffic_class` | Derived server-side | Subscriber metadata/tag |
| `landing_variant` | Fixed to `hero_restore_v1` | Subscriber metadata/tag |
| `referrer_host` | Derived without path/query | Optional metadata |

Server-side allowlist:

```text
reddit
niche_community
x
paid
warm_outreach
direct
unknown
```

Never accept an arbitrary client-provided traffic class without mapping it server-side.

### Submission behavior

1. Browser validates the email and sends the form to `/api/waitlist`.
2. The Pages Function validates and normalizes all fields.
3. It derives `traffic_class` from allowlisted UTMs.
4. It calls Buttondown from the server.
5. If Buttondown explicitly accepts the request, return `accepted_pending_confirmation`.
6. If Buttondown reports a duplicate, invalid address, rejection, or provider error, return that distinct state.
7. Only `accepted_pending_confirmation` may show:

   > Request received—check your inbox to confirm your email.

8. The page must never show “You’re on the list” merely because the request was attempted.
9. A waitlist conversion counts only when Buttondown records the subscriber as double-opt-in confirmed.

Before launch, verify the current Buttondown API’s exact subscriber metadata/tag and confirmation behavior. That could not be checked in this run.

### Double opt-in

Use double opt-in because the experiment values list quality and attributable intent more than the largest possible raw list.

**Confirmation-page copy**

> Email confirmed. You’re now on the ClientTabs early-access waitlist.

This page should only be reachable through Buttondown’s verified confirmation flow.

### Plausible events

| Event | Trigger | Properties | Evidence class |
|---|---|---|---|
| `view_landing` | One page load per Plausible visitor | source, medium, campaign, content, traffic_class, variant | Visit only |
| `click_cta` | Any button labeled with the single CTA | location: nav/hero/offer/final, traffic_class, variant | CTA interest |
| `attempt_waitlist` | Valid client-side form sent | traffic_class, variant | Attempt only |
| `waitlist_request_accepted` | Pages Function returns explicit Buttondown acceptance | traffic_class, variant | Pending confirmation, not conversion |
| `waitlist_error` | Rejected or failed request | reason category, traffic_class | Operations |
| `confirm_waitlist` | Only through a verified provider callback/webhook if supported | traffic_class, variant | Confirmed conversion |

If a verified Buttondown confirmation callback is unavailable, omit `confirm_waitlist` from Plausible. Use Buttondown’s confirmed-subscriber export as the conversion source of truth. Do not manufacture it from a success-page visit.

Do not send email addresses, workflow answers, company names, or other personal information to Plausible.

### Attribution contract

Source-specific CVR is permitted only if a test submission proves all of the following:

1. The landing visit appears in Plausible under the expected source.
2. The same source or derived traffic class appears on the confirmed Buttondown subscriber.
3. Duplicate and rejected states are distinguishable.
4. Double-opt-in status is visible.
5. Exports preserve the source and confirmation state.

Run one valid and one invalid test for every capture path. If Buttondown cannot retain source metadata, create distinct provider-native forms/tags for each source and verify them. Until that works, pause traffic; page-level UTMs plus an undifferentiated subscriber total cannot support the required decisions.

### Pre-launch acceptance checklist

- Cloudflare preview and production URLs load over HTTPS.
- Mobile and desktop layouts work without horizontal overflow.
- All visible buttons use exactly “Join the early-access waitlist.”
- One valid submission reaches Buttondown with correct attribution.
- One invalid email is rejected and not counted.
- One duplicate is not counted as a new conversion.
- Double opt-in confirmation is verified.
- Error, attempt, and confirmation messages remain distinct.
- Plausible receives no PII.
- Every UTM resolves to the same page without losing parameters.
- Buttondown export can be joined to the matching Plausible source class.
- OG image and metadata render correctly in a share debugger when browser access becomes available.
- Privacy and contact copy use real addresses before publishing.

## 6. Decision rules

### Proceed to a thin prototype

Proceed only if:

- At least one cold source reaches its strong threshold at its minimum sample;
- at least two confirmed cold subscribers provide concrete workflow replies;
- at least three total subscribers express credible price acceptance from $9/month; and
- tracking and confirmation integrity pass.

Build only a disposable prototype that demonstrates save, name, and restore—not a production extension.

### Run customer interviews before building

Interview 5–7 subscribers if:

- CVR is strong but replies are scarce;
- warm traffic is strong while every cold source is inconclusive;
- people describe the pain but disagree on the needed workspace behavior; or
- price acceptance is unclear.

### Retest the offer

Run a second controlled smoke test if:

- a source meets its minimum sample but lands in the mixed band;
- CTA clicks are healthy but confirmed CVR is weak;
- confirmation drop-off suggests poor email messaging or deliverability; or
- replies reveal a sharper ICP, such as technical support engineers rather than all support agents.

Change one major variable only: headline, ICP, or promise.

### Reshape or park

Reshape the offer if two cold sources each meet their minimum samples and fall in the weak band.

Park the concept if:

- the reshaped second test also produces less than 2% confirmed cold CVR across at least 200 qualified cold visits;
- there are no concrete cold-source replies;
- no credible price interest appears; and
- tracking and distribution quality are sound.

Insufficient traffic means the distribution thesis is unproven; it does not prove the product problem is absent.

### Paid intent

Any unsolicited request to pay, pre-order, obtain an invoice, or purchase a team plan is a high-value lead. Interview that person and test a paid prototype offer next. One request is not proof of scalable demand.

## 7. What not to build during the smoke test

Do not build:

- The Chrome extension.
- Authentication or team accounts.
- Workspace sync.
- Tab-content access.
- Client-data storage.
- Billing or Stripe integration.
- Admin dashboards.
- CRM, help-desk, or ticketing integrations.
- AI categorization.
- Chrome Web Store assets or submission.
- A multi-page marketing site.
- A brand identity system.
- Referral loops or waitlist counters.

The only implementation is the landing page, verified email capture, analytics, and attribution.
