# Recruit scripts — z-customer-discovery

Tone: human, short, non-salesy. Adapt language to audience.

## Screening (use before booking)

1. What is your role and main workflow around {domain}?  
2. When did you last hit {problem area}? (if “never” → maybe anti-ICP)  
3. What do you use today for that?  

## Cold DM / email (template)

```text
Subject: 20 min? learning how {ICP} handle {problem area}

Hi {name} — I'm a solo builder researching how {ICP} deal with {problem},
not selling anything on the call. Curious about the last time you ran into it
and what you did. 20–25 min voice/video at your convenience?
Happy to share notes afterward if useful.
```

## Warm intro

```text
Hey {name} — I'm talking to ~10 {ICP} about how they handle {problem} today
(past experiences, not a pitch). Could I steal 20 min this week? Or is there
someone on your team closer to that workflow?
```

## Community post (where allowed)

```text
Building in public / research (not a launch):
I'm interviewing {ICP} about {problem area} — how you handle it today,
what you've tried, what it costs you. 20 min, no pitch deck.
Comment or DM if you're open; happy to share anonymized takeaways.
```

## Pipeline targets

| Stage | Indie default |
|-------|----------------|
| List of candidates | 25–40 |
| Outreach | 15–25 |
| Booked | 8–15 |
| Completed | 5–15 |

Expect funnel drop-off. Over-recruit.

## Mix rule

Track in `README.md`:

- Cold ICP (not friends): target ≥ 50%  
- Warm / adjacent OK for early learning but label them  

## Anti-patterns

- “Can I show you my demo?” as the ask  
- Paying random survey farms for “interviews” without quality control  
- Only interviewing people who already love you
