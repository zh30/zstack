No. A 6% aggregate conversion rate is encouraging, but it is not enough evidence to build the full product.

The key problem is attribution:

- 12 emails from 200 visits = 6%.
- If all 12 came from the 180 broad-ad visits, cold conversion was 6.7%—promising.
- If all 12 came from the 20 friends, cold conversion was 0%—weak.
- Without signup source data, you cannot distinguish those outcomes.
- Email signup demonstrates curiosity, not willingness to use or pay.

Build only the cheapest testable slice—prototype, concierge workflow, or thin MVP—after this next validation:

1. Contact all 12 signups and ask about their current workaround, urgency, and willingness to try or pay.
2. Run another test with at least 200 qualified cold visitors, preserving source attribution on each provider-confirmed signup.
3. Pre-register these directional thresholds:

   - **Continue to thin MVP:** at least 10 confirmed signups (≥5%), plus 3 meaningful replies or 2 customer calls.
   - **Strong build signal:** the above plus a preorder, deposit, or credible request to be invoiced.
   - **Revise and retest:** 4–9 signups (2–4.5%) or good conversion with no engagement.
   - **Pivot the offer/ICP:** fewer than 4 signups (<2%) after two controlled page variants.

Evidence is limited to your reported aggregate counts; signup attribution, traffic quality, replies, and payment intent were not supplied.
