# 10-Day Smoke Test: “ClientDeck” Chrome Extension

“ClientDeck” is a temporary working name; trademark and domain availability are unverified. This package tests demand for an unbuilt product—it does not claim that the extension exists.

Because web access and file writes are unavailable, I could not verify current Buttondown or Plausible API contracts or create the deployable Cloudflare Pages artifact. Those integrations remain explicit launch blockers below.

## 1. Smoke protocol

### Audience

Hands-on customer support agents and support leads at small US SaaS companies, especially people who repeatedly switch between client-specific tabs, dashboards, tickets, documentation, and admin tools.

Exclude enterprise procurement teams, general-purpose tab-management shoppers, and people who do not regularly revisit client-specific browser contexts.

### Offer

A planned Chrome extension that saves a client-specific collection of tabs as a workspace and restores it in one click.

- Single CTA: **Join the early-access waitlist**
- Planned price: **from $9/month**
- No payment during this test
- No firm release date
- No claim that the extension is already available

### Hypothesis

If qualified support agents at small US SaaS companies see a credible promise of restoring a client’s working context in one click, enough will join an early-access waitlist—and some will reply with a concrete current workaround—to justify customer interviews and a thin MVP.

### Dates

Run from August 3 through August 12, 2026.

Freeze the shipped headline and offer for the first seven days. Only fix defects. Use days 8–10 for a pre-registered headline retest if the first version receives adequate traffic but weak conversion.

### Metrics

Primary metric:

```text
Confirmed waitlist CVR =
provider-confirmed, source-attributed subscribers / unique landing visitors
```

A subscriber counts only after Buttondown confirms acceptance according to its verified official API semantics. A button click, form attempt, client redirect, query parameter, or local success message is not a conversion.

Secondary metrics:

- Hero CTA click rate
- Valid form-attempt rate
- Buttondown double-opt-in confirmation rate, if enabled
- Welcome email open rate
- Welcome email reply rate
- Number of replies describing a repeated client-context workflow
- Number explicitly saying $9/month sounds reasonable
- Cost per provider-confirmed signup
- Conversion and engagement by decision lane

Do not combine warm outreach with cold ads or organic community traffic in any headline metric.

### Traffic lanes and thresholds

#### Cold paid traffic

Target: 120–150 attributable unique visitors from Reddit and X ads combined, while retaining each platform separately in raw reporting.

- Continue: at least 5 confirmed signups from 125 visitors, CVR ≥4%, and at least 2 engaged replies across cold signups.
- Promising but unresolved: 3–4 signups from 125 visitors or CVR 2.5–3.9%. Interview signups and retest the offer once.
- Reshape or stop: no more than 2 signups after at least 125 visitors, CVR <2%, with technically sound attribution and form delivery.
- Ad-level early stop: pause a creative after at least 40 landing visits if it produces zero confirmed signups.
- Cost guardrail: pause a platform if confirmed cost per signup exceeds $30 after at least $60 spent.

Reddit and X results may be summed only into the pre-registered `cold_paid` lane for the lane decision. Keep platform rows visible; do not disguise a losing platform with another platform’s results.

#### Cold organic communities

Target: 60–80 attributable visitors from permitted Reddit posts and support/SaaS communities.

- Continue: at least 5 confirmed signups from 75 visitors, CVR ≥6%, and at least 2 engaged replies.
- Promising but unresolved: 3–4 signups or CVR 3–5.9%.
- Reshape: no more than 2 signups after 75 visitors, CVR <3%.
- If fewer than 50 attributable visitors arrive, report “insufficient sample”; do not declare failure.

#### Warm direct outreach

Target: 25–30 individually selected people who plausibly fit the ICP. Report recipients, visits, signups, and replies separately.

- Continue: at least 5 confirmed signups from 30 delivered messages, signup rate ≥15%, plus at least 3 substantive replies.
- Soft signal: 3–4 signups or replies that express interest without describing current behavior.
- Reshape: no more than 2 signups and no more than 1 substantive reply after 30 delivered, personalized messages.
- Never add these visits or signups to cold-traffic CVR.

A substantive reply must mention at least one of:

- A repeated client-specific workflow
- A current workaround
- Frequency of rebuilding context
- A recent failure or wasted-time incident
- A price reaction
- A request to try the product

Compliments such as “cool idea” do not count.

### Experiment-wide decision minimum

Do not make a build decision until either:

- One cold lane reaches its minimum sample and conversion threshold; or
- The 10 days end, in which case classify underpowered lanes as inconclusive.

Warm success alone authorizes interviews, not an MVP.

# 4. Implementation pack

## Architecture

Use:

```text
Cloudflare Pages
├── Static single-page landing site
├── /api/join Cloudflare Pages Function
├── Buttondown subscriber API
└── Plausible analytics
```

The server-side function is necessary to validate submissions, protect provider credentials, preserve attribution, and return confirmed success only after verified provider acceptance.

Required environment variables:

```text
BUTTONDOWN_API_KEY
VERIFY_BUTTONDOWN_API_ENDPOINT
VERIFY_BUTTONDOWN_AUTH_SCHEME
VERIFY_BUTTONDOWN_SUCCESS_STATUSES
VERIFY_BUTTONDOWN_SUBSCRIBER_PAYLOAD
VERIFY_BUTTONDOWN_TAG_OR_METADATA_SEMANTICS

VERIFY_PLAUSIBLE_SCRIPT_URL
VERIFY_PLAUSIBLE_EVENT_API
VERIFY_PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN
VERIFY_PLAUSIBLE_PRIVACY_BEHAVIOR
```

All `VERIFY_*` values are launch-blocking. Do not load Plausible or call Buttondown until each relevant value has been checked against dated official documentation and tested with one accepted and one rejected submission.

## Form

Visible fields:

- `email`: required, valid email syntax, maximum 254 characters
- `workflow`: optional, plain text, maximum 160 characters; prohibit sensitive customer data in nearby copy

Server-derived fields:

- `decision_lane`
- `placement`
- `variant`
- `campaign`
- consent timestamp
- page version, such as `smoke-v1`

Do not trust hidden attribution fields supplied by the browser. Recompute the lane server-side from exact allow-listed campaign and placement tokens. Discard raw unknown values.

Recommended consent: double opt-in for list quality and proof that the address is controlled by the subscriber. Track provider acceptance and email confirmation as separate states unless Buttondown’s verified data model defines a trustworthy confirmed state.

## Request handling

`POST /api/join` should:

1. Accept only `application/json` with the exact allowed media type.
2. Cap actual streamed request bytes—for example, 4 KB—rather than trusting `Content-Length`.
3. Reject malformed JSON and any null, scalar, or array root.
4. Allow-list keys and validate field lengths.
5. Normalize the email for duplicate handling without logging it.
6. Map only registered attribution combinations; otherwise use `unattributed`.
7. Call Buttondown with a short timeout, such as five seconds.
8. Treat a signup as confirmed only when the verified provider response means the subscriber record was accepted.
9. Return finite public states such as `accepted`, `invalid`, `duplicate`, `rate_limited`, `provider_unavailable`, and `configuration_error`.
10. Never return or log raw provider bodies, emails, workflow text, credentials, stack traces, or exception messages.

Rate-limit by a privacy-conscious mechanism available on Cloudflare. Add a honeypot and minimum-fill-time check, but never count bot or rejected attempts as conversions.

## Source of truth

Use the provider-confirmed Buttondown subscriber record/export as the conversion authority. Every accepted subscriber must carry a verified source tag or metadata value matching one of:

- `cold_paid_reddit`
- `cold_paid_x`
- `cold_organic_reddit`
- `cold_organic_community`
- `warm_direct`
- `unattributed`

If Buttondown cannot persist these values, create distinct provider-native forms or tags per lane. If neither method works, source-specific CVR is unavailable and traffic must not launch until fixed.

## Plausible pageview boundary

Before analytics loads:

1. Read the URL’s UTM values.
2. Match them against the exact allow-list.
3. Reduce them to bounded canonical values.
4. Replace the browser history URL with the origin and pathname only, removing query and fragment.
5. Load Plausible only if all `VERIFY_PLAUSIBLE_*` configuration is resolved.
6. Emit only canonical properties.

Never send the email, workflow answer, raw UTM, referrer, query string, fragment, full URL, provider response, or exception text to Plausible.

## Event specification

| Event | Trigger | Authority | Allowed properties |
|---|---|---|---|
| `landing_view` | One sanitized pageview | Plausible | `decision_lane`, `placement`, `variant` |
| `click_waitlist` | Waitlist button reveals/focuses form or scrolls to it | Plausible | Same three properties |
| `attempt_waitlist` | Locally valid form is sent | Plausible, diagnostic only | Same three properties |
| `waitlist_accepted` | Verified Buttondown acceptance | Server/provider only | Canonical lane, placement, variant |
| `email_confirmed` | Verified double-opt-in state, if supported | Buttondown only | Canonical source metadata |
| `welcome_opened` | Provider-recorded open | Buttondown, directional | No browser event |
| `welcome_replied` | Manually coded from an actual reply | Measurement sheet | Lane and coded intent only |

`attempt_waitlist` must never be labeled `submit_waitlist` or used in visitor-to-email CVR.

Plausible’s server-side custom event contract is unverified. Until it is confirmed, do not attempt to send `waitlist_accepted` to Plausible; reconcile visits from Plausible with accepted subscribers from Buttondown in the dashboard.

## Privacy and retention

- Buttondown stores email and waitlist status.
- Cloudflare processes the submission and should retain no form content in logs.
- Plausible receives only canonical, non-PII event properties.
- Do not sell or share the list for unrelated marketing.
- Delete optional workflow answers after synthesis, with a preselected retention period such as 90 days.
- Provide a real privacy page or modal before launch, without introducing another CTA.
- Do not collect client names, URLs, ticket IDs, or customer information.

# 9. Decision rules

## Proceed to customer discovery and a thin prototype

Proceed only if either cold lane reaches its continue threshold and:

- At least three total cold-origin replies describe recent repeated behavior or a current workaround.
- At least two respondents say $9/month is reasonable, already pay for an alternative, or ask how to get access.
- No major privacy or Chrome-permission objection invalidates the proposed mechanism.

This authorizes interviews and a narrow prototype—not a polished extension, billing system, team administration, or broad launch.

## Interview before building

Do interviews first if:

- Conversion meets a cold threshold but replies are vague or absent.
- Warm outreach is strong while cold traffic is weak.
- People want the outcome but resist a browser extension.
- Signups cluster around a different role, such as implementation consultants or customer-success managers.

## Retest the offer once

Retest one headline for another controlled sample if:

- Cold traffic reaches minimum volume but CVR is in the unresolved band.
- CTA click rate is healthy but confirmed signup CVR is weak.
- Form attempts materially exceed provider-confirmed acceptance because of a correctable implementation problem.

Do not call an integration failure market evidence.

## Narrow or pivot

Narrow the ICP or problem when replies consistently indicate:

- The workflow matters only during onboarding or escalations.
- Teams organize by account rather than support ticket.
- Existing browser profiles, bookmarks, tab groups, or help-desk tooling already solve it.
- Privacy policy blocks client-named workspaces.
- Users want case-level rather than client-level restoration.

## Park the idea

Park it if both cold lanes meet their minimum samples and remain below their kill thresholds, with:

- Technically valid provider confirmation
- Defensible source attribution
- Honest, relevant placements
- No meaningful workflow replies
- No credible willingness-to-pay signal

Do not respond by adding more features.

## Budget rule

Hard stop at **$150**. Never shift money into a losing lane merely to exhaust the budget. Any future spend requires a new registered test.
