## 1. Executive summary

- **Primary keyword (main money term):** `client onboarding workflow for agencies`  
- **Primary page:** Homepage `/` (rewrite from generic agency software positioning)  
- **Strategy in one paragraph:** Own one **problem-language cluster** around **client onboarding workflow + ownership of next steps** for small agencies. Supplied notes suggest broad “agency software / CRM / portal” SERPs are vendor-dominated (poor fit for a zero-authority domain), while **agency workflow** and **owner-specific** results look mixed and community language already asks *who owns the next step* and *why checklists don’t get done*. Map a small hub + 2–3 spokes so the thin checklist article **supports** the hub (checklist failure → ownership) instead of competing with it. Founder capacity: **~6 hours/week** → ship depth on one cluster, not a content factory.  
- **Do not chase:** Head `agency software`, CRM/client-portal category terms, generic “automation software,” and multi-cluster expansion before GSC proves impressions on the primary cluster.
