## 1. Method limits

- **No verbatim quotes** were supplied; patterns are tied to summary anchors only, not debrief-grade language.
- **Summary-only depth:** frequency/cost details exist for some people (e.g. 45 minutes, weekly failures, Zapier, VA, assistant) but not full timelines, owners, or last-incident dates for everyone.
- **Sample bias:** N=5, pattern detection only — not market sizing. Four cold / one warm is a decent mix for a first checkpoint, not saturation.
- **Warm contact risk:** the founder interview is compliment + no recent pain/spend — classic soft signal; must not weight demand.
- **No observed experiment, public, or search evidence** in this run (web/browser/apps unavailable); do not upgrade interview behavior into conversion or payment proof.
- **Still unknown:** budget owner vs. recommender, exact onboarding workflow steps, willingness to pay *this* product (vs. current spend), retention after a pilot, and whether sensitive-file constraints block a multi-tenant SaaS path for bookkeepers.

## 7. Decision

**Verdict:** Advance  

**Why:**  
Among cold operators who already pay time/money (agency PM, bookkeeper, consultant), G1 and G2 have repeated behavioral support. G3 is only mixed, but the agency PM’s pilot ask plus prior switching/abandonment behavior is enough to justify a **narrow smoke or paid pilot**, not a large unscoped build. The founder interview is polite-only and must not dilute or inflate the verdict. Marketer is a secondary interoperability signal (CSV), not the primary payer story.

**If Advance — wedge to take into smoke/PRD:**  
- **Primary wedge:** agency / ops PMs and bookkeeping-style operators who already pay for Zapier and/or VA/assistant time because client onboarding still means multi-tool copy and recurring failures.  
- **Offer shape:** time-boxed **real pilot** (not a template dump) with explicit ownership of the client record, CSV import/export, and a migration path that does not require full cutover on day one.  
- **Secondary:** free-automation marketers only as a thin import/CSV path — not the monetization core.  
- **Anti-ICP for next round:** founders with no recent onboarding pain/spend who mainly compliment the pitch.

**If Reshape — new hypothesis:** *(not chosen)* Would apply if next 3–5 cold interviews show only free-stack marketers and no repeat paid labor/Zapier pattern.

**If Park — what would reopen:** *(not chosen)* Would need G1/G2 collapsing after more cold ICP (no recent incidents, no spend) and pilot interest disappearing.

**Concrete downstream handoff (next actions):**  
1. **`z-landing-smoke`** — copy aimed at “45 minutes copying client data across tools” / “still paying Zapier + manual work” / “VA still fixing weekly onboarding messes”; single CTA = **request pilot** (not “learn more”). Success: contact-intent from cold ops roles; fail: only founder-like compliments.  
2. **`z-write-prd`** — scope only: multi-tool onboarding copy reduction, pilot workflow, ownership model, CSV in/out, migration assist; non-goals: founder vanity features, full enterprise migration suite before pilot proof, anything requiring inventing unstated integrations.  
3. **Next interviews (discovery continuity):** +3–5 more **cold** bleeding+paying (agency PM, bookkeeper, consultant-like); screen out compliment-only; probe budget owner, last broken onboarding, and “what if nothing changes for 6 months.”
