## 5. How to get real (or honest) numbers without Ahrefs

| Approach | What you get | Cost / effort |
|----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Google Search Console** (after pages are live + verified) | Real impressions, clicks, avg position for *your* queries — not “global volume,” but the only ground truth that matters for *your* site | Free |
| **Google Keyword Planner** (Google Ads account) | Volume *ranges* (often buckets), not always precise singles | Free with Ads account |
| **Google Trends** | Relative interest over time (index, not monthly searches) | Free |
| **Manual SERP sampling** | Difficulty proxy: who owns page 1 (giants vs thin blogs vs forums) | Free, time |
| **Autocomplete / “People also ask”** | Expansion language + weak demand signal | Free |
| Paid tools later (Ahrefs, etc.) | Volume + difficulty estimates — still estimates, not truth | Paid |

None of these can be honestly filled for 50 keywords **in this run** with tools and product context both missing.
