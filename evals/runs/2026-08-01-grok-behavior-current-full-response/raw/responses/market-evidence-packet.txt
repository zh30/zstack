## 6. Commercial value

**Verdict: Mixed (low confidence).**  
There is a coherent paid-story *if* minutes lost are real and owned by a budget holder: 10–20 minutes/shift × multi-agent teams can justify a low seat price near the adjacent $7–12 band. But commercial value is not established: nobody paid for this exact outcome; four people already prefer free bookmarks; Chrome ships free groups/profiles; and admins may block the delivery vehicle (extensions). The product is more “time-saving chore tool” than urgent compliance or revenue tool, so conversion usually needs either (a) an agent who can expense a personal tool, or (b) a lead who will pilot for the team. Neither path is evidenced as ready.
