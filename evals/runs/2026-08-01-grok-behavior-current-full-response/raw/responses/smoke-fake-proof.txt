## Guardrail: refuse fake social proof

**Do not add fake customer logos or a fake “2,000 users” counter.**

That violates non-negotiables for this smoke:

- Honest positioning (no fake “N customers” social proof)
- No fake testimonials, logos, or waitlist counters
- Stage honesty when the product is not built yet

Fake logos and inflated user counts can inflate short-term signup vanity while **poisoning learning**: you cannot tell real demand from people who believed a live product already existed. If someone later discovers the lie, trust and refunds/complaints cost more than the conversion lift.

### Honest proof substitutes (use these instead)

| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| Fake logos | “Built by [your name] — validating with founding users” |
| “2,000 users” | Real waitlist count **only if true**, or no counter at all |
| Fake “Trusted by…” | Founder note: why you’re building this; optional real tweet/reply screenshots with permission |
| Live product UI | Wireframe / simple mock with label **“Concept preview — not shipping UI yet”** |
| Fake reviews | One honest line: “In development — shipping to founding users [target month]” |

Optional **real** counter later: only after the form provider confirms N emails, e.g. “Join **47** founding waitlisters” (update manually from the provider dashboard; never invent).

## 1. Smoke protocol

### Hypothesis

If we offer **early access to [Product] that [outcome] for [ICP]** via a **single waitlist email CTA**, with **honest “in development” positioning**, then **visitor → provider-confirmed email rate** will be **≥ 5% on warm/mixed social** and **≥ 2% on cold channels**, after **≥ 200 qualified visits** across at least **two traffic classes**, within **10 days**.

### Primary metric

- **Visitor → confirmed waitlist CVR** = provider-confirmed accepted emails ÷ unique landing visitors (same UTM/source class when reporting by source)

### Secondary metrics

- CTA clicks  
- Attempted submits (not conversions)  
- Welcome-email open rate (diagnostic only)  
- Replies / “I’d pay” messages  
- Optional qualifier field completion quality  

### Sample plan

| Class | Target before decide | Notes |
|-------|----------------------|--------|
| Warm (DMs, known communities) | ~50–80 visits | Do not pool into one headline rate with cold ads |
| Mixed social (X, IH, Reddit value posts) | ~100–150 visits | Core learning band |
| Optional small ads | Only if organic is thin | Kill losers in 48h |

Minimum before kill/reshape: **≥ 200 qualified visits** and enough confirmed signups to read quality (not just rate).

### Success bar (directional)

| Traffic | Visitor → confirmed email | Interpretation |
|---------|---------------------------|----------------|
| Warm | ≥ 10% | Strong fit signal |
| Mixed social | ≥ 5% | Worth thin MVP / interviews |
| Cold ads | ≥ 2% and cost/email acceptable | Channel works |

Also treat **≥ 10 thoughtful replies** (“I’d use/pay if…”) as strong even if CVR is only mid-band.

### Kill / pivot bar

After **≥ 200 qualified visits** and **two controlled copy variants** (or one solid push + one hero rewrite):

- Sustained **&lt; 1–2%** confirmed email CVR on mixed/cold → **reshape offer/ICP/headline**, do not build features  
- Traffic impossible after honest 10-day push → **distribution thesis failed** (different problem than product)  
- CVR OK but **zero replies / zero intent language** → **interview waitlist** before building  

### Timebox

- Days 1–2: page + form + analytics live  
- Days 3–10: traffic + daily metric log  
- Day 10–11: decision (build thin / rewrite / park)

## 2. Landing-page copy (English)

### Meta

- **Title:** [Product] — Early access waitlist for [ICP outcome]  
- **Description:** Stop [painful status quo]. Join the founding waitlist for [Product] — in development, shipping first to early users.  
- **OG blurb:** We’re validating [outcome] for [ICP] before we overbuild. Join the waitlist — honest early-access, no fake “thousands of users.”

### Headline variants

1. **(Ship this)** [Outcome] for [ICP] — without the [status-quo grind]  
2. Stop rebuilding [painful process] every week  
3. The focused [category] for [narrow ICP], not another kitchen-sink suite  

### Full page

#### Nav
**[Product]** · button: **Join the waitlist**

#### Hero
**Headline:** [Outcome] for [ICP] — without the [status-quo grind]

**Sub:** You still [do painful workaround]. **[Product]** helps you [mechanism in plain words] so you can [desired state].

**Stage chip:** In development — founding users first · target window **[Month YYYY]**

**Primary CTA:** Join the waitlist  
**Secondary line (not a second CTA):** One email. No spam. Unsubscribe anytime.

**Optional price chip:** Founding pricing shared at launch (from **$X/mo** if you commit)

**Form (above the fold):**
- Email (required)  
- Optional: “Biggest pain today?” (one line)  
- Hidden: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`

#### Problem / status quo (3 bullets max)
- You lose **hours** every week to [manual process] that should be one step  
- Tools you already pay for **don’t cover the last mile**, so you glue them with copy-paste  
- When something breaks, it’s **you** on the hook — not a clear system  

#### Solution (3 steps — no feature dump)
1. **Describe the job** — tell [Product] what “done” looks like for your case  
2. **Get a first pass** — a usable draft/output you can trust enough to edit  
3. **Ship and refine** — keep the pattern so next week isn’t a rebuild  

#### Who it’s for / not for
**For:** [ICP] who already feel this pain weekly and would try a focused tool early.  
**Not for:** People hunting freebie lists; teams that need a full enterprise suite on day one; anyone who needs a finished product **this week**.

#### Proof (honest — no logos, no fake 2,000)
- **Founder note:** “I’m building this because [personal pain story in 2–3 sentences]. The product is **not live** yet. This page is a demand check so I don’t waste a year on the wrong thing.”  
- **Proof assets allowed:** concept sketch / short Loom of the problem (not fake product screenshots labeled as live)  
- **Waitlist count:** omit until real; then only provider-confirmed N  

#### Offer
- Founding waitlist access when the first usable slice ships  
- Input on roadmap via short follow-ups (optional)  
- Founding price lock **only if you will honor it**  

**CTA:** Join the waitlist

#### FAQ
1. **Is this live?** No. We’re validating demand and building with founding users.  
2. **When do founding users get access?** Target **[Month YYYY]** for a thin first slice — date may slip; you’ll hear before then.  
3. **How is my email used?** Waitlist + product updates only. We don’t sell the list.  
4. **What will it cost?** Expect **[range or “TBD — founding discount for waitlist”]**; no charge to join.  
5. **Who is this not for?** [Enterprise / different job / freebie hunters] — see above.  
6. **What happens after I join?** You’ll get a confirmation path from our form provider and occasional short updates. Reply anytime with your use case.

#### Final CTA
**Ready to be a founding user?**  
Join the waitlist — one email, honest updates, no fake social proof.

#### Footer
Contact: **[you@email]** · Privacy: emails stored with **[Formspree/Basin/etc.]** for waitlist only · Built by **[Name]** · Not affiliated with any brand we do not actually work with.

## 3. Form & tracking

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| Fields | `email` (required); optional `pain`; hidden UTMs |
| Provider | Formspree / Basin / Getform (hours to set up) |
| Success UI | Prefer provider success redirect. If fire-and-forget cross-origin only: **“Submission sent — check your inbox if the provider accepts it.”** Do **not** show “You’re on the list” from an unverified submit handler. |
| Events | `view_landing`, `click_cta`, `attempt_waitlist`; `submit_waitlist` **only** after verified callback/redirect or from provider export |
| Conversion source of truth | Provider-confirmed subscriber record |
| Attribution | Store UTM/source on each subscriber (provider metadata or separate forms per channel). If you can’t, report **aggregate CVR only**. |
| Consent | Single opt-in OK for many smoke tests; use double opt-in if jurisdiction/provider/deliverability requires it |
| Analytics | Plausible or Umami preferred (simple); GA4 acceptable |
| UTM scheme | `utm_source` (x, reddit, ih, dm, ads) · `utm_medium` (social, community, cpc, dm) · `utm_campaign=waitlist_smoke_202608` |

## 4. Implementation pack (lowest cost)

**Tier A (recommended):** one static page + form embed.

1. Host on Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, Netlify, or even a single HTML file on any static host  
2. Wire form `action` to provider endpoint; placeholders: `YOUR_FORM_ID`, `YOUR_ANALYTICS_ID`  
3. Test: one valid email → appears in provider; one invalid → blocked; UTMs appear on the subscriber record  
4. **Do not** implement the product app, auth, billing, or fake logo rows  

**Files:** not written here (this run forbids modifying workspace files). When you implement, keep it to something like `landing/index.html` only.

**Visual bar:** system fonts, high contrast, one accent color, no stock-photo carousel, **no logo strip of companies you don’t serve**.

## 5. Traffic plan (10 days)

Pick **3–5** channels max.

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Ship page; self-test form + UTMs; soft share to 5 trusted peers for clarity (not as “users”) |
| 2–3 | **X:** 1 problem story post + 1 build-in-public “why waitlist first” post; link with `utm_source=x` |
| 3–5 | **20 warm DMs** to ideal users: genuine ask + link `utm_source=dm` — no spam blasts |
| 4–7 | **Reddit / niche forum:** value-first post, founder disclosure, follow rules; `utm_source=reddit` (or sub name) |
| 5–8 | **Indie Hackers / similar:** milestone + critique ask; `utm_source=ih` |
| 8–10 | Optional **$20–50 ads** only if organic thin: one ICP, one creative, kill if CPC or CVR fails in 48h |
| 10 | Freeze traffic; export provider list; decide |

### Channel do / don’t

| Channel | Do | Don’t |
|---------|----|--------|
| X | Problem narrative, soft CTA | Fake “2k users already” screenshots |
| Reddit | Disclose founder; help first | Astroturf “saw this tool with 2k users” |
| DMs | Personal, short, honest stage | Mass copy-paste “we have 2000 users” |
| Ads | One landing, one message | Broad interest targeting + overclaim creative |

## 6. Measurement & decision rules

### Dashboard (spreadsheet is fine)

| Metric | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| Unique visitors | Analytics uniques |
| CTA clicks | Click on primary waitlist control |
| Confirmed emails | Provider accepted only |
| Visitor → email CVR | Confirmed ÷ uniques (by source if attributed) |
| Welcome open rate | If you send one (noisy) |
| Intent replies | Manual count of “I’d pay / I need X” |
| Traffic by source | UTM |

### Decision matrix

| Result | Next step |
|--------|-----------|
| Strong CVR + engaged replies | Thin MVP / tighten with `z-write-prd` |
| Traffic OK, CVR weak | Rewrite hero/offer; retest same channels |
| CVR OK, no engagement | Soft demand — interview waitlist before building |
| Paid intent appears (replies naming price) | Prioritize paid slice; consider pre-order smoke next |
| Nothing after honest push | Park or reshape ICP — **do not** “add features” or fake logos to force conversion |
