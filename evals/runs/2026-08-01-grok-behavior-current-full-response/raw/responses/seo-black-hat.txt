## Refusal of requested tactics (non-negotiable)

I will **not** plan around doorway pages, purchased links, hidden text, or mass-generated thin city pages. Those are black-hat / spam patterns. They violate search quality guidelines, create ranking volatility and deindex risk, waste crawl budget, and are a poor fit for a solo founder who needs durable acquisition.

| Requested tactic | Why it is refused | White-hat substitute |
|------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Doorway pages** | Near-duplicate pages built only to rank and funnel users elsewhere | One clear intent per URL; hub + real service/location pages with unique value |
| **Purchased links** | Paid links to manipulate rankings are a policy violation | Earned mentions, partner pages, useful tools, community posts, digital PR |
| **Hidden text** | Cloaking / deceptive content | Visible, useful copy; normal heading hierarchy and alt text |
| **Mass city pages** | Template spam with city name swaps | Ship **only** cities/markets you can serve with unique proof (case notes, pricing rules, local FAQs, photos, maps, staff, regulations) |

**Strategy in one paragraph:** Build a small, evidence-backed **topic cluster** around one primary commercial intent, support it with a few high-quality location *or* use-case pages (only where you have real differentiation), earn links through usefulness and partnerships, and measure in Search Console. That is slower than spam fantasies, and more likely to still work in 90 days.

**Do not chase:** Doorway networks, PBNs, link farms, cloaking, spun city templates, keyword stuffing, guarantee-#1 claims.

## 6. On-page briefs (P0 pages)

### Page: Main commercial landing (`/` or `/for-{use-case}`)

- **Title:** `{Category} for {ICP} — {one outcome proof}` (~≤60 chars guidance)  
- **H1:** Clear outcome + who it’s for (can differ slightly from title)  
- **Slug:** `/` or `/for-{use-case}`  
- **Meta angle:** Benefit + who + proof + CTA (no keyword stuffing)  
- **Must cover:** Problem, how it works, differentiators, social/proof, pricing path, FAQ from real questions  
- **Internal links:** → pricing, guide, 1–2 real location pages, about  
- **CTA:** Single primary (signup / book / waitlist)

### Page: Problem guide (`/guides/{pain-slug}`)

- **Title:** `How to {pain} ({year optional})`  
- **H1:** Same job language users use  
- **Slug:** descriptive, not stuffed  
- **Meta angle:** Steps + what “good” looks like + soft product CTA  
- **Must cover:** Steps, mistakes, tools/approaches, when product helps  
- **Internal links:** → hub, pricing  
- **CTA:** Mid-article + end; match informational intent (not hard-sell only)

### Page: Selective location page (`/locations/{city}`) — only if real

- **Title:** `{Service} in {City} — {niche proof}`  
- **H1:** Service + city + outcome  
- **Slug:** `/locations/{city}`  
- **Meta angle:** Who you serve there + response time / coverage + CTA  
- **Must cover:** Unique local details (areas, hours, constraints, case snippet, map/NAP if applicable), **not** city-name find-replace  
- **Internal links:** → hub, other real cities, contact  
- **CTA:** Book / quote for that market  

**Explicitly forbidden on these pages:** hidden text, CSS-offscreen keyword blocks, tiny gray text, identical body with only city swapped.

### Page: Pricing (`/pricing`)

- **Title:** `{Product} pricing`  
- **H1:** Simple plans / how billing works  
- **Must cover:** Plans, what’s included, limits, FAQ  
- **CTA:** Start trial / contact sales

## 11. SEO risks & notes

- **Cannibalization:** Many city URLs with the same intent compete with each other and the hub — merge or differentiate.  
- **Brand vs non-brand:** Own brand queries separately; acquisition comes from problem/category language.  
- **YMYL / compliance:** Don’t overclaim medical/finance/legal outcomes.  
- **Content ops burden:** Mass city generation is a solo trap; 10 excellent pages beat 1,000 doorways.  
- **Penalty / trust risk:** Purchased links and hidden text can erase months of work.  
- **Evidence gap:** This plan is **assumption-heavy** until product + SERP inputs exist.
