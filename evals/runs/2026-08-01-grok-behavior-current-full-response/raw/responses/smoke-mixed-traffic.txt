## 3. What would *start* to justify building (still thin MVP, not “full product”)

Pre-register something like this before more spend:

| Gate | Suggested bar (tune to your offer) |
|------|-------------------------------------|
| Cold-only sample | ≥ **150–200** ICP-ish cold visits (not friends) with UTM + subscriber source preserved |
| Cold email CVR | **≥ ~3–5%** for low-friction waitlist on semi-targeted traffic; **≥ ~1–2%** floor before kill/reshape after honest copy variants |
| Warm | Report separately; **do not** count toward the cold success bar |
| Engagement | **≥ ~20–30%** of waitlist answer a short “why / would you pay?” follow-up, **or** several concrete “I’d pay for X” replies |
| Paid signal (stronger) | Any real pre-order/deposit or credible invoice request → paid thin slice, still not full product |
| Kill / reshape | After **200+** cold qualified visits and **two** copy variants, sustained **&lt;1–2%** cold email CVR → reshape offer/ICP, don’t “build more features” |

Until cold CVR **and** engagement clear those bars, **full product = premature**.
