## 7. Independent developer fit

**Verdict: Conditional (confidence: low).**

- **Build scope for MVP:** Feasible if scoped to one job (e.g. “turn time logs / proposals into draft invoices”) rather than full accounting.  
- **Support / ops burden:** Can become high (wrong amounts, tax fields, client disputes)—unquantified here.  
- **Distribution:** Unknown; freelancers hang out in communities that were **not** scanned.  
- **Regulation / liability:** Invoices touch money, tax, and legal documents—liability risk is material for an indie; **not legal advice**, flag for later.  
- **Skills match:** Unknown (founder skills not provided).

Solo fit improves only with a **narrow, low-liability wedge** and manual-in-the-loop drafts—not autonomous tax/AR for multi-entity companies.

## 9. If you go deeper — entry directions

### Primary wedge (recommended *after* research, not before)

- **Who:** Independent freelancers who already invoice monthly from proposals/time logs (not full bookkeeping).  
- **Job-to-be-done:** “Turn completed work into a correct client-ready invoice draft in minutes.”  
- **MVP shape:** Concierge or semi-manual AI draft + human confirm before send (no auto-file taxes).  
- **Where to find first 10 users:** Buyer subreddits / freelance Discords / local freelancers — **after** unblocking search or offline outreach.  
- **Metric in 14 days:** ≥5 interviews with a past-behavior story of invoicing friction + ≥1 paid pilot commitment.

### Backup wedges

1. **AR follow-up assistant** for freelancers who already create invoices but hate chasing payment (still high support risk).  
2. **PDF/email invoice data extraction → accounting CSV** for bookkeepers (B2B workflow; sales cycle may be longer).

### Explicitly do **not** start with

- “AI accounting OS for all SMBs”  
- Autonomous send/charge without human confirm  
- Broad horizontal “ChatGPT for invoices” with no ICP  
- Competing head-on with full QuickBooks/Xero feature parity
