## 1. Executive summary

- **Primary keyword (main money term):** `agency client onboarding workflow`
- **Primary page:** `/` (homepage rewrite as cluster hub)
- **Strategy in one paragraph:** Own one problem-language cluster—**client onboarding workflow for small agencies with clear step ownership**—not generic “agency software.” Supplied notes say broad software SERPs are CRM/portal-heavy (poor fit for a zero-authority domain) while agency workflow / owner-specific results look mixed and community language centers on *who owns the next step* and *why checklists don’t get done*. With ~6 hours/week, rewrite the homepage for the commercial primary, refresh the checklist article as the informational spoke (different intent, no cannibalization), keep pricing transactional, and ship a few ownership-framed long-tails. Expand only after GSC shows impressions on the cluster.
- **Do not chase:** Agency CRM / client portal / “agency software” head terms, generic automation, and broad “checklist software” SERPs dominated by large vendors.

## 6. On-page briefs (P0 pages)

### Page: `/` (homepage — P0)

- **Title:** Agency Client Onboarding Workflow with Clear Ownership | {Brand}
- **H1:** Client onboarding workflow for agencies—know who owns the next step
- **Slug:** `/`
- **Meta angle:** Small agencies get a repeatable onboarding workflow where every step has an owner—so checklists actually finish. CTA: start / book demo.
- **Must cover:**
  - Problem: stalled onboarding, unclear ownership, checklists that die in docs
  - Job: run client onboarding as a workflow, not a static list
  - Differentiator: ownership-aware steps (who, by when, what’s blocked)
  - Process overview (high-level stages)—not a full CRM feature dump
  - Who it’s for: small agencies (and who it’s not: enterprise portal buyers)
  - Proof placeholders: founder story, early user quotes when real; no invented metrics
  - CTA above fold + after process section
- **Internal links:** → checklist article (“full checklist”), → ownership post, → “why checklists fail” post, → pricing
- **CTA:** Primary signup/demo; secondary “see the checklist”

### Page: Checklist article (P0)

- **Title:** Client Onboarding Checklist for Agencies (With Owners per Step)
- **H1:** Client onboarding checklist for agencies
- **Slug:** keep existing if indexed; prefer `/blog/client-onboarding-checklist-agencies` or equivalent single stable URL
- **Meta angle:** Step-by-step agency client onboarding checklist with a named owner per item—so work doesn’t stall after kickoff.
- **Must cover:** Phased checklist; **owner role per step**; common failure modes (brief); link to product workflow for enforcement; FAQ (2–4 real Qs only)
- **Internal links:** → homepage (workflow product), → pricing, → “why checklists fail” when live
- **CTA:** Soft: “Turn this checklist into an ownership-aware workflow”

### Page: Ownership long-tail post (P0)

- **Title:** Who Owns the Next Step in Client Onboarding?
- **H1:** Who owns the next step in client onboarding?
- **Slug:** `/blog/who-owns-next-step-client-onboarding`
- **Meta angle:** Onboarding stalls when ownership is implicit. How small agencies assign the next step.
- **Must cover:** Handoff patterns; RACI-lite for agencies; link to checklist + homepage
- **Internal links:** → `/`, → checklist article
- **CTA:** Product trial/demo

### Page: `/pricing` (align only)

- **Title:** Pricing | {Brand} ownership-aware onboarding
- **H1:** Pricing
- **Primary KW:** none competitive—brand/transactional
- **Must cover:** Plans, limits, what “ownership-aware” means in-product; link back to homepage value prop

## 11. SEO risks & notes

- **Cannibalization:** Homepage and checklist must not share the same primary; checklist = checklist intent only.
- **Brand vs non-brand:** Brand queries will be near-zero until awareness; all acquisition work is non-brand problem language.
- **YMYL / compliance:** Not medical/finance; still avoid unverifiable ROI claims.
- **Content ops (solo):** Prefer depth on 3–4 URLs over 15 thin posts; 6 hrs/week cannot support a media company.
- **Category trap:** Sliding into “agency software” messaging invites CRM SERPs the domain cannot win.
- **Evidence quality:** Plan is executable but **search demand is unproven** until GSC/SERP work is done live.
