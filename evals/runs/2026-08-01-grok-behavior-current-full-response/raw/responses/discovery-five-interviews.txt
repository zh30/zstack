## 7. Decision

**Verdict: Advance** (narrow wedge only — not a broad build)

**Why:**  
Three of five show **bleeding + paying** behavior (time and/or money/labor). G1 and G2 clear enough in agency PM / bookkeeper / consultant cluster. G3 is mixed but not dead: pilot ask + prior switches + concrete barriers that product can design around. Founder is polite-only and must not drive the roadmap. This is pattern detection for smoke/thin pilot scope — **not** market sizing or a large multi-feature build.

**If Advance — wedge to take into smoke/PRD:**  
- **Who:** Agency / ops people who onboard clients across tools and already pay automation or VA/assistant labor.  
- **Job:** Cut repeated multi-tool copy and weekly failure loops during client onboarding.  
- **Must-haves implied by evidence:** clear ownership model; CSV import/export; migration story; sensitive-file handling.  
- **Immediate next:** Scope a **real pilot** with the agency PM (they asked); use bookkeeper constraints as security requirements; treat marketer CSV as onboarding path; do not optimize for compliment-only founders.

**If Reshape — new hypothesis:** (secondary, if pilot fails)  
Pain is “client data handoff + ownership under compliance,” not generic onboarding UX — re-ICP to bookkeepers/agency PMs only and drop marketer/founder messaging.

**If Park — what would reopen:** N/A for primary verdict. Would reopen a Park only if pilot refusal + no further cold bleeding+paying interviews in next 5.
