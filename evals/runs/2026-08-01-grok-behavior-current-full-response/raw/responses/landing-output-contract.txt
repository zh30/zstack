## 1. Hypothesis

If we offer **one-click restore of client-specific Chrome tab workspaces** to **hands-on support agents at small US SaaS teams**, via a single **early-access waitlist** CTA and a truthful **from $9/month** planned price, then within **10 days**:

- **Paid cold ads** alone: **≥ 2%** confirmed CVR **and** **≤ $25** cost per confirmed email after **≥ $80** spend and **≥ 80** ad-attributed unique visitors; else kill or rewrite ads before burning the full $150.
- **Warm outreach** (tracked separately): **≥ 15%** confirmed CVR after **≥ 25** warm-attributed unique visitors, **or** **≥ 5** confirmed warm signups with **≥ 2** “I’d try / I’d pay if…” replies.

**Never pool warm outreach with cold traffic** for headline CVR, success, or kill decisions.

## 2. Metrics & decision rules

### Funnel definitions

| Metric | Definition | Source of truth |
|--------|------------|-----------------|
| Unique visitors | Plausible unique pageviews on landing URL, segmented by UTM / referrer class | Plausible |
| CTA clicks | Clicks on primary CTA (`click_cta`) | Plausible custom event |
| Attempted waitlist | Form submit fired (`attempt_waitlist`) — **not** a conversion | Plausible |
| Confirmed waitlist | Email accepted into Buttondown list (active/pending per double opt-in policy) | **Buttondown subscriber record only** |
| Visitor → email CVR | Confirmed waitlist ÷ unique visitors **within the same source class** | Plausible denom + Buttondown numerator with matching source metadata |
| Welcome open rate | Opens on welcome email (diagnostic only) | Buttondown |
| Intent signals | Replies with real client-context pain, “I’d pay”, or ask for beta | Inbox / Buttondown replies |
| Revenue | Not collected this smoke | N/A |
| Traffic by source | UTMs + Buttondown `source` / tag / metadata | Both systems must align |

**Conversion integrity:** Count conversion only when Buttondown confirms the subscriber. Invalid, rejected, duplicate, or merely attempted submissions are **not** conversions. Do **not** fire a client-side success conversion event from the submit handler. Do **not** show “You’re on the list” unless a verified provider success path exists.

### Source-specific thresholds (pre-registered)

| Source class | Min sample before decide | Success bar | Kill / pivot bar |
|--------------|--------------------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Warm** | ≥ 25 unique warm visitors **or** end of day 10 with ≥ 15 warm visits | CVR ≥ 15% **or** ≥ 5 confirmed + ≥ 2 qualified replies | After ≥ 25 warm visits, CVR &lt; 8% **and** 0 qualified replies → warm channel message failed (not global product death) |
| **Cold community** | ≥ 80 unique **or** ≥ 5 confirmed | CVR ≥ 4% **or** ≥ 5 confirmed + ≥ 1 reply with real workflow detail | After ≥ 80 visits, CVR &lt; 1.5% across honest posts → reshape offer/hero before more community posts |
| **Cold social** | ≥ 60 unique **or** ≥ 4 confirmed | CVR ≥ 3.5% **or** ≥ 4 confirmed + any reply | After ≥ 60 visits, CVR &lt; 1.5% → change creative/thread angle, not ICP yet |
| **Cold ads** | ≥ $80 spend **and** ≥ 80 ad uniques | CVR ≥ 2% **and** CPE ≤ $25 | After $80, CPE &gt; $40 **or** CVR &lt; 1% → pause ads; do not auto-spend remaining $70 on same creative |
| **Aggregate cold** (community + social + ads only) | ≥ 150 cold uniques | ≥ 4% cold CVR **or** ≥ 8 cold confirmed with ≥ 2 intent replies | After ≥ 200 cold uniques and cold CVR &lt; 1.5% with weak replies → **reshape promise/ICP**, do not build extension |
| **Pooled all traffic** | — | **Forbidden** for pass/fail | Warm must never dilute or inflate cold rates |

### Secondary diagnostics (not pass/fail alone)

- Welcome open rate: compare to your Buttondown baseline; 0 opens → check deliverability before killing demand.
- Qualifier field completion (“Biggest pain when switching clients?”): quality signal; lower CVR is an acceptable tradeoff.
- CTA click → attempt rate: if high click, low attempt → form friction; if low click → hero mismatch.

### Decision matrix

| Result | Next step |
|--------|-----------|
| **Strong** — cold success bar met **and** ≥ 2 intent replies (any class, logged separately) | Thin MVP scope → `z-write-prd`; interview waitlist before coding full workspace sync |
| **Warm strong, cold weak** | Message works 1:1; rewrite landing for cold strangers; retest cold only 5–7 days; **do not** treat warm CVR as market proof |
| **Cold OK CVR, zero engagement** | Soft demand; run 5–8 waitlist interviews (`z-customer-discovery`) before build |
| **Traffic OK, CVR weak** | One hero/headline rewrite; keep channels; retest same source classes |
| **Ads hopeless early** | Kill ads; keep organic; do not burn full $150 |
| **Nothing after honest push + min samples** | Park or reshape ICP (e.g. broader “CS ops” vs agent-only); **do not** build more extension features |
| **Any credible “invoice me / take my card”** | Capture as paid-intent lead; still not scale proof; prioritize paid-slice learning after smoke |

#### Problem / status quo (3 bullets max)
- You jump clients all day and lose 5–15 minutes reopening the right CRM, status page, billing admin, and last thread.  
- Bookmarks and “pin these tabs” fall apart when two clients share similar tools but different accounts.  
- Shared docs and tribal “here’s my setup” notes don’t travel with the ticket you’re on right now.  

#### Solution (3 steps)
1. **Save a workspace per client** — Capture the tab set you actually use for that account (names you choose, e.g. “Acme · L2”).  
2. **Switch with one click** — Open that client’s workspace when the next ticket hits—no manual rebuild.  
3. **Stay in Chrome** — Built as an extension for the browser you already live in during support shifts.

**For**
- Hands-on support / CS agents at small US SaaS companies who live in Chrome across many accounts

**Not for**
- Enterprise IT wanting full VDI / remote browser isolation (different category)  
- People who only need a general session manager with no client naming model  
- Teams banned from installing extensions without a long security review (smoke won’t solve procurement)
