FAQ

Before you join

Is Client Restore available now?

No. It is a planned Chrome extension, and this waitlist is testing whether the problem is worth solving. There is no firm ship date.

What would it cost?

Planned pricing starts at $9/month. That is a genuine pricing hypothesis, not a locked offer; scope and final pricing may change as we learn.

What does “one-click restore” mean?

The planned core action is to reopen a saved set of client-specific tabs from the extension in one click. Exact permissions, behavior, and limits still need to be designed and tested.

What about client privacy?

The intended product should minimize data collection and explain every Chrome permission. The storage model has not been finalized. For this waitlist, Buttondown stores your email and optional response; Plausible measures aggregate page activity without cross-site advertising cookies.

What happens after I join?

Buttondown will send a confirmation or welcome email, depending on the final list settings. We may ask about your workflow and will send occasional product updates. You can unsubscribe anytime.

Is this for whole-team workspace sharing?

Not in this smoke test. The first question is whether individual support agents value fast, client-specific context restore.
