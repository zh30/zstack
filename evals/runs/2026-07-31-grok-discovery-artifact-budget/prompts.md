# Prompt

## discovery-five-interviews

> Synthesize five client-onboarding interviews. Four are cold and one warm. An agency PM repeatedly spends 45 minutes copying across tools, pays for Zapier, abandoned a template over unclear ownership, and asked for a real pilot. A consultant pays an assistant but named migration as a barrier. A founder had no recent pain or spend and only complimented the pitch. A bookkeeper has weekly failures, paid VA labor, and sensitive-file concerns. A marketer uses free automation, has switched tools before, and wants CSV. Treat these summaries as the full evidence; do not invent quotes. Audit evidence, segment the sample, score G1/G2/G3 with confidence separate, choose Advance/Reshape/Park, and produce a concrete downstream handoff.
