## Verdict

# Advance

**Not** “build the full product.” **Advance** means: gates support a **smoke test + scoped pilot** aimed at the bleeding+paying segment — not a broad roadmap green light from compliments or from the warm founder.

---

## Why

| Gate | Score | Confidence | Role in decision |
|------|-------|------------|------------------|
| G1 Pain | 4 | medium | Timed repeat copy (**01**), weekly failures (**04**), labor patches (**02**, **04**) |
| G2 Spend | 4 | medium | Zapier + assistant + VA = people already pay for incomplete solutions |
| G3 Switch | 3 | medium | Pilot ask + abandoned template + prior tool switches; offset by migration & sensitive-file barriers |

**Why not Reshape:** Pain and spend already cluster on a clear wedge (ops roles running client onboarding with multi-tool handoffs). ICP is refinable without abandoning the problem space.

**Why not Park:** Three of five interviews show real cost-bearing workarounds; one cold interviewee asked for a **real pilot**. Parking would ignore the strongest behavioral signals.

**Why not “large build” Advance:** G3 is only 3; notes are summary-grade; no $ figures; security and migration are unresolved. Large build would overfit thin notes.

---

## If Advance — wedge to take into smoke / pilot / PRD

**Wedge one-liner:**  
Help **agency and bookkeeping operators** who already **pay Zapier or human labor** stop **losing 45+ minutes (or weekly failures)** to **cross-tool client onboarding copy-paste**, with **clear step ownership** and **low-migration / CSV-friendly** adoption — and a path that respects **sensitive files**.

**Primary segment:** Bleeding + paying — Agency PM (**01**), Bookkeeper (**04**); Consultant (**02**) if migration is designed out of the critical path.

**Ignore for demand:** Warm founder praise (**03**).

**Price / packaging hypothesis (to test, not assume):** Pilot priced against a fraction of VA/assistant or Zapier+time waste — only after $ amounts are collected in pilot sales calls.

**First design constraints from G3:**

1. Named ownership on every onboarding step (template death mode from **01**).
2. Coexistence > cutover (migration barrier **02**).
3. CSV in/out early (**05**).
4. Explicit sensitive-file policy before bookkeeper pilot (**04**).

---

## If Reshape — (not chosen; kept for audit trail)

Would apply if next pilots showed only marketers on free tools or only founders with no ops pain. **New hypothesis then:** e.g. “CSV glue for marketers” or abandon onboarding for a different workflow. **Not supported as primary read of this N=5.**

---

## If Park — (not chosen; reopen criteria)

Reopen only if:

- 5+ additional cold ICP interviews show no recent timed pain and no spend, or
- Pilot offers get zero budget-owner engagement, or
- Security requirements make a solo-shippable product impossible for the bookkeeper segment *and* agency segment also refuses pilot terms.

### Explicit non-goals for PRD

- Validating via compliments or warm demos
- Requiring full stack migration before first value
- Building deep integrations before CSV + one coexistence path
- Positioning for free-automation-only marketers as primary ICP
- Ingesting arbitrary sensitive client files without a stated trust model

### Phrases for landing copy (paraphrase of evidence — not fake quotes)

- “Forty-five minutes copying the same client across tools”
- “You already pay Zapier — and still re-type the handoff”
- “The template died because nobody owned the next step”
- “Weekly onboarding failures that your VA has to clean up”
- “Switch without a migration project — CSV in, clear owners, pilot in two weeks”
