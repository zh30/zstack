## Smoke protocol

**Assumption:** This is an unbuilt product testing free early-access interest from a narrow audience.

- **Hypothesis:** If `[Product]` promises `[specific outcome]` to `[narrow ICP]`, qualified visitors will join the waitlist.
- **Primary metric:** Qualified unique visitor → provider-confirmed waitlist signup.
- **Secondary signals:** CTA clicks, qualifying answers, welcome-email replies, and credible willingness-to-pay messages.
- **Timebox:** 14 days.
- **Minimum sample:** 200 qualified cold/mixed visitors across two headline variants. Keep warm outreach separate.
- **Continue:** At least 5% confirmed conversion from cold/mixed traffic, at least 10 confirmed signups, and 3 substantive replies describing a real use case.
- **Interview before building:** Conversion reaches 5%, but nobody replies or describes urgent pain.
- **Rewrite and retest:** 2–5% conversion or strong clicks with weak signup completion.
- **Pivot/park:** Each of two variants remains below 2% after at least 100 qualified cold/mixed visits per variant, with no substantive replies.
- **Evidence caution:** These thresholds are preregistered hypotheses, not results.

## Implementation pack

Use one static page on Cloudflare Pages with a hosted form provider such as Formspree, Basin, or Buttondown. No custom application backend is needed yet.

Capture:

- `email` — required
- `biggest_pain` — optional qualifier
- `utm_source`
- `utm_medium`
- `utm_campaign`
- `utm_content`
- Page variant, such as `hero_a`

The optional qualifier will probably reduce total conversion but improve signup quality.

Track these distinct events:

- `landing_view`
- `waitlist_cta_click`
- `attempt_waitlist`
- `confirmed_waitlist`

Only count `confirmed_waitlist` after the provider accepts the address. If the form cannot expose a verified callback or controlled success redirect, show:

> Submission sent. Check your inbox if the provider accepts it.

Do not show “You’re on the list” from a client-side submit handler alone. Treat the provider’s confirmed subscriber export as the source of truth.

Before launch, submit one valid and one invalid address and confirm:

- Invalid submissions are excluded.
- UTMs reach the confirmed subscriber record.
- Each traffic source remains attributable.
- Consent and unsubscribe behavior work.
- Emails are stored only by the named provider.

Use double opt-in where required or where list quality matters more than raw signup volume. Pick one lightweight analytics tool—Plausible or Umami is sufficient.

## Decision rules

- **Strong conversion plus detailed replies:** Interview the best matches, test a price, then define a thin MVP.
- **Good conversion but silent subscribers:** Do not build yet; interview them and test urgency.
- **Clicks but few confirmed signups:** Fix the offer, trust, form friction, or provider behavior.
- **Weak results from adequate qualified traffic:** Narrow or change the ICP/problem and repeat once.
- **No qualified traffic:** The distribution thesis failed; that does not independently disprove the product problem.
- **A payment or credible “invoice me” request:** Treat it as a strong lead, not proof of a scalable market.
