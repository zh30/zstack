Questions before you join

Is Switchboard available now?

No. It is an unbuilt concept in demand validation. Joining expresses interest in early access; it does not guarantee access or a delivery date.

What will it cost?

Pricing is planned to start at $9/month. Final plans and features may change as we validate the product.

When will it ship?

There is no firm ship date. We will share occasional progress updates and be candid if plans change.

What about client privacy?

Privacy and permissions are part of the product design, not a finished claim. The intended model is to save only the workspace information needed to restore tabs; early users will help define the controls before development.

What happens after I join?

Buttondown will ask you to confirm your email. After confirmation, you will receive a short welcome note asking about your current workflow. You can unsubscribe anytime.

How will you use my email?

Only for early-access research and product updates. It will be stored by Buttondown and will not be sold.
