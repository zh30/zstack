## 7. Decision

**Verdict: Advance—only to a narrow concierge pilot, not a broad product build.**

The evidence clears the threshold for testing the agency-PM wedge: recurring quantified pain, current tool spend, a failed alternative, and a request for a real pilot. It does not support a general-purpose onboarding platform across agencies, consultants, bookkeeping, and marketing.

Pilot hypothesis:

> Agency PMs who repeatedly copy client-onboarding data across tools will adopt a lightweight workflow that preserves their existing tools, makes ownership explicit, and provides CSV portability.

### Pilot scope

Include:

- One agency PM and one real onboarding workflow
- Existing-tool integration or a manual concierge bridge
- Explicit owner and status for every handoff
- CSV import/export
- Baseline and post-pilot measurements

Exclude initially:

- Full migration
- Broad workflow builder
- Bookkeeping or other sensitive-file storage
- Multiple vertical-specific templates
- Claims of autonomous or error-free automation

### Concrete downstream handoff

Run a 14-day concierge pilot with I01.

Before starting, record:

- Current 45-minute task baseline
- Frequency per onboarding
- Tools and fields involved
- Current Zapier cost
- Who owns the budget and workflow
- What “unclear ownership” meant operationally

Pass criteria:

- The participant supplies a real workflow and sample data
- Completes at least two real onboarding runs
- Manual copying falls from 45 minutes to 15 minutes or less per run
- Ownership is clear without founder intervention
- Participant agrees to continue with a paid pilot or redirects existing automation budget

Fail/reshape criteria:

- Pilot is not scheduled or real data is not supplied within 14 days
- Migration/setup requires more effort than the saved work
- Ownership remains unclear
- Participant refuses any paid continuation after demonstrated savings

In parallel, recruit five more cold agency PMs with the same cross-tool trigger. Advance toward a thin MVP only if at least three report a recent repeated incident and at least two show existing spend or agree to a real pilot. Otherwise reshape around a different workflow or park.
