## Replace the requested tactics

| Requested tactic | Safe replacement |
|---|---|
| Doorway pages | One strong service hub with useful filters or regional sections; create separate pages only for materially distinct intent |
| Purchased links | Digital PR, integration partnerships, original data, expert contributions, and relevant directories; qualify sponsored placements appropriately |
| Hidden text | Put useful explanatory copy visibly in the page, using summaries, accordions, tables, and FAQs where appropriate |
| Mass-generated city pages | Publish location pages only for areas genuinely served and supply unique local proof, logistics, pricing context, examples, and contacts |

A city page should not exist merely because a city name can be inserted into a template. A defensible page needs most of the following:

- Actual availability or operational relevance
- Location-specific service details and limitations
- Original local examples, case studies, testimonials, or data
- Local pricing, delivery times, regulations, or workflows
- A distinct title, body, FAQ, and conversion path
- Links from a useful locations hub
- Ongoing ownership and accuracy checks

Near-duplicate combinations such as every city × every service should be consolidated or excluded from indexing.

## P0 on-page brief

For the main conversion page:

- **Title:** `[Core Category] for [Primary Customer] | [Brand]`
- **H1:** `[Core outcome] for [Primary Customer]`
- **Slug:** `/` or `/[core-category]/`
- **Meta angle:** Outcome, differentiator, credible proof, and one CTA
- **Must cover:** customer problem, workflow, capabilities, limitations, proof, pricing path, FAQs, and next step
- **CTA:** Start, book, request a quote, or join—choose one primary action
- **Internal links:** Pricing, use case, comparison, guide, and legitimate location hub

Avoid stuffing city names or keyword variants into headings, footers, alt text, or visually concealed elements.

## 7/30/90-day execution

**Next 7 days**

1. Define the product, primary customer, conversion, and single target market.
2. Choose one commercial query after inspecting its live results.
3. Publish or rewrite the main conversion page.
4. Select three P0 long-tails and map each to one URL.
5. Audit existing pages for hidden text, doorway behavior, duplicate city copy, and questionable outbound or inbound link arrangements.

**Within 30 days**

1. Publish the three P0 supporting pages.
2. Create a legitimate locations hub; launch only one to three evidence-rich location pages.
3. Add contextual internal links throughout the cluster.
4. Set up Search Console and conversion tracking.
5. Pursue several relevant editorial mentions through partnerships, useful data, integrations, or expert resources.

**Within 90 days**

1. Expand only queries earning impressions or qualified conversions.
2. Improve pages with impressions but weak click-through or engagement.
3. Consolidate overlapping pages.
4. Add location pages only where operational evidence and distinct content exist.
5. Retire pages that cannot become genuinely useful.

## Measurement

Track:

- Search Console impressions, clicks, positions, and queries by page
- Organic leads, signups, or purchases
- Index coverage for intended pages
- Brand versus non-brand traffic
- Performance of location pages compared with the central hub
- Editorial referring domains and referral conversions—not raw link count

Initial success should mean growing qualified impressions and conversions from the chosen cluster, not an arbitrary number of indexed pages.

## Avoid or defer

- Thousands of city/service permutations
- Swapped place names in otherwise identical copy
- Expired-domain networks, private blog networks, and paid ranking links
- White-on-white text, off-screen keyword blocks, or cloaking
- Fake offices, reviews, authors, or local proof
- Competitor pages without accurate comparisons
- Head terms until live results show a realistic opening
