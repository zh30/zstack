## 1. Smoke protocol

### Hypothesis

If hands-on support agents at small US SaaS teams are offered one-click restoration of client-specific tab workspaces, qualified cold visitors will join an early-access waitlist at the source-specific rates below, and some subscribers will reply with a concrete workflow or price-intent signal.

### Primary metric

Confirmed visitor-to-waitlist conversion rate:

```text
Buttondown double-opt-in-confirmed subscribers attributed to source
÷
Plausible unique US visitors attributed to the same source
```

Do not count CTA clicks, form attempts, rejected addresses, duplicates, unconfirmed subscriptions, or visible success messages as conversions.

### Secondary metrics

- CTA click rate.
- Valid form-attempt rate.
- Double-opt-in completion rate.
- Welcome-email delivery and open rate, used only as operational diagnostics.
- Qualified replies describing a current workaround.
- Replies saying that planned pricing from $9/month is plausible.
- Cost per confirmed subscriber for paid search.
- Attribution completeness.
- Traffic volume achieved per source.

### Qualified visitor

A unique landing-page visitor who:

- Arrived through a registered source/UTM combination.
- Was located in the US according to Plausible.
- Was not an internal tester, known bot, or obvious referral-spam visit.
- Viewed the landing page, not merely an ad impression.

Do not require a CTA click or minimum time on page; doing so would bias conversion upward.

### Evidence separation

Maintain separate reporting sheets or dashboard slices for:

- `cold_google`
- `cold_reddit`
- `cold_linkedin`
- `cold_support_community`
- `warm_dm`

Never combine `warm_dm` traffic or conversions with any cold source, including in an “overall” conversion rate. Cold sources should also retain individual rates.

### Pre-registered source thresholds

Rates below are directional hypotheses, not established benchmarks.

| Source | Minimum before verdict | Continue signal | Retest zone | Weak/pivot signal |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Cold Google Search ads | 50 qualified visitors | ≥4% and ≥3 confirmed subscribers | 2–3.99% or only 1–2 confirmations | <2% or zero confirmations |
| Cold Reddit | 60 qualified visitors | ≥6% and ≥4 confirmations | 2–5.99% or 2–3 confirmations | <2% or ≤1 confirmation |
| Cold LinkedIn organic | 40 qualified visitors | ≥7.5% and ≥3 confirmations | 2.5–7.49% or 1–2 confirmations | <2.5% or zero confirmations |
| Cold support community | 30 qualified visitors | ≥10% and ≥3 confirmations | 3–9.99% or 1–2 confirmations | <3% or zero confirmations |
| Warm personal outreach | 20 qualified visitors | ≥15% and ≥3 confirmations | 5–14.99% or 1–2 confirmations | <5% or zero confirmations |

If a source does not reach its minimum, classify it as `inconclusive—distribution`, not as evidence against the product.

### Engagement thresholds

After at least 20 delivered welcome emails:

- Open rate below 35%: investigate deliverability, subject line, and double-opt-in flow. Do not treat it as a demand failure.
- Strong engagement: at least three qualified replies, including at least two describing a repeated client-switching workflow.
- Stronger price intent: at least two independent replies saying the planned price is reasonable, asking to buy, or asking how soon they can use it.
- A reply is not a payment. No payment demand is tested by this waitlist.

### Timebox discipline

Run for exactly 10 calendar days. Do not extend merely because results are weak. Extend only when a documented operational failure—broken form, lost attribution, ad rejection, or deliverability problem—invalidated part of the test.

## 4. Implementation package

No files were modified because the request explicitly prohibits it. The intended minimal deployment consists of:

```text
/index.html
/confirmed/index.html
/privacy/index.html
/functions/api/waitlist.js
/public/og-clienttabs.png
```

### Cloudflare Pages

- Serve a mobile-first static page.
- Use semantic HTML and system fonts.
- Set `BUTTONDOWN_API_KEY` as a secret environment variable, never client-side.
- Set `PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN` and `BUTTONDOWN_LIST_ID` as configuration values.
- Disable indexing on preview deployments.
- Add the production domain in Plausible.
- Exclude internal QA visits using Plausible’s recommended current mechanism.
- Avoid logging raw email addresses in the Pages Function.
- Add a strict allowlist for stored UTM and traffic-class values.
- Apply server-side email validation, a honeypot, and rate limiting.
- Add CAPTCHA/Turnstile only if actual bot traffic appears; it adds friction and is not part of the initial test.

### Form fields

| Field | Required | Handling |
|---|---:|---|
| `email` | Yes | Validate server-side; send only to Buttondown |
| `workflow_note` | No | Maximum 500 characters; store with subscriber only if Buttondown supports safe metadata |
| `utm_source` | Hidden | Allowlisted value |
| `utm_medium` | Hidden | Allowlisted value |
| `utm_campaign` | Hidden | Must equal `clienttabs_smoke_202608` |
| `utm_content` | Hidden | Creative/post identifier |
| `traffic_class` | Hidden | Derived server-side; never trust arbitrary client input |
| `lp_variant` | Hidden | Initially `a` |
| `landing_path` | Hidden | Expected production landing path |
| `company_website` | Honeypot | Reject silently if populated |

Do not collect client names, ticket contents, URLs, or confidential support data.

### Pages Function contract

`POST /api/waitlist`

1. Validate origin, method, email, lengths, honeypot, and rate limit.
2. Normalize UTMs through an allowlist.
3. Derive `traffic_class` server-side from the registered UTM map.
4. Request a double-opt-in subscription from Buttondown.
5. Preserve attribution using provider-supported subscriber metadata or source-specific tags.
6. Return an attempt acknowledgement after Buttondown accepts the request.
7. Do not return “confirmed” merely because the subscription request was accepted.
8. Treat Buttondown’s confirmed subscriber record as the conversion source of truth.

Suggested responses:

```json
{ "status": "confirmation_required" }
```

```json
{ "status": "already_subscribed" }
```

```json
{ "status": "invalid_email" }
```

```json
{ "status": "provider_error" }
```

Before implementation, verify against current Buttondown documentation:

- Double-opt-in behavior.
- Subscriber metadata and tag support.
- Confirmation redirect support.
- Export fields for confirmation status and source attribution.
- Webhook availability and authentication.

If Buttondown cannot retain the normalized source on each confirmed subscriber, create distinct provider-native forms or tags for every source. Do not calculate source-specific conversion rates until a real confirmation proves attribution survives.

### Consent and privacy

Use double opt-in because this test values list quality and attributable intent more than raw signup volume.

The privacy page should state:

- Controller/contact identity.
- Email provider: Buttondown.
- Analytics provider: Plausible.
- Data collected and purpose.
- Retention policy.
- Unsubscribe/deletion method.
- That the list is not sold.
- That no product browsing data is collected because the extension does not exist.

This is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## 5. Analytics and event specification

Install the current official Plausible snippet from the Plausible dashboard. Its exact API syntax must be verified because web access is unavailable.

| Event | When fired | Properties | Counts as conversion? |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Automatic pageview | Landing page load | UTM segmentation handled by Plausible | No |
| `cta_click` | User activates a nav, hero, offer, or final CTA | `location`, `traffic_class`, `lp_variant` | No |
| `waitlist_attempt` | Browser validation passes and POST begins | `traffic_class`, `lp_variant` | No |
| `waitlist_request_accepted` | Server says Buttondown accepted the request | `traffic_class`, `lp_variant` | No |
| `waitlist_error` | Validation/provider request fails | `error_type`, `traffic_class`, `lp_variant` | No |
| `confirmation_page_view` | Confirmation redirect is reached | `lp_variant` only if reliably preserved | Diagnostic only |
| `waitlist_confirmed` | Only from a verified Buttondown confirmation webhook or equivalent | `traffic_class`, `lp_variant`—never email | Yes, if verified |

Do not send email addresses, free-text qualifier answers, client names, or other personal data to Plausible.

If there is no authenticated confirmation webhook, omit `waitlist_confirmed` from Plausible. Export confirmed subscribers from Buttondown and use that export as the numerator.

### QA before traffic

Run these tests before publishing externally:

- Valid email reaches the confirmation-required state.
- Invalid email does not create a Buttondown record.
- Honeypot submission does not create a record.
- Confirmation produces a confirmed Buttondown subscriber.
- Subscriber record retains the expected source tag/metadata.
- Pageview and CTA event carry the matching traffic source and variant.
- Unconfirmed subscriber is excluded from the primary metric.
- Email and free text never reach Plausible.
- Nav, hero, offer, and final buttons all use exactly the same CTA label.
- Mobile layout, keyboard navigation, labels, focus states, and contrast work.

## 10. Decision rules

### Proceed to a thin MVP

Proceed only if one of these is true:

1. Two independent cold sources each meet their own continue threshold; or
2. One cold source meets its continue threshold and at least three subscribers produce qualified workflow replies, including two repeated-use cases and two credible positive price signals.

Warm performance may strengthen confidence but cannot satisfy either cold condition.

The MVP should test only save, name, and one-click restore for a single user.

### Interview before building

Do interviews first when:

- A cold source meets its conversion threshold but there are fewer than three qualified replies.
- Warm outreach is strong while every adequately sampled cold source is weak.
- Privacy, permissions, authentication, or stale URLs dominate replies.
- Conversion is strong but double-opt-in completion is poor.

This indicates curiosity or relationship-driven demand, not yet a sufficiently understood product.

### Retest the offer

Retest with headline B and unchanged channel targeting when:

- At least one cold source reaches its minimum and falls in its retest zone.
- CTA click rate is healthy but confirmed conversion is weak.
- Visitors consistently misunderstand “workspace restore.”

Run the retest as a new experiment with `lp_variant=b`; do not blend its results with variant A.

### Reshape the ICP or promise

Reshape when:

- An adequately sampled cold source is below its weak threshold.
- Replies reveal that the workflow belongs more to implementation, success, or MSP roles than frontline support.
- Users want shared playbooks or credential/session management rather than tab restoration.

### Park the idea

Park when:

- Two adequately sampled cold sources are below their weak thresholds; and
- Follow-up produces fewer than three qualified pain replies; and
- No credible price-positive signal appears.

A warm-only success cannot override this rule.

### Inconclusive

Return `inconclusive` when:

- No cold source reaches its sample minimum.
- Attribution fails.
- The confirmation flow is broken.
- Posts are removed or ads cannot reach the ICP.
- Most traffic is outside the intended geography.

That is a distribution or experiment failure, not proof that the problem is absent.

## 11. What not to build during the smoke test

Do not build:

- The Chrome extension.
- Account creation or authentication.
- Tab synchronization.
- Team sharing or admin controls.
- Credential, cookie, or session handling.
- AI tab classification.
- CRM/help-desk integrations.
- Billing or checkout.
- Import/export.
- A public roadmap.
- A multi-page marketing site.
- A fake interactive demo.
- Product screenshots representing nonexistent functionality.
- Testimonials, customer logos, user counters, or release countdowns.
