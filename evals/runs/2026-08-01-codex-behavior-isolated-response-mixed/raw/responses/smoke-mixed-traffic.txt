No—the 6% aggregate rate is encouraging, but it is not enough evidence to build the full product.

The problem is attribution:

- 200 visits and 12 confirmed emails = 6%.
- If all 12 came from friends, broad-ad conversion was 0%.
- If all 12 came from ads, broad-ad conversion was 6.7%.
- Friends are warm traffic; broad ads are cold and possibly poorly qualified. Pooling them hides the useful signal.

Treat this as permission to continue validating, not permission to build everything.

Next step:

1. Identify the source of each signup. If attribution was not captured, ask the 12 subscribers how they found you.
2. Contact all 12 and ask about their current workaround, urgency, and willingness to try or pay.
3. Run another test with at least 200 ICP-qualified cold visitors, preserving source attribution.
4. Pre-register a decision bar: for example, proceed to a thin MVP if cold traffic produces at least 5% confirmed-email conversion, 10 conversions, and meaningful downstream intent such as three qualified conversations or one paid commitment.
5. If signups remain silent, interview them before writing product code. If cold conversion falls below 1–2% across two offer variants, reshape the ICP or offer.

Do not build the full product yet. Build only the smallest manual, concierge, or prototype experience needed to test whether these people will use—and preferably pay for—the core outcome.

Evidence limit: I cannot inspect your analytics, UTM data, subscriber records, or ad targeting. This assessment assumes all 12 emails were valid, provider-confirmed signups and that the 200 visits were unique.
