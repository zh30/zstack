## Decision rules

| Result | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥4% cold confirmed CVR, ≥8 cold signups, and ≥3 concrete intent replies | Interview the strongest respondents, define a thin MVP, then consider building |
| 2–4% CVR or signups without substantive replies | Tighten ICP and promise; interview subscribers before building |
| <2% after 200 qualified cold visits and two headline variants | Reshape the offer or ICP; do not solve it by adding features |
| Attribution or provider confirmation fails | Fix measurement and rerun; no product conclusion is defensible |
| Credible payment request appears | Test a transparent paid pilot separately; one request is a lead, not proof of scale |
