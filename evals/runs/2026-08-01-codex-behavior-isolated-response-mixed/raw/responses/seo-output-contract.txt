## 1. Executive decision

The primary cluster should be **agency client onboarding workflow**.

The homepage should own the commercial/mixed-intent query:

> **Primary keyword:** `agency client onboarding workflow`

This is preferable to broad terms such as `client onboarding software` or `agency software`: the supplied notes say those SERPs are dominated by established CRM and client-portal vendors, while agency-workflow and ownership-specific results appear more mixed.

The product’s differentiating angle is not another checklist. It is:

> A client onboarding workflow for small agencies that assigns every next step to an owner and makes stalled work visible.

Confidence is **provisional/medium on relevance, low on measured demand and difficulty**. Live US SERP inspection and GSC data are required to validate the wording.

## 5. Homepage brief

### Search and conversion role

The homepage is the cluster’s commercial hub. It should explain the workflow, demonstrate how ownership prevents stalls, and move qualified agency visitors to the product CTA.

- **Suggested title:** `Client Onboarding Workflow for Small Agencies`
- **Suggested H1:** `Keep every client onboarding step owned and moving`
- **Suggested meta description:** `Give every onboarding task a clear owner, expose blocked handoffs, and run a repeatable client onboarding workflow for your agency.`
- **Primary CTA:** Use the real conversion action, such as `Start your workflow` or `Book a demo`
- **Secondary CTA:** `Get the agency onboarding template`

### Recommended page structure

1. **Hero**
   - Identify small agencies.
   - State the ownership problem and outcome.
   - Show one primary CTA.
   - Avoid vague language such as “streamline your business.”

2. **The failure mode**
   - A checklist records tasks but may not establish accountability.
   - Sales, the client, and delivery can each assume another party owns the next step.
   - Missing inputs and dependencies cause silent stalls.

3. **How the workflow works**
   - Create or reuse a workflow.
   - Assign each step to the agency, client, or named role.
   - Make dependencies and due status visible.
   - Prompt the current owner.
   - Hand off completed onboarding into delivery.

4. **Ownership-aware differentiator**
   - Answer “Who owns the next step?”
   - Show what is waiting, blocked, overdue, and ready.
   - Include an authentic product screenshot or short annotated demo.

5. **Agency onboarding process**
   - Cover the real sequence: signed client, intake, access, assets, kickoff, internal handoff.
   - Explain where ownership changes.
   - Link to the ownership guide when published.

6. **Automation**
   - Describe only real triggers, reminders, assignments, integrations, or status changes.
   - Link to the later automation guide.

7. **Template**
   - Preview the checklist/template resource.
   - Explain that the template includes owners, inputs, dependencies, and completion criteria.
   - Link to the expanded existing article.

8. **Proof and objections**
   - Add founder experience, customer evidence, or an honest worked example.
   - Do not fabricate testimonials, customer counts, or outcomes.
   - Address setup time, client participation, customization, and tool overlap.

9. **FAQ**
   - Use visible, substantive answers for questions such as:
     - What is a client onboarding workflow?
     - Who should own client onboarding?
     - How is a workflow different from a checklist?
     - Can clients own onboarding steps?
   - Add FAQ schema only if these answers appear visibly on the page and remain eligible under current search guidelines.

10. **Closing CTA**
    - Restate the agency-specific outcome.
    - Link to pricing and the primary conversion.

Internal links:

- Homepage → checklist/template, ownership guide, automation guide, pricing.
- Every guide → homepage with descriptive anchors such as “agency client onboarding workflow.”
- Checklist → ownership guide using a contextual link about unclear responsibility.
- Avoid repeating the exact same anchor mechanically.

## 6. Checklist/template page brief

Use the existing URL if it is relevant and indexable. If its slug must change, apply a single permanent redirect from the old URL.

- **Title:** `Client Onboarding Checklist for Agencies + Template`
- **H1:** `A client onboarding checklist your agency can actually finish`
- **Primary keyword:** `client onboarding checklist for agencies`
- **Secondary:** `client onboarding workflow template`
- **Meta angle:** A practical template that specifies the owner, required input, dependency, due point, and completion condition for every step.
- **CTA:** Copy/download the template, then show how the product turns it into an owned workflow.

Required content:

- Checklist grouped by sales handoff, intake, access, assets, kickoff, and delivery handoff.
- Columns for task, owner, dependency, due point, status, and “done” definition.
- Explanation of why a task-only checklist stalls.
- One worked agency example.
- Clear distinction between a static template and the product’s workflow.
- Links back to the homepage and, when available, the ownership guide.

Do not publish a second “workflow template” page during the first 90 days.

## 8. Measurement plan

### Setup in the first week

- Create and verify Google Search Console.
- Submit the canonical sitemap.
- Install privacy-appropriate analytics.
- Record conversions for the real CTA: signup, demo request, waitlist submission, or template acquisition.
- Annotate publication and major rewrite dates.
- Create a simple query/page tracking sheet for the primary keyword and the first five long-tails.
- Record a manual US search baseline in a neutral session. Treat it as directional, not exact rank reporting.

### Weekly review: maximum 45 minutes

| Signal | View | Decision use |
|---|---|---|
| Index coverage | GSC page indexing | Diagnose discovery or technical problems |
| Non-brand impressions | GSC query and page reports | Establish whether the chosen language earns visibility |
| Relevant query mix | Queries containing agency, onboarding, workflow, checklist, owner, process, or automation | Decide which spoke deserves expansion |
| Clicks and CTR | By page/query, only after impressions exist | Improve titles and snippets without confusing ranking with conversion |
| CTA actions | Analytics by landing page | Determine whether traffic has commercial relevance |
| Template acquisition | Checklist page | Measure interest in the resource and route users toward the product |
| Assisted navigation | Guide → homepage/pricing/product CTA | Check whether informational content creates a product path |

### Decision rules

- **Continue:** Relevant non-brand impressions expand across consecutive review windows and visitors take product or template actions.
- **Refine:** The pages receive relevant impressions but weak clicks; test clearer titles and descriptions while preserving intent.
- **Narrow:** Checklist queries appear but workflow queries do not; emphasize the ownership-aware template wedge instead of publishing more generic workflow pages.
- **Consolidate:** Multiple URLs receive impressions for the same query family without distinct intent.
- **Reassess:** A correctly indexed primary page receives no relevant impressions after roughly eight weeks; inspect live SERPs and reconsider the exact primary phrasing before creating more content.
- **Do not infer product-market fit:** Impressions, clicks, or rankings alone do not prove willingness to pay or retention.

No traffic or ranking target is promised. The first 90 days are intended to establish indexation, query-language evidence, and early conversion signals.

## 9. Capacity model

Keep the workload within six hours per week:

- Three hours: one page or content improvement.
- One hour: technical checks and internal links.
- One hour: distribution and legitimate relationship outreach.
- Forty-five minutes: measurement.
- Fifteen minutes: update decisions and backlog.

Ship one meaningful page improvement every two weeks rather than several thin posts.

## 10. 30/60/90 backlog

### Days 1–30: establish the cluster

**Desired outcome:** A technically indexable commercial hub and one useful supporting asset.

1. Verify product facts, ICP wording, and primary CTA.
2. Set up GSC, analytics, sitemap submission, and conversion events.
3. Check indexability, canonicals, redirects, rendering, and internal links.
4. Rewrite the homepage around `agency client onboarding workflow`.
5. Expand the existing checklist article into the checklist/template resource.
6. Add contextual links among homepage, checklist, and pricing.
7. Publish or improve About, privacy, terms, and contact pages.
8. Record the baseline and publication annotations.
9. Share the template through existing founder/customer channels without spam; collect the exact objections and phrases people use.

Do not add more articles until the homepage and checklist page are complete.

### Days 31–60: establish ownership authority

**Desired outcome:** Demonstrate the differentiator and learn whether ownership language earns search visibility.

1. Review GSC page and query data, acknowledging that early data may be sparse.
2. Publish the ownership guide targeting `who owns each step in client onboarding`.
3. Cover responsibilities, client-versus-agency ownership, dependencies, escalation, and sales-to-delivery handoff.
4. Add a genuine screenshot, workflow example, or founder-created process diagram.
5. Strengthen homepage proof with real feedback or a clearly labelled example.
6. Update titles or descriptions only where impression data shows a mismatch.
7. Conduct limited white-hat outreach to relevant agency operators, newsletters, communities, or partners where the template genuinely helps.
8. Track questions and CTA actions from each landing page.

Do not create a separate roles page, responsibilities page, and handoff page; the ownership guide owns that family initially.

### Days 61–90: expand only from evidence

**Desired outcome:** Double down on a demonstrated query branch or revise the cluster before increasing production.

1. Compare the homepage, checklist, and ownership guide by relevant impressions, clicks, and CTA actions.
2. Choose one evidence-led action:
   - If automation queries appear, publish the agency onboarding automation guide.
   - If checklist/template queries dominate, improve the template, example, and product bridge.
   - If ownership queries appear, deepen the ownership guide with FAQs and a worked handoff.
   - If none appear, inspect live US SERPs and revise the keyword/page positioning before publishing another article.
3. Merge or redirect any page showing overlapping intent.
4. Refresh homepage FAQs and copy using genuine GSC or customer language.
5. Seek a small number of editorial mentions through useful templates, partnerships, or founder expertise—not purchased links.
6. Document the next 90-day decision: continue the cluster, narrow to the template/ownership wedge, or pause SEO in favor of another acquisition channel.
