## Smoke protocol

Assumptions:

- The product is an unbuilt software tool.
- The target customer, problem, and promised outcome are still TBD.
- The CTA is a free early-access waitlist.
- Run the test from August 3–16, 2026.

Hypothesis: If `[Product]` offers `[specific outcome]` to `[narrow ICP]`, qualified visitors will join an early-access waitlist and describe a credible use case.

Primary metric: provider-confirmed waitlist signups ÷ unique qualified visitors.

Secondary signals:

- CTA click rate
- Answers to “What are you using today?”
- Welcome-email replies with a real use case
- Requests for pricing, calls, or paid access
- Conversion by traffic source

Pre-register two separate traffic lanes:

- Warm outreach: target 50 qualified visitors and at least 5 confirmed signups.
- Cold/community traffic: target 200 qualified visitors and at least 6 confirmed signups.

Do not combine their conversion rates.

Decision thresholds:

- Continue: at least 5% cold conversion, or at least 10% warm conversion, plus three credible use-case replies.
- Interview before building: conversion reaches the threshold but replies reveal weak urgency.
- Rewrite and retest: 200+ cold qualified visits produce 2–5% conversion.
- Reshape or park: after testing two hero variants, 200+ cold qualified visits produce less than 2% conversion and no meaningful replies.
- Any genuine prepayment or credible “invoice me” request is a strong lead, not proof of scale.

## Implementation pack

Use a single static page hosted on Cloudflare Pages or Vercel, with a provider such as Formspree or Basin. No application backend is needed for this test.

Capture:

- Required email
- Optional current-workaround answer
- Hidden `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`
- A derived traffic lane: `warm`, `community`, `cold_ad`, or `unattributed`

Use Plausible or Umami for:

- `page_view`
- `click_waitlist`
- `attempt_waitlist` if the browser cannot verify acceptance

Count a conversion only from the form provider’s confirmed subscriber record. Test one valid and one invalid submission before sending traffic. If the static integration cannot provide a verifiable success callback, keep confirmed-success messaging on the provider page and show only:

> Submission sent—check your inbox if the provider accepts it.

Do not turn an unsigned URL parameter or submit-handler event into “You’re on the list.”

Use distinct provider forms or provider-supported metadata for each traffic lane. Confirm through a real submission that attribution reaches the subscriber record. Unknown sources remain `unattributed` and are excluded from source-specific rates.

Recommended UTM pattern:

- `?utm_source=personal_dm&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=founding_waitlist`
- `?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=founding_waitlist`
- `?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=founding_waitlist`

Store email with the chosen form provider, enable double opt-in where required or where list quality matters, and publish a short privacy notice. Diagnostic logs must not include emails, form answers, secrets, or raw provider responses.

## 14-day traffic plan

Days 1–2:

- Replace all placeholders using language from five real conversations.
- Publish the page and test analytics, valid submission, invalid submission, welcome email, and UTM retention.
- Do not send traffic until the funnel is verifiably working.

Days 3–5:

- Send 20 personal messages to people who clearly match the ICP.
- Ask about their current process before sharing the link.
- Use the warm-outreach UTM; do not call friends and family qualified traffic unless they match the ICP.

Days 6–8:

- Publish one value-first post in a relevant buyer community.
- Describe the problem, current workaround, and what you are testing.
- Disclose that you are the founder and that the product is not built.
- Follow community rules; do not blast identical posts across groups.

Days 9–11:

- Publish three short X posts: a problem observation, what you learned from replies, and the honest waitlist invitation.
- Engage with responses rather than repeatedly reposting the link.

Days 12–13:

- If qualified traffic remains below 200, run one tightly targeted $20–$50 ad experiment.
- Use one audience, one creative, and its own UTM.
- Stop after 48 hours if clicks are irrelevant; do not optimize around cheap, unqualified traffic.

Day 14:

- Export provider-confirmed signups and source metadata.
- Separate warm, community, advertising, and unattributed results.
- Read every non-sensitive qualifier answer and email reply.
- Apply the pre-registered decision rules.

Because web, browser, app access, and customer research were unavailable in this run, no demand, competitor, signup, or customer evidence was verified. The copy must remain a hypothesis until real traffic and confirmed behavior exist.
