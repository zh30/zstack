# 10-Day Smoke Test: ClientTabs

This package tests demand for an unbuilt Chrome extension that restores client-specific tab workspaces for hands-on support agents at small US SaaS companies.

No files were created because the workspace is read-only and the request explicitly prohibits modifications. Web access was unavailable, so channel availability, current ad inventory, and Buttondown’s current API fields must be verified before launch. All thresholds below are pre-registered assumptions, not observed benchmarks or results.

## 1. Smoke protocol

### Offer

- Working name: **ClientTabs**
- ICP: Hands-on support agents and support leads at small US SaaS teams
- Pain: Reopening and arranging the same client-specific tabs whenever a ticket, call, or escalation resumes
- Promise: **Restore every client tab in one click**
- Stage: Unbuilt concept undergoing demand validation
- CTA everywhere: **Join the early-access waitlist**
- Price signal: **Planned pricing from $9/month**
- Test period: August 3–12, 2026
- Paid-media cap: $150

### Hypothesis

If hands-on support agents at small US SaaS teams are offered one-click restoration of client-specific browser workspaces, then sufficiently qualified visitors will join an early-access waitlist, describe an existing context-rebuilding workflow, and indicate credible interest at a planned price from $9/month.

### Metrics

Primary metric:

- Confirmed waitlist conversion rate = Buttondown double-opt-in-confirmed subscribers ÷ unique qualified landing-page visitors, calculated independently for each traffic source.

Secondary metrics:

- CTA clicks ÷ unique qualified visitors
- Accepted-but-unconfirmed subscriptions
- Welcome email delivery and opens, treated only as diagnostics
- Replies describing a current client-context workflow
- Explicit price-positive replies concerning $9/month
- Cost per confirmed subscriber for paid traffic
- Unattributed visitors and subscribers
- Invalid, rejected, duplicate, and bot submissions

A qualified visit is a non-bot US landing-page visit from an allow-listed UTM combination. Do not exclude genuine bounces based on session duration.

### Source lanes and thresholds

Never combine these lanes into a headline conversion rate or use warm results to rescue cold results.

**Warm personalized outreach**

- Target: 30 qualified visitors
- Continue signal: at least 5 confirmed subscribers, 3 concrete workflow replies, and 2 price-positive replies within this lane
- Ambiguous: 2–4 confirmed subscribers or signups without concrete replies
- Weak: 0–1 confirmed subscribers after 30 visits and one personal follow-up
- Interpretation: Passing this lane warrants interviews; it does not independently justify building

**Reddit organic community traffic**

- Minimum decision sample: 75 qualified visitors
- Continue signal: at least 4 confirmed subscribers, 3 concrete workflow replies, and 2 price-positive replies within this lane
- Retest signal: 2–3 confirmed subscribers or strong replies despite weaker conversion
- Weak: 0–1 confirmed subscribers after 75 qualified visits
- Do not evaluate the lane if a post is removed, attracts mainly founders rather than support staff, or violates community rules

**LinkedIn organic traffic**

- Minimum decision sample: 50 qualified visitors
- Continue signal: at least 4 confirmed subscribers, 3 concrete workflow replies, and 2 price-positive replies within this lane
- Retest signal: 2–3 confirmed subscribers
- Weak: 0–1 confirmed subscribers after 50 qualified visits
- Separate the founder’s general audience from personally contacted warm prospects

**Reddit paid traffic**

- Minimum decision sample: 100 qualified visitors
- Continue signal: at least 5 confirmed subscribers, at least 2 concrete workflow replies, and cost per confirmed subscriber no higher than $30
- Retest signal: 3–4 confirmed subscribers or cost per confirmed subscriber between $30 and $50
- Weak: 0–2 confirmed subscribers after 100 qualified visits
- If $150 cannot buy 100 qualified visits, classify the test as distribution-inconclusive rather than product-negative

Rates below 50 visitors remain unstable. Conversion counts and replies matter alongside percentages.

## 4. Implementation package

### Architecture

- Hosting: Cloudflare Pages
- Form handler: `/api/waitlist` as a Cloudflare Pages Function
- Subscriber system: Buttondown
- Analytics: Plausible
- Secret: `BUTTONDOWN_API_KEY`, stored only as a Cloudflare environment secret
- Optional non-secret settings:
  - `PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN`
  - `BUTTONDOWN_LIST_ID`
  - `SITE_ORIGIN`
- Deployment branches:
  - Production from `main`
  - Preview deployments excluded from Plausible and search indexing

Because no files may be created here, the implementation should consist of:

- `index.html`
- `functions/api/waitlist.js`
- `public/og-clienttabs.png`
- `_headers`
- `robots.txt`
- `privacy.html`, or an inline privacy disclosure if keeping a strict single-page scope

### Form fields

Visible:

- `email`, required, `type="email"`, maximum 254 characters

Hidden, populated from an allow-list:

- `source_lane`
- `utm_source`
- `utm_medium`
- `utm_campaign`
- `utm_content`
- `landing_variant`
- non-PII request ID

Do not collect names or free-text pain answers on the landing page. Ask for workflow detail by email after confirmation; this keeps the first conversion low-friction.

### Allow-listed attribution map

Only these exact combinations can enter a decision lane:

- `founder_email / warm_outreach / clienttabs_smoke_202608` → `warm_outreach`
- `reddit / community / clienttabs_smoke_202608` → `reddit_organic`
- `linkedin / organic_social / clienttabs_smoke_202608` → `linkedin_organic`
- `reddit / paid_social / clienttabs_smoke_202608` → `reddit_paid`

Anything missing or different becomes `unattributed`. Never default it to a known lane.

The Pages Function should recompute the lane from the UTM values. It must not trust a browser-supplied `source_lane`.

### Form response behavior

1. Validate email and allow-listed attribution server-side.
2. Submit the email to Buttondown with double opt-in enabled.
3. Attach the source lane, exact UTMs, campaign, and variant to supported Buttondown metadata or tags.
4. Only after Buttondown returns an authenticated successful response should the page show:

> Check your inbox to confirm your place on the early-access waitlist.

For an accepted but not yet confirmed record, never display “You’re on the list.”

On rejection, show:

> We couldn’t send your request. Check the email address and try again.

The submit handler must not fire a confirmed-conversion event. Buttondown’s confirmed subscriber record is the conversion source of truth.

Before launch, verify Buttondown’s current endpoint, double-opt-in behavior, tag or metadata fields, and webhook support against its live documentation. If attribution cannot survive onto a confirmed subscriber record, stop launch and use distinct Buttondown forms or tags for every lane.

### Privacy and security

- Do not send email addresses to Plausible.
- Do not place the Buttondown API key in client-side JavaScript.
- Do not log email addresses, authorization headers, free text, or raw Buttondown responses.
- Permitted diagnostic fields: HTTP status, bounded error category, source lane, and non-PII request ID.
- Add a honeypot and reasonable rate limiting.
- Reject requests from origins other than the production site.
- Retain only the minimum waitlist data needed for the test.
- Export and reconcile subscribers after the test, then document deletion or continued consent handling.

### Plausible events

Automatic:

- `pageview`, retaining UTMs and pathname

Custom client event:

- `CTA Click`
  - Fire when any CTA scrolls to or focuses the form
  - Properties: `source_lane`, `campaign`, `variant`, `placement`
  - Placement values: `nav`, `hero`, `offer`, `final`

Custom client event:

- `Waitlist Attempt`
  - Fire after valid browser-side form validation and before the request
  - Diagnostic only
  - Never report it as a conversion

Custom server-confirmed event:

- `Waitlist Accepted`
  - Fire from the Pages Function only after Buttondown accepts the request
  - Keep distinct from double-opt-in confirmation
  - Properties: `source_lane`, `campaign`, `variant`
  - Do not include email or other PII

Primary conversion:

- `Waitlist Confirmed`
  - Source of truth: Buttondown’s double-opt-in-confirmed subscriber export or verified webhook
  - Must retain source lane and campaign
  - If no authenticated confirmation webhook exists, calculate this in the experiment spreadsheet rather than manufacturing a client-side Plausible event

Diagnostics:

- `Waitlist Error`
  - Properties limited to `error_category`, `http_status`, `source_lane`
  - Categories: `invalid_email`, `rate_limited`, `provider_rejected`, `provider_unavailable`, `unknown`

### Measurement sheet

Maintain one row per day and source lane with:

- Date

## 9. Decision rules

### Proceed to interviews and a thin MVP definition

Proceed only when at least one cold lane independently reaches its minimum sample and its complete continue threshold:

- Confirmed conversion count passes
- Concrete workflow replies pass
- Price-positive replies pass
- Paid lane also passes its cost ceiling

Warm outreach may reinforce the decision but cannot satisfy this rule.

The next artifact should be a thin PRD focused only on saving, naming, finding, and restoring client tab workspaces. Privacy and extension permissions need validation before implementation.

### Interview before building

Do this when a cold lane passes its conversion threshold but does not produce enough concrete replies or price-positive responses.

Interpretation: email interest exists, but urgency, workflow fit, or willingness to pay remains unproven.

### Rewrite and rerun one controlled variable

Do this when traffic quality and sample minimum are credible but conversion is in the retest range.

Change one item only:

- First change: headline A to B
- Keep source, targeting, price, and CTA constant
- Run a fresh source-specific cohort
- Do not combine the new cohort with the original

### Narrow the ICP

Do this when replies consistently describe the pain in only one segment, such as escalation specialists or support leads managing multiple enterprise accounts.

Use those observed words in a new page and rerun the same cold source. Do not generalize from a few warm conversations.

### Stop or park

Park the concept when at least two cold lanes independently reach their minimum qualified sample and both land in their weak ranges, with working attribution and form delivery.

Do not call it disproven if:

- Paid traffic fails to reach its minimum sample
- A community post is removed
- UTMs fail to persist
- Buttondown confirmations are not attributable
- Deliverability is broken
- Traffic is mostly outside the ICP

Those are experiment or distribution failures requiring repair, not evidence that the product problem is absent.

### Price interpretation

- A waitlist conversion does not validate $9/month.
- A reply saying “sounds useful” does not validate price.
- A reply explicitly accepting $9/month is price-positive but still not payment.
- An unsolicited request to buy, prepay, or receive an invoice is a strong lead, not proof of scalable demand.
- Payment willingness remains unproven until a later paid-intent test.
