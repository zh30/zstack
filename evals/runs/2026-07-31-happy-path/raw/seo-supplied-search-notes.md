## 1. Executive summary

**Primary keyword:** `client onboarding workflow for agencies`  
**Primary page:** `/`  
**Confidence:** Medium

The first 90 days should establish one narrow topic cluster around an **ownership-aware agency client-onboarding workflow**. Rewrite the homepage as the commercial hub, substantially improve the existing checklist article, and publish one genuinely useful workflow template. The positioning should emphasize assigning every step an owner, reusing the workflow, and linking existing email, Notion, Drive, and billing records—without presenting OnboardFlow as a client portal or file repository.

This is more realistic than pursuing the broad `client onboarding software` category, whose supplied SERP notes indicate dominance by large CRM and client-portal vendors. The first 90 days are an indexing, messaging, and evidence-gathering period—not a ranking guarantee.

## 2. Product and search context

### Searcher and job-to-be-done

| Persona | Job | Search mindset |
|---|---|---|
| Small-agency project manager | Start each client engagement consistently | “I need a workflow or checklist I can reuse.” |
| Agency operations lead | Prevent onboarding handoffs from stalling | “Who owns the next step, and why isn’t the checklist getting done?” |
| Founder or delivery lead | Reduce repeated manual copying | “Can I automate this without replacing all our current tools?” |

### Product truth to preserve

- The product provides an ownership-aware, reusable workflow.
- It links to existing email, Notion, Drive, and billing records.
- It is not a full client portal.
- It does not store client files.
- The strongest interview reported 45 minutes per onboarding, a failed Notion template, current Zapier spending, and willingness to pilot with a real client.
- The smoke test produced 27 signups from 300 qualified cold visits, a 9% signup rate. This validates message interest, not organic conversion or payment demand.
- Payment remains untested; SEO success should initially be tied to qualified pilot signups rather than revenue.

## 6. Homepage on-page brief

### Metadata

- **URL:** `/`
- **Title:** `Client Onboarding Workflow for Agencies | OnboardFlow`
- **H1:** `A Reusable Client Onboarding Workflow for Small Agencies`
- **Meta description:** `Give every onboarding step an owner, reuse the workflow for each client, and link email, Notion, Drive, and billing records—without another client portal.`
- **Primary CTA:** `Join the $29/month pilot`
- If access is application-based rather than immediate, use `Apply for the pilot` and say so consistently.

### Hero message

**Eyebrow:** `For small-agency project managers`  
**Supporting line:** `Stop copying the same onboarding tasks across tools. Give every step a clear owner and keep the records you already use connected to the workflow.`

Avoid unsupported words such as “sync,” “native integration,” “automatic import,” or “all-in-one” unless those capabilities exist.

### Recommended page structure

1. **Hero: outcome, audience, and pilot CTA**
2. **The recurring failure**
   - Tasks are copied for every client.
   - Steps lose clear ownership.
   - Templates become stale or go unfinished.
3. **How the workflow works**
   - Reuse the onboarding workflow.
   - Make the responsible owner visible.
   - Link each step to its existing source record.
4. **Keep your current tools**
   - Email, Notion, Drive, and billing records remain their sources of truth.
   - State plainly that OnboardFlow does not store client files.
5. **An example ownership-aware workflow**
   - Show a visible sample with step, owner, definition of done, and source-record link.
   - Do not imply every field is a product feature unless implemented.
6. **Who it is and is not for**
   - For small agencies coordinating repeated client starts.
   - Not a client portal, CRM replacement, or file-storage system.
7. **Evidence**
   - With permission, describe the single interview accurately: one PM reported spending 45 minutes per onboarding, abandoning a Notion template, and paying for Zapier.
   - Do not generalize this into an industry-wide claim.
8. **FAQ**
9. **Final pilot CTA and pricing link**

### Homepage FAQ

Use visible, substantive answers before adding FAQ schema:

- Who owns the next onboarding step?
- Can I reuse the same workflow for every client?
- Is OnboardFlow a client portal?
- Does OnboardFlow store client files?
- Which existing records can I link?
- Can OnboardFlow help automate client onboarding?
- What is included in the $29/month pilot?

### Internal links

- Link “client onboarding checklist” to the existing article.
- Link “workflow template” to the proposed template page once it exists.
- Link pilot price and eligibility to `/pricing`.
- Each supporting article should link back to `/` with descriptive, natural anchor text.
- Add links from `/pricing` back to the workflow explanation and checklist/template resources.

## 7. Supporting-page briefs

### `/blog/client-onboarding-checklist`

- **Title:** `Client Onboarding Checklist With Clear Owners`
- **H1:** `A Client Onboarding Checklist That Assigns Every Step`
- **Intent:** Provide a usable checklist, not a generic list of tips.
- **Must cover:** sales-to-delivery handoff, billing/setup, access and records, kickoff, owner assignment, definition of done, and where the source record lives.
- Include a visible ownership matrix or sample workflow.
- Explain why a checklist fails when steps are copied repeatedly or nobody owns the next action.
- Link to `/`, `/pricing`, and the future template page.
- CTA: `Reuse this workflow in the OnboardFlow pilot`.

Improve usefulness and completeness rather than targeting an arbitrary word count.

### Proposed template page

- **Proposed route:** `/templates/client-onboarding-workflow`
- **Title:** `Client Onboarding Workflow Template for Agencies`
- **H1:** `A Reusable Client Onboarding Workflow Template`
- Provide an actual HTML-visible, copyable sample—not a gated teaser.
- Show an ownership field and source-record link for every step.
- Explain how to adapt it without creating a new Notion copy for every client.
- CTA: reuse the template in the pilot.
- Link back to the checklist for process guidance.

### Proposed failure article

- **Proposed route:** `/blog/why-client-onboarding-checklists-fail`
- **Title:** `Why Client Onboarding Checklists Don’t Get Done`
- **H1:** `Why Your Client Onboarding Checklist Isn’t Getting Done`
- Organize around the evidence-backed problems: unclear ownership, repeated copying, stale templates, and disconnected records.
- End with a short repair framework and links to the checklist, template, and homepage.

## 9. Sequenced 30/60/90-day backlog

The backlog fits approximately six hours per week.

### Days 1–30: establish the hub and baseline

**Week 1**

- Set up Search Console and conversion analytics.
- Rewrite the homepage title, H1, hero, product-boundary copy, and CTA.
- Add links between `/`, `/pricing`, and the checklist article.

**Week 2**

- Rebuild the checklist article around ownership and a usable workflow example.
- Add a clear author/byline and an honest “last updated” date.
- Add its contextual CTA and homepage link.

**Week 3**

- Verify robots, sitemap, canonicals, status codes, metadata, and server-rendered/indexable content.
- Submit the homepage, pricing page, and checklist through Search Console inspection.
- Implement the core analytics events.

**Week 4**

- Align `/pricing` with the pilot offer and clarify that payment evidence is not yet established internally.
- Add About/contact, privacy, and terms coverage if missing.
- Record the first GSC and analytics baseline; do not draw conclusions from a small sample.

**Day-30 outcome:** the commercial hub and checklist are useful, internally linked, measurable, and eligible for indexing.

### Days 31–60: publish the differentiated resource layer

**Weeks 5–6**

- Create the ownership-aware workflow template page.
- Make the template visible and useful without requiring signup.
- Link it from the homepage and checklist.

**Week 7**

- Publish the article about why onboarding checklists do not get done.
- Use the supplied community wording naturally, not as repetitive exact-match text.
- Add a small number of thoughtful outreach messages to relevant agency-operator newsletters, communities, or resource curators. Ask for feedback or inclusion; do not buy links or mass-email.

**Week 8**

- Review GSC by query and landing page.
- Adjust titles or descriptions only where actual impressions show a relevance/CTR mismatch.
- Interview pilot users about the ownership workflow. Request explicit permission before publishing any quote or company detail.

**Day-60 outcome:** the site has one commercial hub and three differentiated supporting resources, with the first real query and conversion observations available if Google has begun showing them.

### Days 61–90: follow observed demand

**Weeks 9–10**

- Identify which of workflow, checklist, template, automation, or ownership language is earning impressions or qualified signups.
- Improve the winning page’s examples, answers, and internal links.
- Create a separate automation or agency-process guide only if GSC demonstrates distinct intent that the existing pages cannot satisfy.

**Weeks 11–12**

- Publish one real pilot case study if permission and outcomes exist.
- If no publishable result exists, improve the product walkthrough instead; do not manufacture proof.
- Continue small, relevant resource outreach around the checklist or template.

**Week 13**

- Review the full funnel: organic landing → CTA → qualified signup → pilot participation → payment when available.
- Assign every page a `keep`, `improve`, `merge`, or `defer` decision.
- Set the next 90-day backlog from observed queries and qualified conversions rather than generating another speculative keyword list.

## 10. Measurement plan

### Events

| Event | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `organic_landing_view` | Identify search landing pages |
| `pilot_cta_click` | Measure commercial intent |
| `pilot_signup_start` | Detect form friction |
| `pilot_signup_complete` | Primary SEO conversion |
| `pricing_view` | Secondary commercial signal |
| `template_copy_or_use` | Measure template usefulness |
| `checklist_to_product_click` | Measure resource-to-product movement |

Use the project’s existing naming conventions if analytics is already installed.

### Weekly dashboard

Track by landing page and non-brand query:

- Indexed/eligible page status
- GSC impressions and clicks
- Query and landing-page CTR
- Average-position trend as a directional diagnostic, not a promise
- Pilot CTA clicks
- Completed and qualified pilot signups
- Template usage
- Eventual pilot-to-payment conversion

Add lightweight qualification after signup—such as role, agency context, and onboarding frequency—without making the initial form unnecessarily heavy.

### Decision rules

- **Impressions but weak clicks:** revisit title, description, and intent match.
- **Clicks but few CTA actions:** clarify audience, value, product boundaries, and proof.
- **CTA actions but incomplete signups:** investigate form friction.
- **Qualified signups but no payment:** treat this as pricing/product validation, not an SEO failure.
- **No impressions:** first verify indexability and internal links; then reconsider the page/query fit.
- **Two pages appearing for the same query:** consolidate or differentiate before publishing more.

Do not use the smoke test’s 9% cold-traffic signup rate as an organic conversion forecast.
