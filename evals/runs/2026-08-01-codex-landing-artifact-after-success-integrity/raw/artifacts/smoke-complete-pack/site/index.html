FAQ

Straight answers, before you join.

Is the Chrome extension available now?

No. This is a demand test for an unbuilt product. We will only start with a thin version if enough qualified support agents show real interest.

When will it ship?

There is no firm ship date. If we proceed, early-access members will hear about research and testing first.

What will it cost?

Planned pricing starts at $9 per month. That is a pricing intention, not a charge or a locked offer; final pricing may change as we validate the scope.

What exactly does “one-click restore” mean?

The planned core job is to reopen a saved set of tabs for one client with one action. Sync, collaboration, and deeper integrations are not promised in this test.

What about client privacy?

The product’s data model has not been finalized. Privacy and least-data storage will be requirements before any extension is tested. For this waitlist, Buttondown stores your email and the optional use case you submit; we will not sell the list.

What happens after I join?

Buttondown will ask you to confirm your email. We may send a short research question or product update. You can unsubscribe at any time.
