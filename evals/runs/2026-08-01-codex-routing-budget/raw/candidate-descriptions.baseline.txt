z-customer-discovery	Run Mom Test–style customer discovery for independent developers: recruit and structure 5–15 real user interviews focused on past behavior (not future intent), debrief each talk for violations, and synthesize evidence on three gates — pain real/frequent, people already paying for bad solutions, and willingness to switch. Use for user interviews, customer discovery, The Mom Test, 用户访谈, or 客户发现.
z-landing-smoke	Build a lowest-cost demand smoke test for independent developers: a concise English landing page plus waitlist / email capture / pre-order CTA, a traffic plan (Product Hunt, Reddit, X, small ads, niche communities), and success metrics (conversion, opens, contact intent, prepaid). Use before writing lots of product code to test if anyone cares. Triggers: smoke test, landing page, waitlist, pre-order page, demand test, 落地页, or 等待列表.
z-market-validate	Market-validate a rough product idea for independent developers: scan social and community demand (X/Twitter, Reddit, HN, Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, optional CN platforms), collect pro and con signals, and produce a realistic report on commercial value, solo-founder fit, short-term return odds, and best entry wedges. Use when the user has an early idea, wants market research, demand validation, "is this worth building", or idea vetting.
z-seo-plan	SEO execution expert for independent developers: from a PRD, live URL, or project page code, research real search demand, pick primary and supporting keywords, map intent and page targets, and deliver an actionable SEO plan with on-page, technical, and content notes. Use when asked for SEO, keyword research, 关键词, 搜索优化, SEO 方案, or a ranking plan.
z-write-prd	Write a professional Product Requirements Document (PRD) from provided material — idea notes, market-validate reports, user interviews, competitor notes, or rough specs. Acts as a PRD author for independent developers: problem, goals, users, scope, requirements, UX flows, metrics, risks, and MVP cut. Use when asked for a PRD, product requirements, 需求文档, 写 PRD, or a product spec.
