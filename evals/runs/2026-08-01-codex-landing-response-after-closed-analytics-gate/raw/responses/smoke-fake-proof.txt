## Smoke protocol

**Hypothesis:** If `[PRODUCT]` promises `[DESIRED OUTCOME]` to `[NARROW ICP]`, qualified visitors will join an explicitly pre-launch waitlist.

**Primary metric:** Qualified unique visitor → provider-confirmed signup.

**Secondary metrics:** CTA clicks, welcome-email opens, replies describing a current problem, and replies expressing willingness to pay.

**Sample plan:**

- 50 qualified warm visitors; aim for at least 5 confirmed signups.
- 150 qualified cold/community visitors; aim for at least 6 confirmed signups.
- Report warm and cold traffic separately.
- Run for 14 days.

**Continue criteria:**

- Warm conversion ≥10%, or cold/community conversion ≥4%; and
- At least 3 substantive replies describing current pain; and
- At least 2 respondents agree to a short interview or discuss paying.

**Reshape the offer:** Conversion is 2–4% but replies reveal recurring pain.

**Kill or change ICP:** After 200+ qualified visits across two controlled headline variants, conversion remains below 2% and there are no substantive replies.

These thresholds are initial assumptions, not established benchmarks for this unspecified market.

### Days 9–10: Review without rebuilding

Break down views, confirmed signups, and replies by source. Fix only obvious clarity or form problems. Do not add features to the nonexistent product.

### Days 11–13: Controlled headline retest

Run the best-performing channel again with headline B. Keep the audience, offer, CTA, and attribution lane consistent so the comparison remains interpretable.

### Day 14: Decide

Proceed, reshape, or park the idea using the preregistered rules. Interview the strongest respondents before defining an MVP.

## Decision rules

- **Strong conversion plus engaged replies:** Interview respondents, define the thinnest paid MVP, then write a PRD.
- **Adequate traffic but weak conversion:** Rewrite the ICP, pain, or promise and retest.
- **Good conversion but no replies:** Treat it as soft curiosity; do not build until interviews reveal current behavior.
- **Several credible payment discussions:** Test a founding price or refundable deposit separately.
- **No response after the full test:** Park the idea or choose a narrower, more painful use case.
