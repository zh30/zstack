# Landing smoke test: ClientDeck

> Working name only; check trademark and domain availability before launch.

## Experiment brief

- **Timebox:** August 3–12, 2026
- **ICP:** Hands-on support agents and support leads at US SaaS companies with roughly 5–50 employees, where agents repeatedly open different tools and tabs for each client.
- **Promise:** Restore a saved client-specific tab workspace in one click.
- **Stage:** Unbuilt; validating demand before development.
- **CTA:** “Join the early-access waitlist”
- **Planned price:** From $9/month
- **Stack:** Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare Pages Functions, Buttondown, Plausible
- **Paid-media cap:** $150 total
- **Primary metric:** Provider-confirmed waitlist conversion rate, evaluated separately for each traffic source.
- **Evidence limitation:** Live web and provider documentation were unavailable. Current Buttondown and Plausible API details, community rules, ad inventory, pricing, and domain availability have not been verified. Explicit `VERIFY_*` items below block launch until tested.

## 1. Smoke protocol

### Hypothesis

If hands-on support agents at small US SaaS teams see a credible promise to restore every client’s working tabs in one click, qualified cold visitors will join an early-access waitlist at the source-specific rates below, and some subscribers will reply with a concrete recurring workflow or willingness to pay from $9/month.

### Funnel

```text
Qualified unique landing visitor
→ Waitlist CTA click
→ Submission attempt
→ Provider-confirmed subscriber
→ Welcome email
→ Reply describing a recurring workflow or willingness to pay
```

### Metrics

- **Qualified unique visitor:** A non-bot US visitor arriving through one registered campaign directed at the stated ICP.
- **CTA rate:** Unique visitors who click “Join the early-access waitlist” ÷ qualified unique visitors.
- **Confirmed waitlist CVR:** Buttondown-confirmed subscribers ÷ qualified unique visitors in the same source lane.
- **Contact-intent rate:** Subscribers who reply with a specific client-context workflow ÷ confirmed subscribers in that lane.
- **Price-intent signal:** A reply explicitly saying the person would pay, trial, expense, or ask their manager to buy at a starting price of $9/month.
- **Open rate:** Diagnostic only; never a demand decision because email privacy features make it noisy.
- **Cost per confirmed signup:** Ad spend ÷ confirmed paid-ad subscribers.
- **Unknown attribution:** Recorded as `unattributed`, reported separately, and excluded from source decisions.

Invalid addresses, duplicates, rejected requests, bot traffic, form attempts, and unconfirmed records are not conversions.

### Source-specific thresholds

Rates are directional test bars, not established market benchmarks.

| Source lane | Minimum sample | Continue signal | Weak signal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `warm_dm` | 20 qualified visitors | ≥4 confirmed signups (≥20%) | 0–1 signup (<10%) |
| `reddit_organic` | 40 | ≥4 (≥10%) | 0–1 (<5%) |
| `linkedin_organic` | 30 | ≥3 (≥10%) | 0–1 (<5%) |
| `x_organic` | 30 | ≥3 (≥10%) | 0–1 (<5%) |
| `reddit_paid` | 50 | ≥3 (≥6%) and CPL ≤$25 | 0 signups, or CPL >$50 |
| Any individual lane | ≥5 confirmed signups | ≥2 specific workflow replies and ≥1 price-intent reply | No substantive replies within 72 hours |

A lane below its minimum sample is **inconclusive**, not weak.

Warm outreach is never added to cold denominators, conversion rates, or sample requirements. Organic and paid cold sources also retain separate lane results.

### Problem section

**A client switch should not mean starting over**

- You hunt through history, bookmarks, and old tickets to find the same pages again.
- Similar-looking tabs make it easy to work in the wrong client account.
- Every interruption turns a two-minute support task into another round of setup.

### Solution section

**Your client context, ready when you need it**

ClientDeck is planned around one focused workflow:

1. **Save a workspace**  
   Group the browser tabs you use for a specific client.

2. **Name the client context**  
   Keep each client’s tickets, admin tools, docs, and dashboards together.

3. **Restore it in one click**  
   Reopen the saved workspace when that client needs attention.

This describes the intended product direction, not currently available functionality.

### For / not for

**Built for**

- Hands-on support agents who move between several client accounts every day
- Support leads at small SaaS teams without a dedicated internal-tooling team
- Workflows that repeatedly combine tickets, admin pages, docs, and dashboards

**Not built for**

- General browser-session backup across every personal device
- Large enterprise browser administration or employee monitoring
- Teams looking for a replacement for their help desk or CRM

**What will it cost?**

The current plan is to start at $9/month. Pricing may change as the intended product and team plans are defined. This page does not collect payment or lock a price.

**What would the extension access?**

The permission model has not been finalized. Before any early-access installation, the exact permissions, storage behavior, and data handling will be disclosed plainly. The product should request only what its workspace-restoration job requires.

**Will you see client tabs or customer information?**

That architecture has not been finalized, so the page should not claim that tab data is local-only or inaccessible. The intended design is to minimize collected data. Early testers must receive a concrete privacy and permissions explanation before installation.

After confirming your email, you will receive a brief welcome message asking what client context you currently rebuild. You may later receive a product-decision or early-access update. You can unsubscribe at any time.

**Is this a replacement for our support platform?**

No. The concept complements the support, admin, documentation, and monitoring tools your team already uses.

### Form and conversion behavior

Browser-to-function payload:

```json
{
  "email": "person@example.com",
  "workflow": "support ticket, admin, logs",
  "utm_source": "reddit",
  "utm_medium": "community",
  "utm_campaign": "restore_smoke_202608",
  "utm_content": "support_post_01",
  "variant": "a"
}
```

Requirements:

- Email required; maximum 254 characters.
- Optional workflow answer capped at 300 characters.
- Actual request body capped at 2 KB while reading the stream; never trust `Content-Length`.
- Accept `application/json` with valid parameters such as `charset`; reject lookalike media types.
- Reject malformed JSON, `null`, arrays, scalars, unknown fields, oversized bodies, and invalid emails before field access.
- Server independently maps the exact UTM tuple to a lane. A client-supplied `lane` is never trusted.
- Provider request has an explicit timeout, suggested starting point: five seconds.
- Hard-coded public error categories only: `invalid_media`, `body_too_large`, `invalid_json`, `invalid_shape`, `invalid_email`, `provider_timeout`, `provider_network`, `provider_rejected`, `rate_limited`, `service_unavailable`.
- Never return or log an exception message, stack, cause, authorization header, email, workflow answer, or raw provider response.
- Diagnostic log fields: non-PII request ID, bounded error category, HTTP status, canonical lane, and variant.
- Apply rate limiting and a honeypot. Do not count honeypot or rate-limited requests.

### Confirmation and attribution

The provider-confirmed subscriber record must retain the canonical source lane through a verified Buttondown tag or metadata facility. If that mapping cannot be verified, use separate provider-native forms/tags for each lane. If neither works, halt traffic; page-level Plausible UTMs plus one undifferentiated subscriber total cannot support the required source-specific decisions.

With double opt-in:

- The local form may emit `attempt_waitlist`.
- The local form initially displays only “Request sent. Check your inbox.”
- `submit_waitlist` is emitted only from a verified confirmation mechanism or a daily reconciliation of active/confirmed Buttondown records.
- A URL parameter, fragment, cookie, timeout, `localStorage`, or `sessionStorage` flag never proves confirmation.
- If no secure provider confirmation callback exists, use the provider record/export as the conversion source of truth and do not display confirmed-success copy locally.

### Plausible events

| Event | Trigger | Allowed properties |
|---|---|---|
| `view_landing` | One sanitized landing view | `decision_lane`, `placement`, `variant` |
| `click_waitlist` | CTA scrolls/focuses form | Same three properties |
| `attempt_waitlist` | Valid client form sent | Same three properties |
| `submit_waitlist` | Provider-confirmed subscriber only | Same three properties |
| `welcome_reply` | Manual daily aggregate or verified server event | `decision_lane`, `reply_type` |
| `price_intent` | Manual classification of a reply | `decision_lane`, `price_band` |

Canonical values only:

- `decision_lane`: registered lanes or `unattributed`
- `placement`: registered placements or `unattributed`
- `variant`: `a`
- `reply_type`: `specific_workflow` or `other`
- `price_band`: `from_9`

Before Plausible initializes:

1. Parse the landing query locally.
2. Match the exact tuple against the allow-list.
3. Discard raw and unknown values.
4. Remove the query and fragment from the analytics-visible URL with `history.replaceState`.
5. Load Plausible only if every `VERIFY_PLAUSIBLE_*` value has been replaced and verified.
6. Emit only canonical properties.

Never send Plausible raw UTMs, referrers, full URLs, fragments, email addresses, workflow text, or provider content. While verification is incomplete, events may enter a local no-op queue but must produce no network request.

### Preflight tests

Traffic remains blocked until all pass:

- Valid submission creates the expected Buttondown record.
- Invalid email creates no record and no conversion event.
- Double-opt-in confirmation changes the record to the expected confirmed state.
- Every registered source produces the correct provider tag/metadata.
- Unknown source stays `unattributed`.
- Warm and cold test records remain visibly separate.
- Oversized, malformed, `null`, array, scalar, stalled-provider, and provider-error requests return bounded responses.
- No response or log contains PII, secrets, raw provider content, or exception text.
- Plausible sees a clean page URL and canonical properties only.
- Unresolved Plausible placeholders cause zero analytics network requests.
- Mobile layout, keyboard navigation, focus state, contrast, and form labels work.
- Cloudflare preview and production behave identically.

## 8. Decision rules

### Proceed to a thin MVP

Proceed only if:

- At least two cold lanes independently meet their continue thresholds; and
- At least one qualifying lane produces two specific workflow replies; and
- At least one person expresses credible price intent at the planned $9/month starting point.

Warm results may support interpretation but cannot satisfy the cold requirement.

The thin MVP should test only saved named tab groups and one-click restore, plus the minimum permission/privacy design required to do that safely.

### Interview before building

Do this if a cold lane meets its conversion bar but subscribers give no specific replies or price intent. The page may be attracting curiosity without exposing a strong problem.

Success for the next step: five interviews containing at least three recent, concrete examples of repeated client-context reconstruction.

### Retest the offer

Retest headline B as a new, separately attributed experiment if cold traffic meets its minimum samples but produces middling conversion. Keep the audience, price, and CTA fixed.

Continue only if the new variant improves the same source’s confirmed CVR by at least 50% relative and produces at least two additional confirmed signups. With tiny samples, treat the change as directional.

### Reshape or park

Reshape the ICP or promise if:

- `reddit_organic` and `reddit_paid` both reach their minimum samples and remain weak; and
- At least one of `linkedin_organic` or `x_organic` also reaches its minimum and remains weak; and
- Fewer than two specific workflow replies appear across the qualifying cold lanes.

Do not conclude that the entire problem is dead if traffic samples were not reached or attribution failed. That is a distribution or measurement failure.

### Paid-ad stop rules

Stop paid traffic immediately when:

- Spend reaches $150;
- Attribution or provider confirmation breaks;
- The creative or landing page becomes misleading;
- After at least 30 qualified paid visitors, there are zero confirmed signups;
- CPC exceeds $5 and there is no downstream activity after 48 hours; or
- The audience cannot be restricted closely enough to the ICP.
