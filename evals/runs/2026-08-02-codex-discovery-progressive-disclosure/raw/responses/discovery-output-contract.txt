## Method and evidence limits

- Five interview summaries are the full evidence base: four cold and one warm.
- The warm participant is not identified, so cold-versus-warm results cannot be compared.
- These are thin summaries, not transcripts. No verbatim quotes, dates, funnel context, or interviewer questions were provided.
- Leading questions and pitch leakage therefore cannot be reliably scored. Compliments, feature requests, and future intentions receive limited weight.
- This sample supports pattern detection, not market sizing or broad ICP claims.

## Decision

**Verdict: Advance — but only to a narrow, instrumented pilot, not a broad build.**

Advance the wedge for operators in client-service businesses who have recurring multi-tool onboarding work and already pay through labor or automation. I01 is the strongest design partner because the participant supplied measurable time cost, existing software spend, a failed alternative, and requested a pilot.

Treat these as exclusions or non-goals for now:

- Generic founder onboarding without recent pain or spend.
- Broad automation-platform replacement.
- Building CSV functionality before clarifying import/export and the underlying job.
- Bookkeeper production use involving sensitive files before security expectations are understood.
- Full-service migration before the migration barrier has been tested.

## Concrete downstream handoff

Run one concierge pilot with I01, then seek two additional cold pilot candidates matching the bleeding-and-paying segment.

Pilot scope:

- Use one real onboarding workflow.
- Record the current baseline: 45-minute copying time, tools touched, handoffs, and responsible owner.
- Preserve explicit ownership throughout the workflow.
- Avoid unsupported broad integrations; manually support the narrow workflow where necessary.
- Measure completion time, correction work, failures, and migration/setup time.

Decision threshold after three qualified pilots:

- Continue toward a thin PRD if at least two participants complete a real onboarding, reduce manual work by at least 20 minutes per run, identify a responsible workflow owner, and make a concrete continuation commitment such as payment, deposit, or authorized paid trial.
- Reshape if time savings occur but migration, security, or ownership prevents continued use.
- Park if fewer than two participants expose a real workflow, complete a run, or make a continuation commitment.

Copy inputs for a landing smoke test must remain paraphrased themes—not invented customer quotes:

- Repeated cross-tool copying
- Forty-five-minute onboarding overhead
- Unclear workflow ownership
- Weekly operational failures
- Migration and sensitive-file risk
