## 1. Method and evidence limits

The five supplied summaries are the full primary evidence. No transcripts, dates, verbatim quotes, or normalized interview notes were provided.

Consequently:

- This supports pattern detection, not market sizing.
- Each role appears only once, so role-level conclusions are fragile.
- The warm interview cannot be identified, preventing source-bias analysis by interview.
- Interviewer questions are unavailable, so leading-question and future-tense audits are limited.
- Pilot interest is not payment, completed switching, adoption, or retention.
- No exact customer language should be presented as quotation or used as proven landing-page copy.

## 8. Decision

**Verdict: Advance—only into a narrow, instrumented pilot and smoke test, not a broad product build.**

Advance around this hypothesis:

> Teams with recurring client onboarding, an identifiable process owner, and existing paid manual/tooling cost will trial a lightweight workflow layer if it avoids forced migration, assigns ownership clearly, handles sensitive-file boundaries, and supports CSV portability.

The initial wedge should be the agency PM’s repeated cross-tool workflow because it has the densest combined evidence: quantified time, existing software spend, a failed alternative, a specific failure cause, and a direct pilot request.

Do not initially target founders with no recent incident or spend. Do not assume the marketer’s free-tool behavior belongs in the same paid segment.

## 9. Concrete downstream handoff

### Immediate validation: agency PM concierge pilot

Owner: founder.

Within seven days, ask the agency PM to schedule a scoped pilot around the exact recurring workflow already described. Before configuration, document:

- Current trigger, tools, steps, and the measured 45-minute baseline.
- Owner for every onboarding handoff.
- What data or files may not enter the system.
- Whether CSV import or export is required.
- Migration boundaries: begin with one new onboarding flow, not a full historical migration.
- Commercial commitment: paid pilot, deposit, or an explicit post-pilot purchasing decision with the budget owner.

Run the pilot on three real onboarding instances if that volume occurs during the agreed test window; otherwise define the number from their actual near-term pipeline rather than fabricating volume.

Advance to a thin MVP only if the pilot:

- Is scheduled and receives real workflow/data access.
- Completes multiple live instances.
- Reduces the stated 45-minute task to 15 minutes or less.
- Produces no unresolved ownership failure.
- Satisfies the agreed CSV and sensitive-data requirements.
- Ends with payment or a concrete purchasing step from an identified budget owner.

Reshape or park the wedge if the PM will not schedule/provide access, the workflow cannot be repeated, migration or security blocks use, or the measured time/cost improvement is immaterial.

### Next interview round

Recruit five additional cold participants matching all three conditions: recurring onboarding, a named workflow owner, and current monetary or substantial labor cost. Ask about the last onboarding incident, frequency, current spend, prior attempted replacements, ownership, migration, CSV, sensitive data, and purchasing authority.

The next round should determine whether the agency pattern repeats independently or is a single-client exception.

### Landing-smoke handoff

Test one CTA: **Apply for a workflow pilot**.

Copy concepts may paraphrase the supplied evidence—cross-tool copying, unclear ownership, migration avoidance, CSV portability—but must not be presented as customer quotes. No verbatim phrases were supplied.

### PRD boundary

In scope for a pilot:

- Explicit owner per step
- Cross-tool workflow orchestration
- Incremental/no-forced-migration setup
- CSV import/export
- Minimal and transparent handling of sensitive files
- Basic time and failure measurement

Non-goals until further evidence:

- Broad all-in-one CRM replacement
- Full historical migration
- Support for every professional-services segment
- Enterprise compliance claims
- Advanced customization unrelated to observed barriers
- Building for compliment-only founders or a price-sensitive self-serve segment
