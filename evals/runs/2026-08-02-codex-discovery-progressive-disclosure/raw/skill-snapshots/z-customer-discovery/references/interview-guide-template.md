# Interview guide template — z-customer-discovery

**Length:** 25–40 minutes  
**Rule:** Product pitch only in the last minutes (optional).

---

## 0. Open (2 min)

- Thanks + purpose: “Understanding how you handle {area} today.”  
- Permission to take notes.  
- “No right answers; please be blunt.”  

## 1. Context (5 min)

- Role, team size, tools of record  
- What a normal week looks like around {job}  

## 2. Last-time story (10–15 min) — G1

- “Tell me about the **last time** {problem trigger} happened.”  
- What led up to it? Who was involved?  
- What did you do next?  
- How often does this show up (week/month)?  
- What was the cost (time, money, stress, lost deal)?  

Probe: another example if first was mild.

## 3. Current solutions & spend (8–10 min) — G2

- How do you solve it today (tools, people, hacks)?  
- What do you pay (subscriptions, freelancers, your hours)?  
- What have you tried and abandoned? Why?  
- Where does the budget / decision live?  

## 4. Switching & constraints (5 min) — G3

- What would have to be true to change workflow?  
- What broke last time you tried a new tool?  
- If this stayed broken for 12 months, what happens?  

## 5. Optional idea flash (3 min max)

- One plain sentence, no slideware  
- “Does this map to anything you already do?”  
- Listen for skepticism — gold  

## 6. Close (2 min)

- “Who else should I talk to?”  
- Thanks; optional follow-up permission  

---

## Must-ask bank (pick per ICP)

- Last time / frequency / cost  
- Status quo tools  
- Failed alternatives  
- Budget owner  
- Emotional peak of the incident  

## Never-ask bank

- Would you use this?  
- How much would you pay? (as a first-line question)  
- Do you like my idea?  
- Anything that only gets a compliment
