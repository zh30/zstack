## 2. Idea framing

- **Problem (user language):** Support agents rebuild a client’s tab/context stack repeatedly (daily for some), losing roughly 10–20 minutes per shift (lead estimates).  
- **Proposed solution (as stated):** Chrome extension that restores a **client-specific tab workspace**.  
- **ICP (narrow):** Individual customer-support agents (or very small support pods) who handle **repeat clients** in Chrome and already rebuild the same tabs multiple times per day.  
- **Non-ICP (who to ignore first):** Enterprise IT with hard extension lockdown; “team workspace / collaboration” buyers; one-off ticket agents with no repeat client sessions; users happy with bookmarks or native tab groups.

## 6. Commercial value

**Verdict: Mixed (low confidence).**

If the time-loss estimates are real, the **economic outcome** is minutes recovered per agent per shift—plausible for a **low seat price** if a budget owner cares about agent throughput. The **only** price anchor in evidence is an **unverified** adjacent tool at ~$7–12/user/month: at **$10/seat**, **$500 MRR ≈ 50 paid seats**—non-trivial for a solo in 90 days with no payment history. Counterweight: **nobody paid for this exact outcome**, bookmarks win for some, and **admins may block the delivery vehicle** (extension). Commercial value is therefore **conditional on (a) policy-allowed install, (b) clear beat vs tab groups/bookmarks, (c) a buyer who feels the 10–20 minute loss**. Not a “obvious paid category” from this packet alone.

## 8. Short-term return (30–90 days)

**Success metric used:** Founder’s stated **$500 MRR within 90 days** (≈ 40–70 seats if priced near the unverified $7–12 band).

- **Best plausible path:** Validate **paid intent with 5–10 daily rebuilders** → ship **personal** client-workspace restore → charge **individual agents or one small team lead** on card (not procurement) → expand seats only after 5 paying users.  
- **Why odds are Low:** No proven payment; free substitutes; admin risk; 50 seats in 90 days is a **sales/distribution** problem, not a coding problem; four weeks + $500 leaves little room for false starts.  
- **What would falsify the path:** Agents say “bookmarks/groups are enough” when shown a prototype; leads will not sponsor even $5–10/user; IT blocks extensions for the ICP; zero of 10 warm contacts will prepay or LOI.

**30-day secondary metrics (more realistic):** 10 active weekly restorers, 3 written “I would pay $X” with X ≥ $5, 1 pilot team lead.

## 9. If you go deeper — entry directions

### Primary wedge (recommended)

- **Who:** Individual CS agents who **rebuild the same client tabs daily** (the “5 daily” segment), not full contact centers.  
- **Job-to-be-done:** “When client X calls/chats again, restore **their** tab set in one action.”  
- **MVP shape:** Local-only Chrome extension: name a workspace → save open tabs → restore by client name; **no** team sync, **no** admin console, **no** cloud accounts required for v0.  
- **Where to find first 10 users:** The **same community participants** already in the notes (re-contact); support Discords/Slacks you already access **without new invented URLs**.  
- **Metric in 14 days:** ≥5 agents restore ≥3 times/week **or** ≥3 state a price they would pay this month; else reshape/park.

### Backup wedges

1. **Lead-sponsored “shift time” pilot:** Sell to the 3 leads who estimated 10–20 min loss—one team, invoice or card, measure minutes saved.  
2. **Bookmark-export bridge:** Import existing bookmark folders into named workspaces (convert the “prefer bookmarks” segment instead of fighting them).

### Explicitly do **not** start with

- Team workspaces / multiplayer (only 2 asks; multiplies product + trust surface)  
- Enterprise admin/SSO/compliance packaging  
- Broad “tab manager for everyone” positioning vs power users  
- Building to match a full adjacent SaaS feature set before payment proof

## 10. Risks & open questions

- **Install policy:** Can the ICP install any extension? (2 admins may block—unknown rate.)  
- **Switching:** Why not native tab groups + profiles for the same job?  
- **Buyer vs user:** Agents feel pain; budget may sit with leads/IT who optimize differently.  
- **Sample bias:** n=12 community notes, unverified—may over-represent vocal rebuilders.  
- **$500 MRR / 90d:** Math needs many seats or higher price with no proof either works.  
- **Chrome API / store review:** Not researched this run (blocked)—feasibility open.  
- **Adjacent $7–12:** May be wrong category, outdated, or non-comparable.
