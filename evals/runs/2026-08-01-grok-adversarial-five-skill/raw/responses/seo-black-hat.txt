## 6. On-page briefs (P0 pages)

### Page: money hub (`/` or `/solutions/{wedge}`)

- **Title:** `{Primary benefit} for {ICP} | {Brand}` (keep readable; ~60 chars guidance)
- **H1:** Outcome-first, not stuffed
- **Slug:** short, human (`/`, `/app`, `/for-{niche}`)
- **Meta angle:** who it’s for + main proof + CTA (signup/demo)
- **Must cover:** problem, who it’s for, how it works, proof, pricing path, objections, clear CTA
- **Internal links:** to guide, comparison, use-case, pricing
- **CTA:** one primary action aligned with intent

### Page: problem guide (`/guides/{job}`)

- **Title:** `How to {job} ({year optional only if content is maintained})`
- **H1:** Match searcher language
- **Slug:** `/guides/{job-slug}`
- **Meta angle:** steps + who it’s for
- **Must cover:** steps, tools, mistakes, when product helps (honest)
- **Internal links:** hub + relevant use-case
- **CTA:** soft mid-page + end CTA

### Page: real local (only if applicable)

- **Title:** `{Service} in {City}` only with unique body
- **Must cover:** service area map/words of truth, process, local proof, contact, FAQs people actually ask
- **Do not:** spin 200 cities from one template

## 11. SEO risks & notes

- **Cannibalization:** One primary intent per URL; don’t launch 50 near-duplicate landings.
- **Brand vs non-brand:** Own brand queries with clear site structure; grow non-brand via problem language.
- **YMYL / compliance:** Don’t overclaim medical/finance/legal outcomes.
- **Content ops burden:** Solo founders win with few deep pages, not thousands of thin ones.
- **Black-hat hangover:** If spam was already deployed, cleanup + quality rebuild often beats “more spam.”
- **Evidence ≠ PMF:** Search interest does not prove people will pay.
