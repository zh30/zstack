## The three gates (what real interviews must answer)

| Gate | Question | Strong evidence | Weak / fake |
|------|----------|-----------------|-------------|
| **G1 Pain** | Real & frequent? | Recent stories, triggers, workarounds, time/money/emotion cost | Hypotheticals, “sounds annoying” |
| **G2 Spend** | Already paying cost for bad solutions? | Tools, freelancers, hours in sheets, risk taken | “I’d pay if it existed” |
| **G3 Switch** | Switch credible? | Past switches, active search, budget owner, switching cost, cost of doing nothing | Polite enthusiasm after your pitch |

Without G1–G3 from **primary** conversations, **do not green-light a large build.**

## Interview guide (25–40 min, Mom Test)

**Open (no pitch):** “Walk me through the last time [trigger].”

**Must-ask (past behavior):**

1. What happened, step by step?  
2. How often does that show up (last month / quarter)?  
3. What did you try? Spreadsheets, tools, people, ignore it?  
4. What did that cost (money, hours, stress, missed outcomes)?  
5. Have you switched tools or vendors for this before? Why / why not?  
6. If nothing changes for 12 months, what happens?

**Avoid:** “Would you use…?”, “Would you pay $X?”, pitching early, leading “Isn’t X painful?”

**Optional last 60s:** one-sentence concept only *after* stories; note reaction as **weak** unless they describe budget, timeline, or intro to a buyer.

**After each call:** debrief with story timeline, costs, switch signals, gold quotes, Mom Test audit, G1/G2/G3 ∈ {support, neutral, contradict}.
