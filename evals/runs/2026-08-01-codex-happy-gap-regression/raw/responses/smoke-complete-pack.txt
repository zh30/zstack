## 1. Smoke protocol

### Hypothesis

If hands-on support agents at small US SaaS teams see an honest offer for a planned Chrome extension that restores each client’s working tabs in one click, then qualified cold visitors will join an early-access waitlist at the source-specific rates below, and some subscribers will reply with a concrete workflow and accept planned pricing from $9/month.

### Funnel

```text
Attributed landing view
→ CTA click
→ Waitlist attempt
→ Buttondown accepts address
→ Subscriber confirms double opt-in
→ Welcome email open
→ Reply with use case and price reaction
```

A waitlist conversion is counted only when Buttondown records an active, confirmed subscriber. Attempts, rejected addresses, duplicates, bots, and unconfirmed subscribers do not count.

### Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Qualified visitor | Unique US visitor attributed to one test source, excluding internal traffic, bots, obvious recruitment traffic, and accidental visits |
| CTA click rate | `click_cta ÷ qualified visitors` |
| Confirmed waitlist CVR | Buttondown-confirmed subscribers attributable to the source ÷ qualified visitors from that same source |
| Cost per confirmed subscriber | Ad spend ÷ confirmed paid-source subscribers |
| Substantive reply | Reply naming a recurring client-tab workflow, current workaround, or explicit price reaction |
| Price-positive reply | Credible statement that $9/month is acceptable or that the sender would pay for the proposed result |
| Welcome open rate | Diagnostic only; never a standalone build/kill signal |

Do not calculate a combined warm-and-cold conversion rate. Warm outreach receives a separate URL, source tag, dashboard row, and decision path.

### Target samples and thresholds

| Source | Traffic class | Minimum before judgment | Strong signal | Weak signal |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Reddit organic | Cold community | 60 qualified visits | ≥5% CVR and ≥3 confirmed | <2% after 100 visits |
| Permissioned Slack/Discord/community | Cold niche | 40 qualified visits | ≥7.5% CVR and ≥3 confirmed | <2.5% after 60 visits |
| X posts | Cold/mixed social | 50 qualified visits | ≥4% CVR and ≥3 confirmed | <2% after 75 visits |
| Paid campaign | Cold paid | 60 qualified visits | ≥3% CVR, ≥3 confirmed, CPL ≤$30 | <1.5% after 100 visits, or zero confirmed after ≥30 visits and $60 spent |
| Personal outreach | Warm DM | 20 qualified visits | ≥15% CVR, ≥3 confirmed, and ≥2 substantive replies | <5% and zero substantive replies after 20 visits |

Rates below 50 visits are unstable unless accompanied by unusually strong downstream behavior. Warm performance may justify interviews, but cannot compensate for failed cold acquisition.

## 4. Implementation pack

### Minimal architecture

```text

├── Static one-page landing
├── /api/waitlist Pages Function
│   └── Buttondown API
└── Plausible client events

Buttondown
├── Double opt-in
├── Welcome email
└── Confirmed-subscriber export with source attribution
```

Suggested files, described only because file modification was prohibited:

```text
/
├── index.html
├── styles.css
├── app.js
├── functions/api/waitlist.ts
├── public/og-clienttabs.png
├── public/privacy.html
└── wrangler.toml
```

Do not scaffold the Chrome extension, database, authentication, billing, or product backend.

### Cloudflare configuration

Environment variables:

- `BUTTONDOWN_API_KEY` — encrypted Cloudflare secret
- `BUTTONDOWN_ENDPOINT` — current provider endpoint, verified against Buttondown documentation
- `PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN` — public domain identifier
- `ALLOWED_ORIGIN` — exact production landing origin

Pages Function behavior:

1. Accept `POST` only.
2. Enforce same-origin requests.
3. Validate normalized email and required consent.
4. Reject honeypot hits, oversized bodies, and unknown `source_class` values.
5. Apply basic Cloudflare rate limiting.
6. Map UTMs to an explicit source class; never trust a client-provided arbitrary class.
7. Send the subscriber to Buttondown with double opt-in enabled.
8. Preserve `source_class`, UTMs, and `landing_variant` in a provider-supported custom field, metadata field, tag, or distinct form.
9. Return “confirmation pending” only after Buttondown returns a verified success response.
10. Return no API key, provider error body, or subscriber data to the browser.

Buttondown’s current metadata/tag field names could not be verified here. Before launch, confirm that these values appear in the provider’s confirmed-subscriber export after a real double-opt-in. If they do not, use distinct provider-native forms/tags for each source. Do not begin traffic until numerator attribution survives this test.

### Plausible events

Use consistent names and attach the allowlisted source properties.

| Event | Trigger | Conversion status |
|---|---|---|
| `Landing View` | Once after attribution is parsed | Page traffic only |
| `CTA Click` | CTA button or anchored form focus | Intent only |
| `Waitlist Attempt` | Valid client form sent to Pages Function | Attempt only |
| `Confirmation Requested` | Pages Function reports Buttondown acceptance | Pending only |
| `Waitlist Confirmed` | Only if a verified provider confirmation redirect/webhook can support it | Confirmed conversion |
| `Welcome Reply` | Manual import or separate research sheet | Downstream evidence |

Properties:

```text
source_class
utm_source
utm_medium
utm_campaign
utm_content
landing_variant
```

Buttondown’s confirmed-subscriber export remains the source of truth. If a verified confirmation event cannot be implemented with the current integration, omit `Waitlist Confirmed` from Plausible and reconcile confirmed subscribers from Buttondown instead.

Never fire `Waitlist Confirmed` from the form submit handler.

### Internal traffic

Exclude founder/team traffic using a Plausible exclusion rule or a private `?internal=1` flag that disables analytics. Do not use production campaign URLs for testing.

### Preflight verification

Before any public traffic:

- Test mobile and desktop rendering.
- Test keyboard navigation, focus states, labels, contrast, and reduced motion.
- Submit one valid address and complete double opt-in.
- Verify its exact source and UTMs in Buttondown.
- Submit an invalid address and confirm it is not counted.
- Submit a duplicate and document Buttondown’s behavior.
- Verify every Plausible event fires once.
- Confirm no confirmation event fires on an attempt or failed request.
- Confirm the ad and landing page use identical promise and price language.
- Confirm privacy and contact links work.
- Check candidate communities’ current promotion rules manually.

## 9. Decision rules

### Proceed to a thin MVP

Proceed only if either:

- Two separate cold sources reach their strong thresholds, with at least eight cold confirmed subscribers and three substantive replies overall; or
- One cold source produces at least eight confirmed subscribers, ≥8% CVR, three substantive replies, and at least two credible price-positive replies.

Warm results may strengthen this decision but are not included in the cold thresholds.

The thin MVP should test only save, identify, and restore for one client workspace. Pricing still requires validation.

### Interview before building

Interview confirmed subscribers when:

- A cold source reaches its CVR threshold but produces fewer than three substantive replies;
- Signups are strong but price-positive replies are absent;
- Users describe conflicting workspace needs; or
- Warm demand is strong while all cold sources are inconclusive.

### Retest the offer

Run one controlled second smoke test when:

- CTA click rate is healthy but confirmed CVR is below threshold;
- Unconfirmed subscriptions are unusually high;
- Replies reveal that “client workspace” is unclear; or
- Hero A is weak after its minimum sample but visitors are demonstrably in the ICP.

Change one variable: problem language, promise, or price presentation. Keep the source and CTA constant.

### Reshape

Reshape the ICP or job when every adequately sampled cold source is weak, even if warm contacts are polite or curious.

Possible pivots suggested by replies—not assumptions—might include restoring workflows by ticket type, incident, or account rather than by client.

### Park

Park the concept when:

- The original offer and one controlled rewrite both remain below the source-specific weak thresholds;
- No substantive reply describes recurring pain;
- No credible price-positive signal appears; and
- Attribution and deliverability have been verified, ruling out measurement failure.

Do not interpret insufficient traffic as product rejection. That is a distribution failure and requires a different acquisition test.

## 10. What not to build during the smoke

- Chrome extension code or Chrome Web Store package.
- Account system, cloud sync, or team sharing.
- D1 database, Durable Object, KV, or product API.
- Stripe checkout or subscription billing.
- AI tab classification or automatic client detection.
- Browser-history ingestion.
- Admin dashboard.
- Multi-browser support.
- Polished demo, fabricated UI, testimonials, logos, or waitlist counter.
- Pricing tiers beyond the truthful “from $9/month” signal.
- A firm roadmap or ship date.
