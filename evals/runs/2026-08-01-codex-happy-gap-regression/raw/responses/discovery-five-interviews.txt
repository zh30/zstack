## 8. Decision

**Verdict: Advance — narrowly, to a concierge pilot; do not green-light a broad product build.**

The evidence supports a wedge around recurring, multi-tool onboarding for agency operations/PMs. I01 combines the best available indicators: frequent quantified labor, existing software spend, a failed workaround, a diagnosed ownership problem, and an explicit pilot request.

Do not initially combine agencies, consultants, bookkeeping, marketing, and low-pain founders into one ICP. Bookkeeping may become a separate security-sensitive segment. The founder is an anti-ICP based on current evidence.

### Pilot wedge

Help agency PMs turn a real incoming client into an owner-assigned onboarding workflow while reducing cross-tool copying. The pilot should:

- Work alongside current tools without requiring full migration.
- Make responsibility explicit for every task and handoff.
- Accept and export CSV.
- Link to sensitive files in existing storage rather than copying them by default.
- Measure manual time and failures before and after.
- Be concierge-assisted until the workflow is proven.

This scope is supported by interview evidence. A generalized workflow platform, document vault, autonomous migration engine, or bookkeeper-specific compliance product is not yet supported.

## 9. Concrete downstream handoff

The founder should run one three-account concierge-pilot cycle within 14 days, beginning with I01 and recruiting two additional cold agency PMs who recently completed an onboarding.

For each account:

1. Replay the most recent onboarding and record baseline manual time, tools, owners, and failures.
2. Configure the smallest owner-assigned workflow without requiring migration.
3. Process one real client onboarding.
4. Ask for payment or a deposit before treating interest as commercial validation.
5. Observe whether the participant uses it for a second onboarding without intensive founder prompting.

**Advance to a thin PRD if:**

- At least 2 of 3 participants complete a real onboarding.
- Median copying/coordination time falls by at least 50% from each participant’s baseline.
- At least one pays or places a non-refundable deposit.
- At least one initiates a second onboarding.
- No unresolved ownership, export, or security blocker prevents normal use.

**Reshape if:** workflows complete but payment or repeat use fails, or agencies consistently need a different wedge.

**Park if:** fewer than 2 participants provide real workflow access or complete an onboarding, despite qualifying for recent recurring pain.
