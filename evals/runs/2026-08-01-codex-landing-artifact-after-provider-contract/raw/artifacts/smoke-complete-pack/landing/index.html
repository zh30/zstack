Questions, answered plainly.

Is ClientTab available now?

No. This page tests whether the problem and proposed one-click restore are worth building. There is no firm ship date.

What would it cost?

Planned pricing starts from $9/month. Final tiers and terms are not set, and joining the waitlist does not lock you into a purchase.

What would the extension access?

The intended workflow requires browser-tab permissions to save and reopen chosen URLs. Exact permissions, storage, and security design are still unproven and would be documented before release.

Will it replace our help desk or CRM?

No. It is intended to restore the browser context around those tools, not replace them.

What happens after I join?

If Buttondown accepts your email, you will see a confirmed message and receive a short welcome email. You may reply with the client setup you rebuild most often.

How is my email used?
