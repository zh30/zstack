## 1. Executive summary

- **Primary keyword (main money term):** `client onboarding workflow for agencies`  
- **Primary page:** `/` (homepage rewritten as the cluster hub / commercial landing)  
- **Strategy in one paragraph:** Own one tight cluster around **agency client onboarding as a workflow with clear next-step ownership**, not generic “agency software” or CRM portals. Supplied notes say broad software SERPs are CRM/portal-heavy (hard for a zero-authority domain), while **agency workflow + owner-specific** results look **mixed**—a plausible indie wedge. Build the homepage as the commercial hub; use **one** thin checklist article rewrite plus **2–3** new problem/process spokes that answer community language (“who owns the next step,” “why checklists don’t get done”) without competing for the same primary intent. Six hours/week → ship depth on one cluster, not a blog farm.  
- **Do not chase:** `agency CRM`, `client portal software`, `agency management software`, bare `checklist template`, generic `workflow automation`, head “best agency software” listicles.

## 6. On-page briefs (P0)

### Page: `/` (homepage hub)

- **Title:** Client Onboarding Workflow for Agencies \| {Brand}  
- **H1:** Client onboarding workflow built for small agencies  
- **Slug:** `/`  
- **Meta angle:** Stop stalled kickoffs—assign who owns every next step, not another generic checklist. CTA to try/demo.  
- **Must cover:**  
  - Problem: checklists without owners stall  
  - Workflow stages (high level only; deep steps on process spoke)  
  - **Ownership model** (role + next step visible)  
  - Who it’s for (small agencies) / who it’s not (full CRM replacement)  
  - Proof placeholders (founder story, 1 workflow screenshot—no fake metrics)  
  - CTA to trial + secondary link to process/checklist guides  
- **Internal links:** → process guide, checklist article, ownership guide, failure guide, `/pricing`  
- **CTA:** Start trial / book demo (align with commercial intent)

- **Meta angle:** Checklist items plus **named owner** and done definition—so tasks actually finish.  
- **Must cover:** Phased checklist; owner column; “definition of done”; what not to put on a checklist; link to product workflow
