## 1. Method limits

- **No full notes / transcripts.** Summaries cannot support quote-level precision; gate scores rest on reported behaviors only.
- **No web, browser, apps, computer use, or subagents** in this run — no external corroboration; evidence is limited to the five summaries.
- **N=5** is pattern detection, not sizing; one warm polite outlier and one free-tool user limit saturation.
- **Unknown:** exact product, price willingness, who owns budget beyond “pays Zapier / assistant / VA,” security requirements detail, and whether pilot would convert to paid.
- **Sample bias:** roles cluster around service ops; one founder is anti-signal for broad “everyone onboarding clients.”

## 7. Decision

**Verdict: Advance** (smoke / thin pilot only — not a large build)

**Why:**  
Among cold interviews, a coherent **bleeding + already paying** cluster (agency PM, consultant, bookkeeper) shows real pain frequency, cash or labor cost, and at least one explicit pilot ask. That is enough to **Advance** into demand smoke and a tightly scoped pilot—not enough to claim market size or retention.

**If Advance — wedge to take into smoke/PRD:**  
- **Wedge:** Agency / client-services operators who **copy client data across tools for ~30–45+ minutes** and already pay **Zapier or VA/assistant labor**.  
- **Promise:** cut copy time and failed handoffs; clear **ownership** of onboarding templates/process.  
- **Pilot shape:** real pilot with one agency PM–like account; measure time-on-copy and failed onboarding steps.  
- **Must design for:** migration path (consultant), sensitive files (bookkeeper), optional **CSV** in/out (marketer).

**If Reshape — (secondary, if pilot fails):**  
Narrow further to **bookkeeping / sensitive client files** *or* **agency multi-tool copy**, not both, if security vs speed split the pilot.

**If Park — reopen when:**  
After honest N≥8–10 cold ICP with no more paying bleeders, or pilots refuse when migration/security is concrete.

**Do not:** treat founder compliments as demand; green-light broad platform build from this N.
