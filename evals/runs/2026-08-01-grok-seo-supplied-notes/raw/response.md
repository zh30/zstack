## 1. Executive summary

- **Primary keyword (cluster head):** `client onboarding workflow for agencies`
- **Primary page:** Homepage `/` (rewrite from generic home into a commercial hub for this cluster)
- **Strategy in one paragraph:** With zero domain authority and six hours a week, do **not** fight CRM / client-portal / “agency software” SERPs. Own one **problem-shaped cluster**: agency **client onboarding workflow**, differentiated by **step ownership** (“who owns the next step,” why checklists stall). Keep supporting pages to a **small set** with non-overlapping intents (workflow vs checklist vs process vs failure diagnosis). Ship quality over quantity; measure with GSC setup + manual query checks until data exists.
- **Do not chase:** Broad `agency software`, CRM, client portal, generic automation, and multi-checklist content farms.

**Confidence:** Medium on cluster fit (aligned with product wedge + community language); **low absolute demand certainty** (no volumes; notes only). Treat first 30 days as message + baseline, not ranking proof.

## 2. Product & search context

### 2.1 Who searches and why

| Persona | Job-to-be-done | Search mindset |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Small agency owner / ops lead | Get new clients from sale → kickoff → delivery without drops | “We need a process/workflow that actually runs” |
| Account / project lead | Know **who** does the next step and when handoffs fail | “Checklist exists; nothing moves” |
| Agency PM / coordinator | Standardize onboarding without buying a full CRM suite | Templates, checklists, lightweight tools |

**Product wedge (search-usable language):** Not “another checklist.” An **ownership-aware onboarding workflow**—every step has an owner, next step is visible, work doesn’t die in shared docs.

### 2.2 Current footprint (from notes only)

| URL / route | Apparent target | Issues |
|-------------|-----------------|--------|
| `/` homepage | Generic brand/home | No clear primary query; weak commercial SEO signal |
| `/pricing` | Purchase | Fine as transactional support; not a ranking bet |
| Thin “checklist” article | Checklist queries | Thin content; risk of owning only weak checklist intent; can cannibalize a future hub if rewritten poorly |

### 2.3 Assumptions (explicit)

1. Product is for **small agencies**, US English searchers, **client onboarding** (not employee HR onboarding).
2. Differentiator is **ownership / next-step clarity**, not “AI,” not full CRM.
3. Supplied autocomplete-style phrases and SERP impressions are **unverified notes**, used only as directional evidence.
4. No inventing search volumes, difficulty scores from tools, or competitor rank positions beyond note-level patterns.
5. Founder can ship **~1 substantial content/page unit per 2–3 weeks** at 6 hrs/week if technical baseline is light.

## 6. On-page briefs (P0)

### 6.1 Homepage `/` — primary money page

- **Title:** `Client Onboarding Workflow for Agencies | {Brand}`  
  (~55–60 chars with short brand; prioritize keyword + audience)
- **H1:** `Client onboarding workflow built for small agencies`  
  (or: `Know who owns the next step in client onboarding`)
- **Slug:** `/`
- **Meta angle:** Replace generic checklists with an **ownership-aware onboarding workflow**—assign owners, surface next steps, stop stalled kickoffs. CTA: start free / book demo.
- **Must cover (entities / subtopics):**
  1. Who it’s for (small agencies, client onboarding—not HR)
  2. Failure of ownerless checklists (bridge from community language)
  3. What “ownership-aware” means (owner per step, visible next step, handoffs)
  4. How the workflow runs (stages: win → kickoff → assets → access → first delivery)—**product truth only**
  5. Contrast vs static checklist / vs heavy CRM (honest scope)
  6. Proof placeholders (even “early product” honesty > fake logos)
  7. FAQ: 4–6 real questions (who owns next step; checklist vs workflow; time to set up)
  8. Single primary CTA + secondary “see pricing”
- **Internal links:** Out to checklist, process, failure guide (as they ship); in from all spokes with anchor variety (`onboarding workflow`, `ownership-aware workflow`, product name)
- **CTA:** Start workflow / trial — not “download 100 templates”
- **Do not:** Stuff `agency software`, portal, CRM keywords into title

### 6.2 Checklist article (existing) — Spoke A

- **Title:** `Client Onboarding Checklist for Agencies (With Owners)`
- **H1:** Match title closely
- **Slug:** Keep existing if already indexed/shared; else `/guides/client-onboarding-checklist-for-agencies`
- **Meta angle:** A practical agency client onboarding checklist **with an owner for every item**—so work doesn’t die in shared docs.
- **Must cover:** Phased checklist (pre-kickoff, kickoff, access, brand/assets, success criteria); **Owner** + **Next step** columns; common drop-offs; short “when you outgrow a doc” → link hub
- **Internal links:** → `/` workflow; → failure guide when live
- **CTA:** “Run this as a workflow” (product)
- **Cannibalization:** No second checklist URL; no “ultimate checklist 2026” duplicates

### 6.3 Process guide — Spoke B (P0/P1 depending on capacity)

- **Title:** `Client Onboarding Process for Small Agencies`
- **H1:** Same
- **Slug:** `/guides/client-onboarding-process-small-agencies`
- **Must cover:** Stages, roles (owner/account/client), handoff points, what “done” means per stage; optional **one** template table
- **Internal links:** → hub; → checklist for the list artifact; not a paste of the full checklist

### 6.4 Failure / diagnosis guide — Spoke C

- **Title:** `Why Client Onboarding Checklists Don’t Get Done`
- **H1:** Same or `Why agency onboarding checklists stall`
- **Slug:** `/guides/why-client-onboarding-checklists-fail`

- **Internal links:** → hub (primary CTA); → checklist (improved artifact)

### 6.5 Pricing

- Title: `{Brand} Pricing` — no keyword war
