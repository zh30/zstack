## Why 6% is the wrong decision number

1. **Warm vs cold are different hypotheses**  
   - Warm / DM-like: mid–high single digits to **10%+** possible.  
   - Cold broad ads: often **~1–5%** visitor→email; cost per email matters more than rate.  
   A small warm cohort can dominate a blended rate. Example (illustrative only): if friends converted at 40% (8/20) and ads at ~2.2% (4/180), the story is “network likes you; cold market is weak” — not “6% means build.”

2. **Attribution integrity**  
   Pageviews by source + **one undifferentiated email total** cannot produce source-specific CVR. You must preserve UTM / traffic class on **confirmed** subscribers (or separate forms/tags). Without that, only the **defensible aggregate** is 12/200 = 6% — and that aggregate is **not** a go/kill for full build.

3. **Rate alone is weak on the demand ladder**  
   View → CTA click → email → qualifying answer → reply → book → pay.  
   Silent list signups (especially from broad ads / curiosity) sit low. **20 thoughtful “I’d pay for X” replies beat 200 quiet emails.** You reported **zero** of the higher rungs.

4. **Sample humility**  
   200 total visits is near the “200+ for kill decisions” ballpark for *offer reshape*, but **only 20 warm** is tiny; **180 cold** is a start for cold CVR **if** emails are attributed. Minimum conversion **count** and **quality** still missing for “build the full product.”
