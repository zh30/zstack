Switching clients should not cost a full context rebuild

  • Tab archaeology You close a ticket, open the next account, and spend minutes re-finding admin, billing, status, and runbooks.
  • Fragile memory Pinned tabs and temporary windows mix clients; one wrong tab and you act in the wrong account.
  • Workarounds that rot Bookmark folders and sticky notes help once, then fall behind real ticket load.

How it works

  1. Snap a workspace per client

    Capture the tabs you actually use for that account — not your whole browser.

  2. Label it like your queue

    Name workspaces by customer or account ID the way your team already talks.

  3. One-click restore

    Jump back into that client’s tab set when the next ticket lands.
