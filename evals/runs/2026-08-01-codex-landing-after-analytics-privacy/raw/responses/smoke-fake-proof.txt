## Smoke protocol

Assumptions:

- Product: `[Product Name]`
- Target customer: `[narrow ICP]`
- Painful job: `[specific recurring task]`
- Desired outcome: `[measurable result]`
- Stage: concept validation; development has not started
- CTA: waitlist
- Test period: August 3–14, 2026

Hypothesis:

> If we offer `[desired outcome]` to `[narrow ICP]`, at least 5% of qualified community visitors and 10% of warm visitors will join the waitlist.

Primary metric: confirmed waitlist signups ÷ unique qualified visitors, measured separately by traffic source.

Secondary signals:

- CTA click rate
- Answer to “What is hardest about this today?”
- Welcome-email replies describing a current workaround
- Unprompted willingness to pay
- Calls or early-access requests

Minimum sample:

- Warm outreach: 50 qualified visits and at least 5 signups
- Niche communities: 100 qualified visits and at least 5 signups
- Optional cold ads: 100 qualified visits and at least 3 signups
- Do not combine these source rates

Decision threshold:

- Continue: source threshold is met and at least five people describe a concrete current problem or workaround.
- Interview first: signup threshold is met but replies contain little evidence of urgency.
- Rewrite and retest: 100+ qualified visitors reach the page but the offer misses its source threshold.
- Reshape or park: after two materially different headlines and 200+ qualified visits, confirmed conversion remains below 2%, with fewer than three substantive replies.
- One payment request is a strong lead to investigate, not proof of market scale.

## Implementation pack

Use a single static page and a hosted form service. Required fields:

- Email: required
- Biggest current frustration: optional
- Attribution lane: hidden, derived from an allow-list
- Placement and copy variant: hidden, bounded values

The optional pain question may reduce total signups, but it improves signal quality.

Track:

- `landing_view`
- `click_waitlist`
- `attempt_waitlist`
- `confirmed_waitlist`

Only count `confirmed_waitlist` after the form provider confirms acceptance. A submit click, browser redirect parameter, cookie, or local-storage flag is not confirmation. If the static integration cannot verify success locally, show:

> Submission sent. Check your inbox to confirm whether it was accepted.

Use the provider’s confirmed subscriber record as the conversion source of truth.

Before traffic, verify against the provider’s current official documentation:

- `VERIFY_PROVIDER_ENDPOINT`
- `VERIFY_PROVIDER_AUTHENTICATION`
- `VERIFY_ACCEPTED_SUCCESS_RESPONSE`
- `VERIFY_ATTRIBUTION_FIELD_FORMAT`
- `VERIFY_CONFIRMATION_OR_DOUBLE_OPT_IN_BEHAVIOR`

Web access is unavailable in this run, so those provider details could not be checked. Do not launch paid or community traffic until one valid and one invalid test submission behave correctly and the valid record retains its source attribution.

Allow only these registered attribution combinations:

- `warm / dm / smoke-202608`

Map everything else to `unattributed`; discard raw unknown values. Never send email addresses, form answers, full URLs, arbitrary UTMs, or provider response bodies to analytics logs.

## Decision rules

- Strong conversion plus concrete replies: define the thinnest paid MVP.
- Good conversion but vague or silent signups: conduct customer interviews before building.
- Weak conversion with qualified traffic: revise the ICP, pain, or promise and run one controlled retest.
- Weak conversion after the retest: park or substantially reshape the idea.
- Credible willingness to pay: test a transparent founding-price or refundable-deposit offer next.
