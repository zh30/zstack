The calculation is correct: 12 ÷ 200 = 6%. However, combining friends and broad-ad visitors hides the signal:

- Friends are warm traffic and may convert from goodwill.
- Broad ads are cold traffic; 1–5% email conversion is a directional range, so a verified 6% from ads alone would be promising.
- Because the 12 emails aren’t attributed, cold-ad conversion could be anywhere from 0% to 6.7%.
- Emails show curiosity, not willingness to pay.

Decision: don’t build the full product yet. Follow up with the 12 signups, asking about their current workaround, urgency, and willingness to pay. Then run a source-attributed test with at least 200 qualified cold visitors. If cold traffic sustains roughly 5%+ confirmed signups and produces credible replies, bookings, or payment intent, build a thin paid MVP—not the complete product.

Also verify that the 12 are unique, provider-confirmed signups rather than submission attempts.

Evidence is limited to your reported aggregate counts; without subscriber-level source attribution or engagement/payment results, source-specific demand cannot be established.
