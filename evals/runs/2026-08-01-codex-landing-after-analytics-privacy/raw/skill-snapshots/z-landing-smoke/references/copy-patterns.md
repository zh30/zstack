# Copy patterns — z-landing-smoke

## Hero formulas (pick one)

1. **ICP + outcome**  
   `{Outcome} for {ICP}`  
   Sub: `{Status quo pain}. {Product} helps you {mechanism}.`

2. **Problem-first**  
   `Stop {painful process}`  
   Sub: `{Product} {does job} so you can {desired state}.`

3. **Category + wedge**  
   `The {category} for {narrow ICP}`  
   Sub: `{Unlike broad tools}, focused on {one job}.`

## CTA labels

| CTA type | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Waitlist | Join the waitlist · Get early access |
| Notify | Notify me at launch |
| Pre-order | Pre-order — $X · Reserve founding price |
| Call | Request a demo (use sparingly for smoke) |

Avoid vague CTAs: “Learn more”, “Submit”, “Click here”.

## Problem bullets

Use **cost of status quo**: time, money, embarrassment, risk — not features.

- Bad: “Has AI-powered workflows”  
- Good: “You rebuild the same client report every Monday by hand”

## FAQ seeds

- Is this live or in development?  
- When do founding users get access?  
- How is my email used?  
- Pricing expectations?  
- Who is this *not* for?  
- What happens after I join?

## Honesty lines (use when true)

- “We’re validating demand with founding users before building everything.”  
- “You’ll get a short product update — unsubscribe anytime.”  
- “Pre-order is refundable until {date}.” (only if true)

## Anti-patterns

- Feature laundry lists above the fold  
- “Revolutionary / seamless / next-gen” with no concrete job  
- Fake logos (“Trusted by…”)  
- Multiple competing CTAs  
- Walls of text on mobile
