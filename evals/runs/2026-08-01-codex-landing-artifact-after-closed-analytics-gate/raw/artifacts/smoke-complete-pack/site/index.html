The planned workflow

Save once. Restore when the next ticket arrives.

2

3

A deliberately narrow tool

Built around hands-on client support.

This is for you if…

You support multiple client accounts, live in Chrome, and repeatedly reopen the same account-specific admin, CRM, observability, ticket, and documentation pages.

It’s not for you if…

You want a general bookmark manager, shared team knowledge base, automatic ticket resolution, or a replacement for your help desk.

Questions, answered plainly

Before you join

Is ContextDeck available now?

No. It’s an unbuilt Chrome extension concept, and this page is a demand test. Joining does not guarantee that it will ship.

When will it launch?

There is no firm ship date. If the test shows real demand, we’ll invite a small group to validate the first useful version before expanding.

What will it cost?

Planned pricing starts at $9/month. We’re testing whether that range fits the problem; it may change before any product is offered. Joining is free and no card is collected.

What would the extension store?

The concept is to save the URLs in each named client workspace. Storage, permissions, retention, and security behavior are not finalized; those details must be reviewed before any build is released.

Who is it designed for?

Hands-on support agents at small US SaaS teams who repeatedly switch among multiple client-specific browser contexts.

What happens after I join?

Buttondown will ask you to confirm your email. If accepted, you’ll receive a short welcome note asking about your current workflow. Your reply is optional.
