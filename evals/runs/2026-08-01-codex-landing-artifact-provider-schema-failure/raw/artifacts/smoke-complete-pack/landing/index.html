FAQ

Before you join

Is the Chrome extension available now?

No. This page is testing demand before the product is built. Joining expresses interest; it does not guarantee access by a particular date.

What will it cost?

Planned pricing starts from $9/month. That price may change as the product is defined, and joining the waitlist does not create a purchase or lock in a price.

What would the extension store?

The intended product would need tab titles and URLs to restore a workspace. The storage and permission design is not final. Privacy and least-privilege access will be evaluated before any release.

What happens after I join?

Buttondown will ask you to confirm your email. After confirmation, you may receive short product updates or a request for feedback. You can unsubscribe anytime.

Who is this not for?

It is not a general bookmark manager, a team knowledge base, or an enterprise browser-administration suite.

Will you sell my email?

No. Your email is stored by Buttondown and used only for this early-access list and related product research. It will not be sold.
