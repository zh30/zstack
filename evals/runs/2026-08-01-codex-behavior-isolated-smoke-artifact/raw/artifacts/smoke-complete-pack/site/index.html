Questions

What you should know before joining

Is ClientTabs available now?

No. It is an unbuilt Chrome extension concept under validation. Joining signals interest; it does not guarantee access or a delivery date.

What would it cost?

The planned starting price is $9/month. Pricing may change as the useful scope becomes clearer, and joining the waitlist does not start a subscription.

What would the extension store?

The concept is to save client workspace names and tab URLs needed for restoration—not page contents or passwords. The exact architecture and privacy model are still unbuilt and will be documented before any install is offered.

Will it open or act inside client accounts?

The proposed one-click action restores saved tabs. It would not log in, send messages, or perform account actions for you.

What happens after I join?

Buttondown will ask you to confirm your email. You’ll receive a short welcome note asking about your current workflow, plus occasional product-validation updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How will my information be used?

Your email and optional answers are stored in Buttondown for this early-access list and will not be sold. Plausible provides aggregate, cookie-free page analytics. Contact the founder to request deletion.
