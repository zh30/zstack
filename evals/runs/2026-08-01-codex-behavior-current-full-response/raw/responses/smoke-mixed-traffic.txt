No. The aggregate is **12 / 200 = 6%**, which is encouraging enough to continue validating, but not enough to build the full product.

The main issue is mixed attribution:

- 20 visits were warm friend traffic.
- 180 were broad-ad traffic.
- The 12 emails were not separated by source.

Therefore, the broad-ad conversion rate could be anywhere from **0% to 6.7%**. Friend traffic may also reflect support or curiosity rather than genuine demand.

Recommended next step:

1. Ask all 12 subscribers what they currently use, how painful the problem is, and whether they would pay a specific price.
2. Run another test with at least **200 ICP-targeted cold visitors**, preserving source attribution.
3. Pre-register these directional thresholds:
   - **≥5% confirmed signup rate plus 3+ credible replies/bookings:** build a thin MVP.
   - **2–5% with weak engagement:** sharpen the ICP or offer and retest.
   - **<2% across two variants:** reshape or park the idea.
4. Before a full build, seek stronger intent: a deposit, pre-order, paid pilot, or credible “invoice me” request.

Treat the current result as **“continue validation,” not “full-product approval.”** If speed matters, build only a manual or narrowly functional prototype needed to test payment.
