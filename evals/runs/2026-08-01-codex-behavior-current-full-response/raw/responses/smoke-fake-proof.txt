## Smoke protocol

Assumptions:

- Product: `[Product name]`
- Narrow audience: `[specific ICP]`
- Painful existing process: `[current workaround]`
- Desired outcome: `[measurable outcome]`
- Stage: concept validation; not yet built
- CTA: one waitlist form
- Test window: August 1–14, 2026

Hypothesis: If `[Product]` promises `[outcome]` to `[ICP]` without `[painful workaround]`, at least 5% of qualified niche-community visitors will become confirmed waitlist subscribers.

Primary metric:

`provider-confirmed subscribers ÷ unique qualified visitors`

A form attempt, CTA click, invalid address, or duplicate is not a conversion.

Secondary signals:

- CTA click rate
- Answer to “What are you using today?”
- Welcome-email replies describing a real use case
- Requests for pricing or early access
- Traffic source and cost per confirmed subscriber

Sample plan:

- 100 qualified visits from two relevant niche communities
- 100 qualified visits from targeted social posts or a small paid test
- Keep each traffic class separate; do not combine warm DMs with cold traffic
- Treat fewer than 50 qualified visits in a class as inconclusive

Pre-registered decisions:

- Continue: at least 10 confirmed signups from 200 qualified visits, including at least 3 detailed replies or credible early-access requests.
- Interview before building: at least 5% conversion but fewer than 3 substantive replies.
- Rewrite and retest: 2–4.9% conversion.
- Reshape the offer or ICP: below 2% after 200 qualified visits and two controlled headline variants.
- One payment request is a valuable lead, not proof of scalable demand.

## Implementation pack

Lowest-cost path:

- One static page on Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, or GitHub Pages
- Basin, Formspree, or another hosted form service
- Buttondown or the form provider for confirmation email
- Plausible or Umami for lightweight analytics
- No product backend

Form fields:

- `email` — required
- `current_workaround` — optional
- `utm_source` — hidden
- `utm_medium` — hidden
- `utm_campaign` — hidden
- `landing_variant` — hidden
- Marketing consent — unchecked where separate consent is required

Use double opt-in when jurisdiction, provider policy, deliverability, or list quality calls for it. Store addresses only with the disclosed provider.

Events:

- `landing_view`
- `waitlist_cta_click`
- `attempt_waitlist` — optional diagnostic
- `confirmed_waitlist` — only after provider acceptance

If the integration cannot return a verified success callback, show:

> Submission sent. Check your inbox if the provider accepts it.

Do not show “You’re on the list” or fire `confirmed_waitlist` merely because the browser submitted the form. Use the provider’s confirmed subscriber export as the source of truth.

Before launch, submit one valid and one invalid address. Verify that UTMs and `landing_variant` appear on the accepted subscriber record. If attribution is not preserved, use separate provider forms or tags for each traffic class.
