- The agency PM’s pilot request is useful commitment intent, but it is not yet observed adoption or payment.
- The founder’s praise receives no demand weight.
- No direct quotes can be reported.
- The interview summaries do not reveal the questions asked, so leading questions and future-tense traps cannot be fully audited.
- Pitch leakage occurred at least in the founder interview because the response included praise for the pitch. Leakage scores for the other interviews are unknown.
- The sample meets the minimum synthesis threshold but is spread across four distinct professional contexts. It does not establish one broad, homogeneous client-onboarding market.

## Decision

Verdict: **Advance—but only to a narrow, commitment-bearing pilot and smoke test, not a broad product build.**

The best-supported wedge is:

> Client-onboarding operations for small agencies that repeatedly transfer client information across tools and need explicit ownership of every handoff.

Why this wedge:

- It contains the clearest quantified time cost.
- It has current tool spend.
- An alternative was already tried and abandoned.
- There is a named organizational failure: unclear ownership.
- The participant requested a real pilot.

The bookkeeper segment should not be folded into the first pilot. Sensitive-file handling creates a materially different security and compliance problem. The consultant and marketer remain useful follow-up segments for migration and CSV validation.

## Concrete downstream handoff

### Immediate pilot

Owner: founder.

Participant: agency PM.

Offer: a two-week, manual-first pilot covering one real client-onboarding workflow. Map the existing tools, assign an owner to every handoff, automate only the repeated transfers, and preserve a CSV export.

Required commitment before calling it validated:

- The PM supplies one live workflow and the necessary non-sensitive sample data.
- The PM names the workflow owner and stakeholders.
- The team uses the pilot for at least two real onboarding cases.
- A follow-up review is booked before setup begins.
- Ideally, collect a deposit or written agreement to pay if the agreed outcome is achieved.

Success signal:

- At least two real cases completed.
- Manual copying falls materially below the reported 45 minutes per occurrence.
- Ownership-related exceptions are visible and assigned.
- The PM continues using it or agrees to pay after the trial.

Failure signal:

- The PM does not provide workflow/data access within seven days.
- The pilot is used only with dummy data.
- No second real onboarding occurs.
- Migration or stakeholder coordination prevents activation.
- The PM declines continued use or payment despite the workflow working.

### Landing smoke test

Use paraphrases only; no verbatim customer language was supplied.

- Audience: agency PMs or operations leads managing client onboarding across multiple tools.
- Core problem: repeated copying and unclear ownership during handoffs.
- Promise to test: reduce repetitive transfer work while making every onboarding handoff accountable.
- Single CTA: “Apply for a two-week onboarding workflow pilot.”
- Form qualification: tools used, last onboarding date, time spent copying, failures in the last month, current automation spend, workflow owner, and willingness to provide a real case.
- Advance threshold: 3 qualified pilot applications from 30 directly contacted cold agency PMs, with at least 2 agreeing to provide a live workflow.
- Kill/reshape threshold: fewer than 2 qualified applications, or interest that produces no live-workflow commitment.

### Thin-PRD boundary

Include only:

- A workflow map across existing tools.
- Explicit owner and status for each handoff.
- Minimal field transfer for one agency workflow.
- CSV import/export.
- Exception logging and manual recovery.
- Assisted setup or migration.

Non-goals until further evidence:

- A universal onboarding platform for all professional services.
- Bookkeeping or sensitive-file storage.
- Fully self-serve migration.
- Replacing every existing tool.
- Advanced template libraries.
- Broad integrations beyond those required by the pilot.

### Next interview round

Recruit five additional cold agency PMs or operations leads matching the wedge. Ask about the last completed onboarding, exact copying frequency, who owned each handoff, recent failures, current Zapier/tool spending, attempted fixes, and what happened after those attempts. Avoid showing the product until those stories are collected.
