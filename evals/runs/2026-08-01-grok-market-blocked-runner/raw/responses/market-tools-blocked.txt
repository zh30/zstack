## 9. If you go deeper — entry directions

### Primary wedge (recommended *only after* demand scan)

- **Who:** Freelancers who already get paid via Stripe/PayPal and invoice ≥4×/month.  
- **Job-to-be-done:** Turn completed work notes/emails into a correct draft invoice in minutes.  
- **MVP shape:** One input (email/notes) → draft line items + client fields → human confirm → PDF/Stripe link.  
- **Where to find first 10 users:** Buyer habitats (r/freelance, local freelancer Discords, Twitter freelancers) — **not searched this run**.  
- **Metric in 14 days:** ≥5 users complete a full draft→send workflow twice; ≥1 paid or irrevocable prepay signal.

### Backup wedges

1. **Payment reminder / AR follow-up** for freelancers who already create invoices but hate chasing.  
2. **Receipt → expense line** for solopreneurs (different job; only if AR wedge dies).

### Explicitly do **not** start with

- Full QuickBooks replacement  
- Multi-entity / enterprise AP automation  
- “AI CFO” brand without a single painful workflow  
- Claiming **demand is proven** from this report
