## Smoke-test plan

Use a static page on Cloudflare Pages with Formspree, Basin, or Buttondown. Track:

- `view_landing`
- `click_waitlist`
- `submit_waitlist`
- Traffic source using `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`

Run the test for 10 days and target at least 200 qualified visitors across two headline variants. Treat 5%+ visitor-to-waitlist conversion with several thoughtful replies as encouraging. Below 2% after the full sample is a signal to revise the ICP or offer before building.

Drive traffic through:

- 20 personal messages to people matching the ICP
- One value-first post in a relevant buyer community
- Three founder posts explaining the problem and proposed approach
- Optionally, a $20–$100 tightly targeted ad test

Do not build the application, fabricate screenshots, or add unverified counters during this test. The goal is to discover whether real people care—not merely to make the page appear popular.
