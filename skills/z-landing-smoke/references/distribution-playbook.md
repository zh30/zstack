# Distribution playbook — z-landing-smoke

Goal: **enough qualified humans** to read conversion — not viral vanity.

## Principles

1. **Disclose** you’re the maker when rules/community norms require it.  
2. **Lead with problem or artifact**, not “please sign up”.  
3. **One link**, UTM’d.  
4. **Engage replies** — smoke tests die in broadcast-only mode.  
5. **Stop channels that only bring tire-kickers** after 48h of evidence.

## Channel notes

### X / Twitter

- Threads: pain story → what you’re trying → link.  
- Quote-tweet builders in same niche sparingly (value first).  
- Pin the landing link during the test window.

### Reddit

- Read sub rules + top posts first.  
- Prefer “feedback” / “show” flairs when available.  
- Soft CTA; hard sell gets removed.  
- Never multi-sub spam identical posts in one hour.

### Product Hunt

- Needs a clear product moment (even waitlist tools can launch if framed well).  
- Prepare hunter/maker story, first comment, visuals.  
- Treat PH as **one spike**, not the only channel.

### Indie Hackers / forums

- “Milestone: smoke test” posts work well.  
- Share metrics after (even failures) — builds trust for round 2.

### Small ads ($20–100)

- One ICP, one geo if relevant, one creative.  
- Landing must match ad promise.  
- Kill if CPC or CVR is hopeless in 48–72h.  
- Platforms: whatever the founder can run quickly (not a media team plan).

### Warm DMs (high ROI)

- 15–30 ideal users: short personal note, ask for 2 minutes on the page.  
- Track as `utm_source=dm` or manual “how heard = friend”.

## UTM examples

```text
?utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=smoke_v1
?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=smoke_v1&utm_content=r_sideproject
?utm_source=ph&utm_medium=launch&utm_campaign=smoke_v1
?utm_source=ads&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=smoke_v1
```

## Anti-patterns

- Buying fake traffic / fake signups  
- Cross-posting spam  
- PH + ads + 20 subreddits same morning with no reply capacity  
- Changing headline and ad and audience all at once (can’t learn)
